# Financial Leverage

Using [[Debt Finance]] to **amplify returns** to owners. When the return on investments exceeds the interest rate, gearing lifts shareholder returns — but it magnifies losses too, raising **financial risk**.

The **capital structure** (debt : equity mix) drives the **cost of capital**; the optimal mix minimises it.
