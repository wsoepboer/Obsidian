# Qualities of Financial Information

Qualities that make accounting information useful:

- **Relevance** — capable of influencing a decision.
- **Faithful representation** — reflects what it purports to.
- **Comparability** — like items treated alike, over time and between firms.
- **Verifiability** — independent experts broadly agree it is faithful (direct or indirect).
