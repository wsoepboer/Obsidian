# Debt Finance

Funds raised by **borrowing**.

- **Pros:** cheaper than equity (interest is tax-deductible); no dilution; provides [[Financial Leverage]].
- **Cons:** must be repaid; fixed interest; raises financial risk.

**Contrast:** [[Equity Finance]] · **See also:** [[Liabilities]] · [[Gearing Ratios]]
