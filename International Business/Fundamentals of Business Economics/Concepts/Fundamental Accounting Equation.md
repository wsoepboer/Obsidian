# Fundamental Accounting Equation

The identity underpinning the [[Statement of Financial Position]]:

> **Assets = Liabilities + Equity**   ⇔   **Equity = Assets − Liabilities**

Every transaction keeps it in balance: if assets rise, liabilities and/or equity rise by the same amount.
