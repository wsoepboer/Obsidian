# Preference Shares

Carry a **fixed** rate of dividend and a **preferential** claim on assets in a wind-up. Usually **no voting rights**. Lower risk than ordinary shares.

**Contrast:** [[Ordinary Shares]] · **See also:** [[Shares]]
