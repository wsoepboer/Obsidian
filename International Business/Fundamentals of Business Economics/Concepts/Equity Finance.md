# Equity Finance

Funds raised from [[Shareholders]] by issuing [[Shares]].

- **Pros:** no repayment obligation; flexible [[Dividends]]; lowers financial risk.
- **Cons:** dilutes ownership/control; costlier than debt; disclosure demands.

Includes internal equity via [[Retained Earnings]].

**Contrast:** [[Debt Finance]] · **See also:** [[Financial Leverage]]
