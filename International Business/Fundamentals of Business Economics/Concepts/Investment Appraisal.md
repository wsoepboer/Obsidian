# Investment Appraisal

Techniques to evaluate long-term capital projects:

- **Payback period** — time to recover the outlay; ignores the [[Time Value of Money]].
- [[Net Present Value]] (**NPV**) — discounts all cash flows; the recommended method.
- **IRR** — the discount rate where NPV = 0.
- **Profitability index** — PV of inflows ÷ outlay.
