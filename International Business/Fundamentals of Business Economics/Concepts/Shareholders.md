# Shareholders

The **owners** of a [[Limited Company]], holding [[Shares]] that represent equal stakes in it. Their claim is [[Equity]]; they benefit through [[Dividends]] and rising share value, and enjoy [[Limited Liability]].

- **[[Ordinary Shares|Ordinary shareholders]]** — vote and take the residual risk/return.
- **[[Preference Shares|Preference shareholders]]** — fixed dividend, preferential claim, usually no vote.

A key group of external [[Users of Accounting Information]], focused on profitability and return ([[Wealth Creation]]).
