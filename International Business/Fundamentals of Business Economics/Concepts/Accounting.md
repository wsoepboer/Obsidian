# Accounting

The process of **collecting, analysing and communicating financial information** so that decision makers make more informed decisions. If information does not improve a decision, there is no point producing it.

- Viewed as an **information system**: data → processing → information.
- Two branches: [[Financial Accounting]] (external) and [[Management Accounting]] (internal).
- A **service** function — judged on relevance, timeliness and accuracy.
