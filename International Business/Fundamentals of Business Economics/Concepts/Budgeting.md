# Budgeting

Preparing detailed **financial plans** for a future period. Purposes: planning, **control**, motivation, coordination, communication.

- **Operating budgets:** sales → production → materials/labour/overhead.
- **Financial budgets:** cash, capital, budgeted income statement & position.
- **Control:** actual vs budget = **variance** (favourable / adverse).
