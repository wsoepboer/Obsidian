# Statement of Cash Flows

Explains **cash movements over a period**, split into:

- **Operating** activities (day-to-day trading),
- **Investing** activities (non-current assets),
- **Financing** activities (finance raised/repaid, dividends).

Reconciles why cash changed despite reported [[Profit]] — because **cash ≠ profit**.
