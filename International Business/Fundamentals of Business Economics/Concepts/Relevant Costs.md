# Relevant Costs

For decisions, only costs that **differ between alternatives** are relevant.

- **Opportunity cost** — benefit forgone from the next-best alternative (relevant).
- **Sunk cost** — already incurred, unrecoverable (**irrelevant**).
- Committed costs are also irrelevant.
