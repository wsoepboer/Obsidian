# Cost Behaviour

How costs change with the level of activity:

- **Fixed** — constant regardless of output (rent, [[Depreciation]], insurance).
- **Variable** — change in proportion to output (direct materials/labour, commission).
- **Semi-variable** — both elements (utilities, maintenance, phone).

Understanding behaviour underpins planning, [[Budgeting]] and [[Full Costing]].
