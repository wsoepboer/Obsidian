# Financial Accounting

Accounting for **external users**. Based on the accounting records, presented in a **standardised** format, usually **annual**, and subject to strict legal/accounting rules.

Produces the [[Statement of Financial Position]], [[Income Statement]] and [[Statement of Cash Flows]].

**Contrast:** [[Management Accounting]]
