# Gearing Ratios

Measure the level of debt and ability to service it:

- **Debt-to-equity** = debt ÷ equity
- **Interest cover** = operating profit ÷ interest
- **Debt ratio**

Higher gearing means higher [[Financial Leverage]] and higher financial risk.
