# Users of Accounting Information

Groups that use accounting information, each with different needs:

- **Internal:** managers, department heads, supervisors.
- **External** ([[Stakeholders]]): shareholders/owners, lenders, suppliers, customers, government, employees, the public.

Shareholders assess returns; lenders assess ability to repay; suppliers assess creditworthiness; employees assess stability.
