# Full Costing

Assigns **all** manufacturing costs to products:

> Product cost = direct materials + direct labour + **absorbed overhead**

Overhead is spread using a **cost driver** (labour hours, machine hours). Good for pricing and inventory valuation; weakness is **arbitrary overhead allocation** distorting product costs.
