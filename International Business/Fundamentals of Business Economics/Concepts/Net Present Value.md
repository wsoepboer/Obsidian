# Net Present Value

The present value of a project's cash flows minus the initial investment:

> **NPV = Σ [ CFₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ ] − Initial outlay**

Accept if **NPV > 0**. Fully reflects the [[Time Value of Money]] and is the preferred appraisal method.
