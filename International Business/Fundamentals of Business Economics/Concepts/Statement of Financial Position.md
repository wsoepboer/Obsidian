# Statement of Financial Position

Shows the **accumulated wealth at a point in time** — what the business owns and owes. Built on the [[Fundamental Accounting Equation]]:

> **Assets = Liabilities + Equity**

Lists [[Assets]] (current / non-current), [[Liabilities]] (current / non-current) and [[Equity]]. Must always balance.
