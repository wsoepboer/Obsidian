# Management Accounting

Accounting for **internal users** (managers) — for planning, control and decision-making. Detailed, frequent, tailored to parts of the business, and **not** rule-bound.

Covers [[Cost Behaviour]], [[Full Costing]], [[Budgeting]] and [[Investment Appraisal]].

**Contrast:** [[Financial Accounting]]
