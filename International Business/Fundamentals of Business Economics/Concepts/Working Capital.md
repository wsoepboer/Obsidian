# Working Capital

> **Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities**

The resources financing day-to-day operations. Management balances liquidity vs profitability across:

- **Inventory** — optimal order size (**EOQ**), avoid stockouts and excess holding cost.
- **Receivables** — credit policy, chasing, bad debts.
- **Payables** — pay on time (not early), use discounts.
