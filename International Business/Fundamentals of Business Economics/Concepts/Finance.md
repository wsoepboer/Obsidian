# Finance

Concerned with how a business **raises funds** (from owners and lenders) and **invests** them to create [[Wealth Creation|wealth]].

Helps identify the forms of finance available, their costs, benefits and risks, and the role of financial markets. Drives both **financing** decisions (see [[Equity Finance]], [[Debt Finance]]) and **investment** decisions (see [[Investment Appraisal]]).
