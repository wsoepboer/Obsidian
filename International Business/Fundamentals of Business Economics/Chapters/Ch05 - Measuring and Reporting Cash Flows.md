**Prev:** [[Ch04 - Accounting for Limited Companies]] · **Next:** [[Ch06 - Analysing and Interpreting Financial Statements]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the purpose of the [[Statement of Cash Flows]].
- Distinguish the three categories of cash flow.
- Prepare and interpret the statement.

## Why cash is not profit
A firm can be profitable yet **run out of cash**. The [[Statement of Cash Flows]] explains why cash rose or fell regardless of reported [[Profit]].

Three categories:
- **Operating** — day-to-day trading cash flows.
- **Investing** — buying/selling non-current [[Assets]].
- **Financing** — raising/repaying finance, paying [[Dividends]].

Sum the three for the net change in cash. Closely tied to [[Working Capital]] and the [[Cash Conversion Cycle]].

## Self-test
- Give one cash flow in each of the three categories.
- Why can a profitable business still fail?
