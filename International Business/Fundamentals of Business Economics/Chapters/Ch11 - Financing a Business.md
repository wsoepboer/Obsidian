# Financing a Business

**Part · Financial Management** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch10 - Making Capital Investment Decisions]] · **Next:** [[Ch12 - Managing Working Capital]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the main sources of finance.
- Distinguish [[Equity Finance]] from [[Debt Finance]].
- Explain [[Financial Leverage]] and capital structure.

## Core ideas
Two main sources:
- [[Equity Finance]] — from [[Shareholders]]; no repayment obligation, but dilutes ownership and shares profits.
- [[Debt Finance]] — borrowing; cheaper (interest is tax-deductible) and non-dilutive, but adds fixed obligations and **financial risk**.

Other sources: [[Retained Earnings]], trade credit, leasing, venture capital.

[[Financial Leverage]] (gearing) uses debt to amplify returns to owners — magnifying gains **and** losses. The **capital structure** (debt : equity mix) drives the **cost of capital** and financial risk.

## Self-test
- Why is debt normally cheaper than equity?
- How does gearing magnify returns to shareholders — and the risk?
