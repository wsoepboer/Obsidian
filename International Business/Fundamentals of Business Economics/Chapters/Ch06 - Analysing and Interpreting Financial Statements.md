# Analysing and Interpreting Financial Statements

**Part · Financial Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch05 - Measuring and Reporting Cash Flows]] · **Next:** [[Ch07 - The Relevance and Behaviour of Costs]]


## Learning outcomes
- Identify the main areas of financial analysis.
- Calculate and interpret [[Financial Ratio Analysis|financial ratios]].
- Perform [[Horizontal Analysis]] and [[Vertical Analysis]].

## Ratio families
[[Financial Ratio Analysis]] groups ratios into:
- [[Profitability Ratios]] — margins, ROA, ROE.
- [[Liquidity Ratios]] — current ratio, quick ratio.
- [[Efficiency Ratios]] — asset / inventory / receivables turnover.
- [[Gearing Ratios]] — debt-to-equity, interest cover.

## Trend & structure
- [[Horizontal Analysis]] — compare figures **across periods**.
- [[Vertical Analysis]] — express items as a **% of a base** (revenue or total assets).

Ratios are only meaningful **in comparison** — over time or against similar firms. See the [[Formulas Cheat-Sheet]].

## Self-test
- Which ratio family best signals short-term solvency?
- What does a rising receivables-turnover period suggest?
