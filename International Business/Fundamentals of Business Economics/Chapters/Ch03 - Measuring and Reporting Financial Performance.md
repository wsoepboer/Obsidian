# Measuring and Reporting Financial Performance

**Part · Financial Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch02 - Measuring and Reporting Financial Position]] · **Next:** [[Ch04 - Accounting for Limited Companies]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the purpose of the [[Income Statement]].
- Identify its elements and calculate profit measures.
- Explain its link to the [[Statement of Financial Position]].

## The income statement
Measures performance **over a period**:

> **Profit = Revenue − Expenses**

- [[Revenue]] — recognised when goods/services are **delivered**, not when cash arrives.
- [[Expenses]] — recognised when **incurred**, not when paid (includes [[Depreciation]], [[Cost of Goods Sold]]).
- [[Profit]] — in stages: **gross → operating → before tax → after tax**.

## Link to position
Profit increases [[Retained Earnings]] in the [[Statement of Financial Position]]; a loss reduces them. This *articulation* connects the two statements.

## Self-test
- Distinguish gross profit from operating profit.
- Why can revenue be recognised before cash is received?
- Where does the year's profit end up on the statement of financial position?
