# Making Capital Investment Decisions

**Part · Management Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch09 - Budgeting]] · **Next:** [[Ch11 - Financing a Business]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the nature of capital investment decisions.
- Apply [[Investment Appraisal]] techniques.
- Compare the methods.

## Core ideas
Capital investments are large, long-term and often irreversible, so appraisal matters. Four techniques:
- **Payback period** — time to recover the outlay; simple but ignores the [[Time Value of Money]].
- [[Net Present Value]] (**NPV**) — discounts all cash flows; the recommended method.
- **Internal rate of return (IRR)** — the discount rate where NPV = 0.
- **Profitability index** — PV of inflows ÷ outlay; useful to rank under capital rationing.

The unifying principle is the [[Time Value of Money]]: money today is worth more than money later.

## Self-test
- Why is NPV theoretically superior to payback?
- What does an IRR above the cost of capital tell you?
