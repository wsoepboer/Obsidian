# Accounting for Limited Companies

**Part · Financial Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch03 - Measuring and Reporting Financial Performance]] · **Next:** [[Ch05 - Measuring and Reporting Cash Flows]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the nature of a [[Limited Company]].
- Distinguish types of [[Shares]].
- Account for [[Share Capital]] and interpret company statements.

## Core ideas
A [[Limited Company]] is incorporated — a **separate legal identity** from its owners ([[Shareholders]]). Features: [[Limited Liability]], perpetual succession, management by directors.

Equity is divided into [[Shares]]:
- [[Ordinary Shares]] — voting rights, variable [[Dividends]], last in a wind-up.
- [[Preference Shares]] — fixed dividend, preferential claim, usually no vote.

Issued above par → the excess is [[Share Premium]]. Profits kept in the business are [[Retained Earnings]].

## Self-test
- What does limited liability protect a shareholder from?
- Contrast ordinary and preference shares on risk, return and voting.
- Where does share premium sit in equity?
