# Full Costing

**Part · Management Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch07 - The Relevance and Behaviour of Costs]] · **Next:** [[Ch09 - Budgeting]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the purpose of [[Full Costing]] (absorption costing).
- Calculate product costs.
- Discuss its advantages and limitations.

## Core ideas
[[Full Costing]] assigns **all manufacturing costs** — direct materials, direct labour and a share of overhead — to each product. Overhead is spread using a **cost driver** (labour hours, machine hours).

- **Product cost** = direct materials + direct labour + absorbed overhead.
- Useful for **pricing** and required for financial reporting of inventory.
- Weakness: **arbitrary overhead allocation** can distort product costs and mislead decisions.

## Self-test
- Why can two products with identical direct costs end up with different full costs?
- When would full-cost information mislead a decision maker?
