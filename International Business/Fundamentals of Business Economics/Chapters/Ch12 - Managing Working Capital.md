# Managing Working Capital

**Part · Financial Management** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch11 - Financing a Business]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the nature of [[Working Capital]].
- Manage inventory, receivables and payables.
- Evaluate the [[Cash Conversion Cycle]].

## Core ideas
> **Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities**

Good [[Working Capital]] management balances liquidity against profitability across three elements:
- **Inventory** — order in optimal quantities (**EOQ**), avoid stockouts and excess holding cost.
- **Receivables** — set credit policy, chase late payers, write off bad debts.
- **Payables** — pay on time (not early), use discounts, keep suppliers onside.

The [[Cash Conversion Cycle]] ties them together:
> **CCC = Inventory period + Receivables period − Payables period**

A shorter cycle frees up cash.

## Self-test
- What does the EOQ minimise?
- Why can lengthening payables improve cash flow but damage supplier relations?
- How would you shorten the cash conversion cycle?
