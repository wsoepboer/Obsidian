# The Relevance and Behaviour of Costs

**Part · Management Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch06 - Analysing and Interpreting Financial Statements]] · **Next:** [[Ch08 - Full Costing]]


## Learning outcomes
- Classify costs in different ways.
- Explain how different costs behave.
- Use cost information for decision-making.

## Core ideas
This chapter opens **Part 2 — Management Accounting**. Managers must understand [[Cost Behaviour]] to plan and decide.

Costs can be classified **by function** (manufacturing, selling, admin), **by behaviour** (fixed, variable, semi-variable — see [[Cost Behaviour]]), and **by relevance to a decision** (see [[Relevant Costs]]).

For decisions, only [[Relevant Costs]] matter — costs that **differ between alternatives**. Ignore sunk and committed costs.

## Self-test
- Why is a sunk cost irrelevant to a decision?
- Give an example of a semi-variable cost and split it conceptually.
- How does an [[Relevant Costs|opportunity cost]] differ from an out-of-pocket cost?
