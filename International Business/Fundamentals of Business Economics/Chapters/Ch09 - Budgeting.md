# Budgeting

**Part · Management Accounting** — [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]
**Prev:** [[Ch08 - Full Costing]] · **Next:** [[Ch10 - Making Capital Investment Decisions]]


## Learning outcomes
- Explain the purpose of [[Budgeting]].
- Prepare and link the main budgets.
- Explain how budgets are used for **control**.

## Core ideas
A **budget** is a detailed financial plan for a future period. [[Budgeting]] serves **planning, control, motivation, coordination and communication**.

- **Operating budgets:** sales → production → materials/labour/overhead.
- **Financial budgets:** cash budget, capital budget, budgeted income statement & [[Statement of Financial Position]].
- **Control:** compare actual vs budget; the gap is a **variance** (favourable or adverse) that triggers investigation.
