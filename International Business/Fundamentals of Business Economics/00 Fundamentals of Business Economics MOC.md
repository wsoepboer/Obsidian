## The three financial statements
- [[Statement of Financial Position]] — what we own & owe *at a point in time*
- [[Income Statement]] — wealth generated *over a period*
- [[Statement of Cash Flows]] — cash movements *over a period*

Held together by the [[Fundamental Accounting Equation]].
