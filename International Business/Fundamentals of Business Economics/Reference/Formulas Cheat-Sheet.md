# Formulas Cheat-Sheet

[[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]

## Position & performance
- **[[Fundamental Accounting Equation]]:** Assets = Liabilities + Equity  →  Equity = Assets − Liabilities
- **[[Profit]]:** Profit = Revenue − Expenses
- **Gross profit** = Revenue − [[Cost of Goods Sold]]
- **Operating profit** = Gross profit − operating expenses
- **[[Cost of Goods Sold]]** = Opening inventory + Purchases − Closing inventory

## Ratios ([[Financial Ratio Analysis]])
- **Current ratio** = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities
- **Quick ratio** = (Current assets − Inventory) ÷ Current liabilities
- **Gross margin** = Gross profit ÷ Revenue
- **Net margin** = Net profit ÷ Revenue
- **ROA** = Operating profit ÷ Total assets
- **ROE** = Profit for the year ÷ Equity
- **Asset turnover** = Revenue ÷ Total assets
- **Inventory turnover** = COGS ÷ Average inventory
- **Receivables days** = (Receivables ÷ Credit sales) × 365
- **Debt-to-equity** = Debt ÷ Equity
- **Interest cover** = Operating profit ÷ Interest

## Decisions & finance
- **Payback period** = Initial outlay ÷ Annual cash inflow *(even flows)*
- **[[Net Present Value|NPV]]** = Σ [ CFₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ ] − Initial outlay  → accept if NPV > 0
- **IRR** = discount rate where NPV = 0
- **Profitability index** = PV of future cash flows ÷ Initial outlay
- **[[Working Capital]]** = Current assets − Current liabilities
- **[[Cash Conversion Cycle|CCC]]** = Inventory period + Receivables period − Payables period
- **EOQ** = √( (2 × Annual demand × Order cost) ÷ Holding cost per unit )
