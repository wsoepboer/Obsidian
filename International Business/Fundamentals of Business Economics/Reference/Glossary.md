# Glossary

Alphabetical key terms. Each links to its atomic concept note. [[00 Fundamentals of Business Economics MOC|↩ MOC]]

- **[[Accounting]]** — collecting, analysing and communicating financial information.
- **[[Assets]]** — resources owned by the business (current / non-current).
- **[[Budgeting]]** — preparing detailed financial plans for a future period.
- **[[Cash Conversion Cycle]]** — inventory + receivables − payables periods.
- **[[Cost Behaviour]]** — fixed, variable and semi-variable costs.
- **[[Cost of Goods Sold]]** — cost of inventory sold in the period.
- **[[Debt Finance]]** — funds raised by borrowing.
- **[[Depreciation]]** — allocating an asset's cost over its useful life.
- **[[Dividends]]** — distributions of profit to shareholders.
- **[[Efficiency Ratios]]** — asset/inventory/receivables turnover.
- **[[Equity]]** — owners' residual claim (Assets − Liabilities).
- **[[Equity Finance]]** — funds raised from shareholders.
- **[[Expenses]]** — resources consumed in earning revenue.
- **[[Finance]]** — raising and investing funds to create wealth.
- **[[Financial Accounting]]** — accounting for external users.
- **[[Financial Leverage]]** — using debt to amplify owner returns (gearing).
- **[[Financial Ratio Analysis]]** — assessing performance via ratios.
- **[[Full Costing]]** — assigning all manufacturing costs to products.
- **[[Fundamental Accounting Equation]]** — Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
- **[[Gearing Ratios]]** — debt-to-equity, interest cover.
- **[[Horizontal Analysis]]** — comparing figures across periods.
- **[[Income Statement]]** — performance over a period (Profit = Revenue − Expenses).
- **[[Investment Appraisal]]** — payback, NPV, IRR, profitability index.
- **[[Liabilities]]** — amounts owed by the business.
- **[[Limited Company]]** — incorporated business, separate legal identity.
- **[[Limited Liability]]** — owners liable only up to their investment.
- **[[Liquidity Ratios]]** — current and quick ratios.
- **[[Management Accounting]]** — accounting for internal managers.
- **[[Net Present Value]]** — PV of cash flows − initial outlay.
- **[[Ordinary Shares]]** — voting shares with a residual claim.
- **[[Preference Shares]]** — fixed-dividend, preferential shares.
- **[[Profit]]** — surplus of revenue over expenses.
- **[[Profitability Ratios]]** — margins, ROA, ROE.
- **[[Qualities of Financial Information]]** — relevance, faithful representation, comparability, verifiability.
- **[[Relevant Costs]]** — costs that differ between alternatives.
- **[[Retained Earnings]]** — profits kept in the business.
- **[[Revenue]]** — income earned from sales.
- **[[Risk and Return]]** — higher return demands higher risk.
- **[[Share Capital]]** — amount invested by shareholders.
- **[[Share Premium]]** — amount received above par value.
- **[[Shares]]** — units of company ownership.
- **[[Stakeholders]]** — groups with an interest in the business.
- **[[Statement of Cash Flows]]** — cash movements over a period.
- **[[Statement of Financial Position]]** — position at a point in time.
- **[[Time Value of Money]]** — money today is worth more than later.
- **[[Users of Accounting Information]]** — internal and external users.
- **[[Vertical Analysis]]** — items as a % of a base figure.
- **[[Wealth Creation]]** — creating and retaining profit long-term.
- **[[Working Capital]]** — current assets − current liabilities.
