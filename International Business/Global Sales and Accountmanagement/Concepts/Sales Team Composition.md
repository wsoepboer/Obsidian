# Sales Team Composition

First of the five named elements of the [[Account Strategy]]: *"advice on the composition of the sales team."*

The decisions to actually make, rather than describe:

- **Local or expatriate or hybrid** — local hires bring language, relationships and market knowledge; sent staff bring product depth and company alignment. Most technical exporters end up with a small sent core and local commercial staff.
- **Employed or agent** — a consequence of the entry mode, not a separate choice → [[Market Entry Strategy]]. A distributor entry may mean no local team at all, just an account manager travelling.
- **Roles and mix** — technical sales versus account management versus support. Safety-critical equipment needs someone who can answer a standards question in the room.
- **Size and coverage** — how many accounts per person, and how territory is split.
- **Incentives and management** — what is rewarded, and who they report to.

Cultural fit is graded here: the rubric requires the account recommendation to take *cultural* considerations into account, and how a buyer expects to be sold to is precisely a team-design question → [[Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions]].
