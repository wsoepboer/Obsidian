# Responsible Commercial Decisions

The closing phrase of the learning outcome: *"to make **responsible** commercial decisions based on market data, risk analysis and organisational objectives."* Easy to read past, and it is doing three things.

- **Responsible to the organisation** — decisions the company can actually carry: affordable, resourced, and consistent with its objectives rather than with an ambition you have projected onto it → [[Practical Advice]].
- **Responsible in conduct** — legal and ethical: product safety and certification, anti-corruption, honest claims, fair treatment of partners and agents. For safety-critical equipment sold through intermediaries, this is not abstract → [[Corruption Risk]].
- **Responsible under uncertainty** — grounded in *"market data, risk analysis"*, so the risks are named and the decision is made in view of them rather than despite them → [[Market Entry Risk]].

The practical consequence for the report: a recommendation is not complete at the point it is attractive. It is complete when it is affordable, compliant, and its downside has been stated. Saying what would make you reverse the recommendation is a mark of the third kind of responsibility, not a weakness.
