# Customer Acquisition

The rubric requires it explicitly: *"the student advises on how to **acquire** international customers in the chosen target markets."* The learning outcome's word for it is **build**, in *"build, maintain and expand international customer relationships"*.

In a new market the acquisition problem is not persuasion, it is **access and credibility**: nobody knows the brand, existing suppliers are entrenched, and for safety-critical equipment the buyer's risk of switching is real.

The mechanisms available are the ones the rubric already names — which is convenient, because it means acquisition is where the five [[Account Strategy]] elements come together rather than a separate topic:

| Mechanism | Note |
|---|---|
| Find and recruit distributors or dealers | [[Market Entry Strategy]] |
| Exhibit where buyers already are | [[Trade Fairs]] |
| Generate and capture enquiries | [[Online Strategy]] |
| Borrow credibility | [[Influencer Marketing]] |
| Direct approach to target accounts | [[Sales Team Composition]] · [[Top Customer Segment]] |

Make it concrete for the assigned countries: who is the first customer worth winning, why would they switch, and what proof do they need. Cost per acquisition is a legitimate figure to estimate, and it feeds the financial perspective.
