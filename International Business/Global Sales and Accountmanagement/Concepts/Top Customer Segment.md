# Top Customer Segment

One of the six **Excellent** markers in the assessment matrix — *"++ Top customer segment"* — and the only one that is purely analytical rather than a matter of depth.

The idea: not all customers in a new market are worth the same effort, so identify the segment that is most attractive and build the strategy around it rather than around the market as a whole. For Altrex that could be a buyer type (construction contractors, industrial maintenance, rental companies, retail, public bodies), a size band, or a use-case where its product genuinely wins.

The reasoning the marker rewards:

1. **Segment** the market on criteria that matter — volume, price sensitivity, certification requirements, buying process, service needs.
2. **Score** the segments on attractiveness and on Altrex's ability to serve them.
3. **Name the top segment** and say why, and be explicit about which segments you are choosing **not** to pursue.
4. **Align everything else to it** — entry mode, team, channels, fairs, retention plan.

Done properly this makes the whole report sharper, because a segment-specific recommendation is automatically more [[Practical Advice|practical]] than a country-level one. It is also inherently data-driven → [[Data-Driven Commercial Decisions]].
