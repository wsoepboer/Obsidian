# The Six Perspectives

The analytical grid the rubric imposes, and the single most useful structural fact about this course. The export recommendation must be substantiated from:

**economic · financial · cultural · marketing · logistical · legal**

The same list — in a five-item variant — is then required again for the risk discussion (*"from at least legal, financial, marketing, cultural and logistical perspectives"*), and again in the account recommendation (*"taking cultural, legal, logistical and financial considerations into account"*).

Three times. Which means: build the grid once, apply it three times.

| Perspective | What goes in it for Altrex |
|---|---|
| **Economic** | Market size and growth, construction and industrial demand, competition, price levels |
| **Financial** | Margin, landed cost, payment terms and credit risk, currency, investment required, payback |
| **Cultural** | Buying and negotiation norms, relationship expectations, language, safety culture → [[Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions]] |
| **Marketing** | Positioning, channel expectations, brand recognition, promotion, [[Trade Fairs]] |
| **Logistical** | Bulky-goods freight, mode, lead time, warehousing, returns → [[Transportation Modes]], [[Cross-Border Logistics]] |
| **Legal** | Product safety standards and certification, liability, agency and distributor law, tariffs and compliance |

> [!note] The Excellent markers cluster in two of them
> *"++ Detailed legal and financial implications"* and *"++ Logistical impact analyses"* are three of the six Excellent markers. Depth in **legal, financial and logistical** is the cheapest available route above *Good*.
