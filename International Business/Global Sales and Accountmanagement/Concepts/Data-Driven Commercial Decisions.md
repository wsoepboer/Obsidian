# Data-Driven Commercial Decisions

A graded requirement twice over: the learning outcome asks for *"responsible commercial decisions based on **market data**, risk analysis and organisational objectives"*, the criterion asks that the student *"demonstrates the use of **relevant data** to support decision-making and shows that these decisions are aligned with the organisation's objectives"*, and *"++ Data analysis"* is one of the six Excellent markers.

> [!important] Two separate tests, and the second is the one students fail
> **Use data**, and **show the decision follows from it**. A report full of statistics followed by a recommendation that does not reference them has failed the second test — the criterion's word is *aligned*.

What counts as usable data here: market size and growth, import and export statistics, construction and industrial output, competitor presence and pricing, tariff and duty rates, freight costs and transit times, wage levels, currency series, and corruption or ease-of-doing-business indices. All of it must be **current, reliable and traceable** → [[Traceable Sources]].

How to make the alignment visible: state Altrex's objective, show the figure, draw the inference, make the recommendation. Then attach a measure so the recommendation can be tracked — the same discipline as [[Supply Chain KPIs]] in the sister course.
