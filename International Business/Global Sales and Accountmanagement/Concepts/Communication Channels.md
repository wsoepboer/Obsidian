# Communication Channels

Second of the five named [[Account Strategy]] elements: which channels are used to reach and maintain customers, and which the customer actually expects.

The range is wide and the choice is cultural as much as commercial: face-to-face visits, phone, email, video calls, messaging apps that dominate in some markets and are absent in others, distributor portals, tender platforms, and the [[Online Strategy|digital]] and [[Trade Fairs|event]] channels the rubric names separately.

Three things make this a recommendation rather than a list:

- **Match the channel to the buying process.** A technical, high-value, multi-stakeholder purchase is not closed by email; a repeat consumable order should not require a visit.
- **Match it to local norms.** Formality, directness, response-time expectations and preferred medium vary — this is where the *cultural* consideration lands.
- **Match it to cost.** Channel choice is the main driver of cost to serve, and therefore of which accounts are worth serving how → [[Top Customer Segment]].
