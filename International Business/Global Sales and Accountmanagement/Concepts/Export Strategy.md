# Export Strategy

One of the two strategies the learning outcome names — *"develops a well-substantiated **export** and account strategy"* — and the outward half of the assignment: how Altrex gets its products into a market it is not yet in.

The rubric specifies the deliverable precisely:

> The student has analysed and mapped the export opportunities for **at least one EU country and one non-EU country**, using current, reliable and traceable sources. The student has substantiated the recommendation from economic, financial, cultural, marketing, logistical and legal perspectives.

Three obligations follow. The opportunity must be **mapped**, not asserted — market size, growth, competition, demand drivers, with figures. The EU / non-EU pair is a **floor**, chosen so that the contrast between an internal-market entry and a third-country entry is visible: tariffs, certification, currency and distance all change. And the substantiation runs through all six perspectives → [[The Six Perspectives]].

Where it stops and [[Account Strategy]] begins: export strategy is about **entering** a market; account strategy is about **holding customers** once you are in it.
