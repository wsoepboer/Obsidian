# Online Strategy

Fourth of the five named [[Account Strategy]] elements: *"online strategies."*

For a B2B manufacturer this is rarely e-commerce and mostly **demand generation and enablement**: a localised site in the buyer's language, search visibility for the terms specifiers actually use, technical documentation and certification downloads, product configurators, LinkedIn presence and outreach, and content aimed at the people who write specifications rather than at end users.

Three points worth making in the report:

- **The buyer researches before contact.** Much of a technical purchase decision happens before a salesperson is involved, so online material is part of the sales process rather than marketing decoration.
- **Localisation is not translation.** Standards references, units, product names and the platforms in use differ by market — another place the *cultural* consideration is concrete.
- **It is measurable**, which links it to [[Data-Driven Commercial Decisions]]: traffic, enquiry source, conversion by market. That data is also what justifies the channel mix.

Watch the interaction with the entry mode: pushing online demand generation while selling through exclusive distributors creates leads someone else has to be paid to serve → [[Market Entry Strategy]].
