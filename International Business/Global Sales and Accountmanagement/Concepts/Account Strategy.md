# Account Strategy

The second of the two required strategies, and the course's other name — *Global Sales and **International Account Management*** — puts it on equal footing with export.

The rubric enumerates it, and the list is a checklist rather than a suggestion:

> a well-supported recommendation on account management, including advice on the **composition of the sales team, communication channels, participation in relevant trade fairs, online strategies and influencer marketing**, while taking cultural, legal, logistical and financial considerations into account.

Five named elements, each with its own note: [[Sales Team Composition]] · [[Communication Channels]] · [[Trade Fairs]] · [[Online Strategy]] · [[Influencer Marketing]]. Omit one and a graded element is simply missing.

The learning outcome adds the purpose: *"strategically **build, maintain and expand** international customer relationships"* — three verbs, mapping onto [[Customer Acquisition]] and [[Customer Retention and Development]].

> [!important] It is a plan, not a list
> The five elements must fit together and fit the country: a distributor-led entry implies a different team, different channels and different fairs than a direct-sales entry. Consistency with your [[Market Entry Strategy]] is what makes it *"well-supported"* rather than five paragraphs.
