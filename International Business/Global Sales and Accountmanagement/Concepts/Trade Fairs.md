# Trade Fairs

Third of the five named [[Account Strategy]] elements — *"participation in relevant **trade fairs**"* — and separately one of the six **Excellent** markers (*"++ Trade fairs"*). Two mentions in one rubric makes this unusually cheap marks.

Why they matter for a company like Altrex: in industrial and construction equipment, fairs are where distributors are found, where a physical, certified product can be demonstrated and stood on, and where competitors' ranges and prices become visible in an afternoon.

> [!important] "Relevant" is the graded word
> A generic paragraph about the value of trade fairs earns nothing. **Name the fairs** — the actual industry events in the assigned countries or their regions — with when they run, what they cost to exhibit at, who attends, and what you would aim to leave with (distributor meetings, qualified leads, a demo slot).
>
> That specificity is the whole difference between the *Satisfactory* and the *Excellent* treatment, and it is straightforward desk research → [[Traceable Sources]].

Then connect it: a fair is an [[Customer Acquisition|acquisition]] mechanism and a [[Communication Channels|channel]], and its cost belongs in the financial perspective.
