# Global Sales & Accountmanagement Glossary

Every term named in the course material, one line each. [[00 Global Sales and Accountmanagement MOC|↩ MOC]]

> [!info] Scope
> Built from the Brightspace material (org unit `201011`) captured 3 September 2026: the *Welcome* module, the **assessment matrix** and the **Additional information assignment** page. With no lecture material published, the rubric's vocabulary is the syllabus — terms the course has not named are listed unwritten in [[Sales Frameworks Index]].

## The two strategies
- **[[Export Strategy]]** — how the product enters a market it is not yet in; must cover ≥1 EU and ≥1 non-EU country.
- **[[Account Strategy]]** — how customers are built, maintained and expanded once you are in; five named elements.
- **[[Market Entry Strategy]]** — the entry mode, **plus the modes you rule out and why**.
- **[[Country Selection]]** — the countries are *assigned*, not chosen; the analysis must be tailored to them.

## The analytical frame
- **[[The Six Perspectives]]** — economic, financial, cultural, marketing, logistical, legal. Required three times over.
- **[[Traceable Sources]]** — *current, reliable and traceable*; referencing is a mandatory criterion.
- **[[Data-Driven Commercial Decisions]]** — use the data **and** show the decision follows from it.
- **[[Responsible Commercial Decisions]]** — responsible to the organisation, in conduct, and under uncertainty.
- **[[Practical Advice]]** — advice Altrex could act on; an Excellent marker and an added line in the rubric's learning outcome.

## Risk
- **[[Market Entry Risk]]** — nine named categories, discussed from at least five perspectives.
- **[[Corruption Risk]]** — singled out by name; largely inherited through intermediaries.

## The commercial machinery
- **[[Sales Team Composition]]** — local vs sent, employed vs agent, roles, size, incentives.
- **[[Communication Channels]]** — matched to the buying process, to local norms, and to cost to serve.
- **[[Trade Fairs]]** — named events with dates and cost; a rubric element *and* an Excellent marker.
- **[[Online Strategy]]** — B2B demand generation and enablement, localised, measurable.
- **[[Influencer Marketing]]** — credible practitioners and specifiers, not consumer reach.

## The customer
- **[[Customer Acquisition]]** — the rubric's *acquire*, the outcome's *build*.
- **[[Customer Retention and Development]]** — the rubric's *retain and further develop*, the outcome's *maintain and expand*.
- **[[Top Customer Segment]]** — segment, score, name the best one, say what you are not pursuing.

## Course machinery
- **Altrex** — the case company, Zwolle; ladders and height-access equipment → [[The Altrex Assignment]].
- **Assigned countries** — allocated to you, because Altrex already has a strong presence in many markets.
- **Mandatory criteria** — six pass/fail gates; missing one caps the grade at 5 → [[Assessment Matrix - Global Sales and Accountmanagement]].
- **Coaching sessions** — **attendance is a pass prerequisite**; dates not published.
- **Excellent markers** — the six `++` items: practical advice · legal and financial implications · logistical impact analyses · top customer segment · trade fairs · data analysis.
- **Oral assessment** — written report, then present and defend.
