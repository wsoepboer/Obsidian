## The brief, verbatim
> **Assignment Explanation**
>
> For the Zwolle-based company **Altrex**, you will explore opportunities to expand the sales of its products into new countries. As Altrex already has a strong presence in many markets, you will be **assigned specific countries** to focus on.
>
> For these countries, you will develop a **detailed and well-founded recommendation** based on the **theories and frameworks introduced in the various lectures**. Your recommendation should be specifically tailored to the country or countries assigned to you.
>
> In addition to submitting a written report, you will **present and defend** your findings.

## Three constraints the brief sets
1. **The countries are assigned, not chosen.** *"Altrex already has a strong presence in many markets"* — so the assigned countries will be ones where it does not, and the analysis has to be genuinely tailored rather than generic.
2. **The theories come from the lectures.** *"based on the theories and frameworks introduced in the various lectures"* — which is why the missing lecture material matters for the grade, not just for studying. Chase it. → [[Sales Frameworks Index]]
3. **You defend it orally.** The report is not the end of the assessment.

## The company
**Altrex**, Zwolle — a manufacturer of ladders, steps, scaffolding and other height-access equipment. Establish before you start: its product ranges and who buys them, its current markets and how it sells into them (distributors, dealers, direct, online), its competitors, and the safety **standards and certification** its products must meet per market.

> [!note] Why certification will drive the legal perspective
> Height-access equipment is safety-critical and standards-governed. Approval regimes, conformity marking and liability differ by jurisdiction, and outside the EU they do not carry over. Expect the *legal* perspective and *"++ Detailed legal and financial implications"* to be won or lost here.

Also note that the assignment sells a **physical, bulky product** — which makes the *logistical* perspective real rather than decorative, and connects directly to [[00 Global Dynamics and Supply Chain Management MOC|Global Dynamics & SCM]]: [[Transportation Modes]], [[Cross-Border Logistics]], [[Trade Barriers and Tariffs]], [[Lead Time]].
