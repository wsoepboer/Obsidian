# Country Selection — Picking the Sub-Saharan African market

Applying a country selection model to narrow "south of the Sahara" down to one country. [[00 Global Sales and Accountmanagement MOC|↩ MOC]] · [[Project Plan - Altrex]] · [[Country Selection]] · [[Interview - Rudy (Altrex)]]

> [!warning] Which model, exactly?
> No specific "country selection model" is named anywhere in this course's Brightspace material or in the vault. What follows is the **standard funnel/scorecard version** used generally in export-market selection (preliminary screening → weighted criteria scoring → final pick). If a different named model was taught or handed out in class, swap it in — the structure below (shortlist → criteria → weighted score → pick) will still hold, only the criteria and weights would change.

## Denmark needs no selection step
Denmark is an assigned country, not a shortlist to narrow — go straight to the export/risk/account analysis for Denmark under [[Project Plan - Altrex]] Phase 3 onward. This document is only for the Sub-Saharan Africa side, where "south of the Sahara" is a region and the rubric needs one specific country.

## Step 1 — Candidate shortlist
Five candidates, chosen because each plausibly fits Altrex's stated growth verticals (construction, infrastructure, mining, agriculture, disaster relief) and its new-country focus (retail professional + online pure players, rental):

- **South Africa**
- **Kenya**
- **Nigeria**
- **Ghana**
- **Rwanda**

*(If the team has other candidates in mind — say, a specific region Rudy or Chiel flagged — add them here before scoring.)*

## Step 2 — Screening criteria and weights (draft — adjust as a team)
| Criterion | Weight | Why it matters for Altrex |
|---|---|---|
| Construction & infrastructure growth | 20% | Directly maps to the stated structural growth driver |
| Presence of named growth verticals (mining, agriculture, disaster relief) | 15% | These are Rudy's specific end-customer opportunity list |
| Political & economic stability | 15% | Feeds the risk analysis (financial, political, market entry risk) |
| Logistics access — ports, customs, lead time | 15% | Altrex ships bulky physical equipment; this is not decorative |
| Retail professional / online segment maturity | 15% | This is the stated segment focus for new countries |
| Regulatory / certification compatibility | 10% | Altrex's "broad compliance coverage" product strategy needs a market where that product actually clears certification |
| Entry cost / partner or distributor availability | 10% | Feeds the market-entry mode decision (private label needs a partner) |

## Step 4 — What to source before scoring
| Criterion | Data to pull | Candidate source type |
|---|---|---|
| Construction/infra growth | Construction market size & growth forecasts, infrastructure investment pipelines | Industry reports (e.g. Oxford Business Group, sector-specific market research), government infrastructure plans |
| Growth verticals | Mining/agriculture output data, disaster-risk exposure | World Bank, national statistics offices, sector associations |
| Stability | Political risk indices, currency volatility | World Bank Governance Indicators, IMF, Economist Intelligence Unit |
| Logistics | Port throughput, customs clearance times, Logistics Performance Index | World Bank LPI, national port authority data |
| Retail pro/online maturity | E-commerce penetration, specialised B2B retail presence | Statista, national e-commerce association reports |
| Regulatory fit | Local certification/standards regime for scaffolding/access equipment | National standards bodies, trade/export advisory services |
| Entry cost/partners | Existing distributor networks, private-label partner candidates | Trade directories, chamber of commerce, Altrex's own contacts |
