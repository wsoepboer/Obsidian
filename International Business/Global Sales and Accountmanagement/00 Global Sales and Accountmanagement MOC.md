## The rubric as a syllabus
Read the assessment matrix as a specification and it decomposes into six deliverables. Each is a concept note.

| # | What the rubric demands | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Export opportunities analysed and mapped for **≥1 EU and ≥1 non-EU country**, from current, reliable, traceable sources | [[Export Strategy]] · [[Country Selection]] · [[Traceable Sources]] |
| 2 | The recommendation substantiated from **six perspectives**: economic, financial, cultural, marketing, logistical, legal | [[The Six Perspectives]] |
| 3 | An **account management** recommendation: sales team composition, communication channels, trade fairs, online strategy, influencer marketing | [[Account Strategy]] · [[Sales Team Composition]] · [[Communication Channels]] · [[Trade Fairs]] · [[Online Strategy]] · [[Influencer Marketing]] |
| 4 | **Market-entry strategy** for a selected country — including **why certain entry strategies are not recommended** | [[Market Entry Strategy]] |
| 5 | **Market-entry risks** — nine named categories, discussed from at least five perspectives | [[Market Entry Risk]] · [[Corruption Risk]] |
| 6 | **Customer acquisition, retention and development**, with **data-driven** decisions aligned to organisational objectives | [[Customer Acquisition]] · [[Customer Retention and Development]] · [[Data-Driven Commercial Decisions]] · [[Responsible Commercial Decisions]] |

> [!important] The rubric repeats itself on purpose
> The list of six perspectives appears **three times** — once for the export recommendation, once for the account recommendation, once for the risk discussion. That is not padding; it is the structure of the report. Build the grid once and apply it three times.

## How Satisfactory becomes Good
The two descriptors are nearly identical, and the differences are exactly four words:

| | Satisfactory | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Country analysis | *"analysed and mapped"* | *"**thoroughly** analysed and mapped"* |
| Export recommendation | *"substantiated"* | *"**detailed and well-substantiated**"* |
| Account recommendation | *"well-supported"* | *"**detailed** and well-supported"* |
| Everything else | identical | identical |

So *Good* is not a different report — it is the same structure at greater depth. Depth here means: named sources, real figures, specific channels and named trade fairs, and the rejected options argued rather than skipped.

## The six Excellent markers
The matrix lists what *exceeds* the learning outcome, verbatim:

> ++ Practical advice · ++ Detailed legal and financial implications · ++ Logistical impact analyses · ++ Top customer segment · ++ Trade fairs · ++ Data analysis

Pick two or three and do them properly rather than gesturing at all six. Notes: [[Practical Advice]] · [[Top Customer Segment]] · [[Trade Fairs]] · [[Data-Driven Commercial Decisions]].
