# Plan of Approach — Altrex Export & Account Strategy

## 1. The company

**Altrex**, Zwolle — manufacturer of stairs, ladders, scaffolding and other height-access equipment: stepladders, ladders, mobile towers, suspended platforms. Current project focus is **aluminium mobile towers**. Operates internationally with offices in the Netherlands, Belgium and Spain (Catalonia). Sells through five segments — retail DIY, retail professional, online, rental, end user — and has stated that **retail professional and online (specialised pure players) plus rental are the focus for new-country expansion**, with retail DIY de-prioritised as a structurally shrinking segment. Already has one precedent for entering a new market: Germany, via a local partner on a private-label basis. Source: [[Interview - Rudy (Altrex)]].

## 2. The problem we are assigned to address

The brief: explore expansion of Altrex's sales into **assigned countries** — Denmark (satisfies the rubric's EU floor) and one country to be selected from south of the Sahara (satisfies the non-EU floor) — and deliver a **detailed, well-founded recommendation specifically tailored to each country**, substantiated by the theories and frameworks from the lectures.

The assessment matrix decomposes this into six components, each of which the report must cover for the assigned countries:

1. **Export opportunity analysis** for Denmark and the selected Sub-Saharan country, from current, reliable, traceable sources
2. **Substantiation from six perspectives** — economic, financial, cultural, marketing, logistical, legal
3. **Account management recommendation** — sales team composition, communication channels, trade fairs, online strategy, influencer marketing
4. **Market-entry strategy**, including why certain entry strategies are not recommended
5. **Risk analysis** — nine named categories, discussed from at least five perspectives
6. **Customer acquisition, retention and development**, backed by data and aligned to Altrex's objectives

The selected Sub-Saharan country is not yet fixed. [[Country Selection - Sub-Saharan Africa]] sets out the method — a shortlisted set of candidates scored against weighted criteria tied to Altrex's own stated growth drivers (construction, infrastructure, mining, agriculture, disaster relief) — but the score cells are empty until real data is sourced. The pick follows the data, not the other way round.
