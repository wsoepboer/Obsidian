## Learning outcome
Verbatim from the Brightspace *Welcome* module:

> The student analyses international market opportunities and develops a substantiated export and account strategy for an organisation, integrating cultural, legal, logistical and financial aspects. The student is able to strategically build, maintain and expand international customer relationships, and to make responsible commercial decisions based on market data, risk analysis and organisational objectives.

> [!note] Two wordings exist — the rubric's is the one that counts
> The **assessment matrix** phrases it slightly differently and adds a sentence the Welcome text omits:
>
> > The student analyses international market opportunities and develops a well-substantiated export and account strategy for an organisation, integrating cultural, legal, logistical and financial considerations. **The student is able to provide practical advice applicable to an organisation.** The student is able to provide strategic advice on building, maintaining and expanding international customer relationships and to make responsible commercial decisions based on market data, risk analysis and organisational objectives.
>
> *"Practical advice applicable to an organisation"* is a graded requirement, and *"++ Practical advice"* is the first of the six Excellent markers. Write advice Altrex could act on, not analysis about Altrex.
