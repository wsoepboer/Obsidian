## 0. Company profile and assignment, as of the Rudy interview

Full detail: [[Interview - Rudy (Altrex)]]. Summary relevant to task sequencing:

- **Assigned countries:** Denmark (EU) and a country within Sub-Saharan Africa (non-EU) — specific country inside the region still needs picking (see Phase 0)
- **Products:** stepladders, ladders, mobile towers, suspended platforms; current project focus is **aluminium mobile towers**
- **Offices:** NL, BE, Spain (Catalonia)
- **Segments:** retail DIY, retail professional, online, rental, end user — **retail professional and online (pure players/specialised) plus rental are the stated focus for new-country expansion**; retail DIY is de-prioritised (structurally shrinking market)
- **Entry precedent:** Germany was entered via a **local partner on a private-label basis** — a real option to weigh against generic entry modes in Phase 5
- **Growth drivers to screen against Denmark and Sub-Saharan Africa specifically:** construction, infrastructure, disaster relief, demographic change, changing weather/construction requirements, and named end-customer verticals (nuclear, aircraft/ship maintenance, archaeology, mining, restoration, disaster/search-and-rescue, agriculture)
- **Compliance:** standards differ by country and change over time; Altrex's current practice is mass-production designed for broad regulatory coverage rather than a bespoke product per country
- **MOQ:** flexed depending on relationship — sometimes waived for new customers/countries, sometimes applied

> [!important] The one rule that decides Satisfactory vs Good here
> Unlike the SCM course, this rubric's Satisfactory and Good descriptors are nearly word-for-word identical. The only differences are "thoroughly," "detailed and well-substantiated," and "detailed and well-supported." That means: same structure, same six sections — the grade is won on **named sources with real figures, specific channels and named trade fairs, and rejected entry modes actually argued rather than mentioned.** There is no extra section that buys a better grade.

> [!note] Excellent markers — pick 2–3, don't gesture at all six
> ++ Practical advice · ++ Detailed legal and financial implications · ++ Logistical impact analyses · ++ Top customer segment · ++ Trade fairs · ++ Data analysis. Decide which ones to actually deliver once the assigned countries are known (Phase 0) — logistics and certification/legal are the natural fit given Altrex sells bulky, safety-certified equipment.
