# Ch7 — Human Resource Management

**Lectures · Organisation, Strategy & HRM** — [[00 Organisational Strategy and HR MOC|↩ Lectures MOC]] · [[Welcome|Course Home]]
**Prev:** [[06 The Future of Work]] · **Next:** [[12 Organisational Culture & Ethics]]


## Overview
Managing people as the organisation's key strategic resource.

## What HRM is
[[Human Resource Management]] is an **integrated, strategic** approach to human qualities — people are a **benefit, not a cost**. HRM policy aligns with organisational strategy.

## The HRM instrument cycle
[[HRM Instruments]]: **recruitment → career development → learning & development → assessment → reward → offboarding**.

- [[Recruitment and Selection]] — right person, right position; increasingly tech-driven (chatbots, screening, games) — followed by [[Onboarding (5 Cs)]] (Compliance, Clarification, Culture, Connection, Check-back).
- **Career development** — hiring for a career, not one job; vertical & horizontal moves; tools: mentoring, career counselling, assessment centre, self-study.
- **Learning & Development** — a source of competitive advantage.
- [[Performance Assessment]] — must be **fair, uniform, diligent**; assessment interview (hierarchical, backward) ≠ performance review (equal, forward).
- [[Reward]] — tangible + intangible; based on the job ([[Job Rating]]) and the performance; note the gender pay gap.
- [[Offboarding & Outplacement]] — managing exits well.

## Modern HRM
[[Talent Management]], [[Agile HRM]] (continuous dialogue, team focus), the [[The New Way of Working]] (hybrid), and [[Diversity and Inclusion]].
