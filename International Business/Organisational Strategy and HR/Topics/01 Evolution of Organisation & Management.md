# Ch1 — Evolution of Organisation & Management

**Next:** [[02 Environmental Influences]]


## Overview
How management emerged as a field and how organisations grew into today's multinationals.

## What organisation & management mean
- **Organising** = combining and structuring tasks, people and resources so an organisation's goals are reached.
- **Management** = the leading and steering of an organisation; responsibility sits with owners/senior people and anyone delegated that role.
- The field's original name is **organisational behaviour** — an interdisciplinary (not merely multidisciplinary) science with both a **descriptive** side (how organisations actually behave) and a **prescriptive** side (advice on design and action), judged on **effectiveness**: how successfully direction toward a goal is achieved.

## Origins of the field
- Ancient roots: **Socrates and Plato** (4th century BC) theorised about leadership, task allocation and **specialisation**.
- First taught as a subject in the **US, late 19th century** — as businesses expanded during the [[Industrial Revolutions|Industrial Revolution]], management grew complex enough to need special skills.

## Schools of management thought
[[Schools of Management Thought]] traces the field's development: **Taylor** (Scientific Management) → **Fayol** (General Management) → **Weber** (Bureaucracy) → **Mayo** (Human Relations) → **Likert** (Neo-Human Relations) → **Boulding** (Systems Approach) → **Lawrence & Lorsch** (Contingency Approach) → recent theories (1980+).

## Development of trade & multinationals
- Earliest international trade via **trade routes** — the **Silk Road** (1st century BC) linked Europe, the Middle East and Asia (Roman & Chinese civilisations).
- The **Netherlands** led world trade by shipping around **1600**.
- Multinationals: **~3,000** at the start of the 20th century → **~100,000** by end of 2022.
- ~**445 of the world's largest 500 companies** are founded in North America, Europe and Japan.

See [[Internationalisation vs Globalisation]] for how this expansion continues today.
