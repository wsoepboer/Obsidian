## Overview
How technology and society are reshaping jobs, skills and organisations.

## Industrial revolutions
[[Industrial Revolutions]]: 1st (1760–1840, steam) · 2nd (1870–1914, mass production) · 3rd (1960–1990, automation) · 4th (from 2012). The [[Fourth Industrial Revolution]] brings cyber-physical systems, mobile internet, algorithms and **AI** — reshaping jobs.

## Organisational lifespan
[[Organisational Lifespan]] depends on balancing three "management commandments": run operations effectively, create new business, and end business that no longer fits.

## The future of jobs
- Opportunities: more women on boards (still ~1 in 10), international careers, fewer working hours from higher productivity.
- **New competencies:** active learning (knowledge dates fast), creativity, digital skills.
- **New jobs:** ~65% of today's primary-school children will do jobs that don't yet exist; WEF — ~75M jobs lost but ~133M created (positive net).

## Lifelong learning & the new way of working
[[Lifelong Learning]] — voluntary, self-motivated learning across a career; the Netherlands scores low. Mindsets: growth, serial mastery, own your development. The [[The New Way of Working]] (hybrid work) is the workplace response.
