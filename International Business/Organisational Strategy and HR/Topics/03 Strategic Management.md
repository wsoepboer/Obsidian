## Overview
How organisations set a direction and align to their environment.

## Strategy & the strategic process
[[Strategy & Strategic Management]]: a **strategy** is a plan to achieve goals; **strategic management** keeps the organisation aligned to its environment and able to change. Two approaches — **classical** (strategic *planning*) vs **modern** (strategic *thinking*).

## Situation analysis
[[Situation Analysis]] scans internal strengths/weaknesses and external opportunities/threats, using tools such as the [[BCG Matrix]] (portfolio) and [[Porter's Five Forces]] (competitive environment). Competitive analysis asks: who are the competitors, what do we need to know, how intense is rivalry, and what is our **differential competitive advantage**?

## Strategy formation (three phases)
1. **Define the picture of the future** — can current strategy reach the goals? If not, adjust.
2. **Develop different strategies** — expansion via the [[Ansoff Product-Market Matrix]]; positioning via [[Porter's Competitive Strategies]] (cost leadership, differentiation, focus) and [[Treacy & Wiersema Value Strategies]] (operational excellence, product leadership, customer intimacy).
3. **Evaluate and choose** a strategy.

## Supporting tools & growth
- [[Business Intelligence]] supports effective, efficient decision-making.
- [[Mergers and Takeovers]] are a route to growth — driven by strategic reorientation, competitive position and **synergy** (1+1=3), though **>40% fail**.

- Name the three phases of strategy formation.
- Compare Porter's generic strategies with Treacy & Wiersema's value disciplines.
