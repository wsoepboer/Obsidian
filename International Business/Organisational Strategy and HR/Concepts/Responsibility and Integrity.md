# Responsibility and Integrity

**Three types of responsibility** (Van Dalen, 2017):

- **Liability** — set in **law** (e.g. seat belts).
- **Task responsibility** — from arrangements **at work**.
- **Virtue responsibility** — **moral**, no formal arrangement.

Five criteria of virtue responsibility: autonomous decisions · take obligations seriously · weigh different norms · recognise consequences · be personally accountable.

**Integrity** = **moral self-determinism** — identifying moral obligations, balancing them, and maintaining personal morality.
