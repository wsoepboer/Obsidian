# Onion Model

Hofstede & Hofstede's layered view of culture, outer to core:

- **Symbols** — space, time, language, clothes.
- **Heroes** — admired figures (Steve Jobs, Johan Cruyff).
- **Rituals** — collective habits (ringing the bell, celebrating a milestone).
- **Values** — the invisible core.

Symbols, heroes and rituals are grouped as visible **practices**; values are the hidden centre.
