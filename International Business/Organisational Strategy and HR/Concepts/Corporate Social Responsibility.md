# Corporate Social Responsibility

The idea that organisations are responsible for more than profit.

- **Classic perspective** (Anglo-Saxon, US/UK): profit is management's **only** responsibility; social responsibility should still maximise shareholder profit. Advocated by Nobel laureate **Milton Friedman** (1976).
- **Social-economic view** (Rhineland/Rijnland, NL/DE/CH/Scandinavia): organisations should make a **positive contribution to society** — **people, planet, profit**. Adopted in phases: owners-only → employer-driven → stakeholder-driven → society-as-a-whole (Robbins & Coulter).

Instruments and codes: **OECD Guidelines**, the **UN Global Compact** (four pillars: human rights, labour, environment, anti-corruption; 9,000+ firms) and **ISO 26000** (worldwide CSR guideline).

Related: **social enterprises** (Tony's Chocolonely, Rituals, Ben & Jerry's) and [[Sustainable Development]].
