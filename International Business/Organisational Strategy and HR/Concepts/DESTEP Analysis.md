# DESTEP Analysis

A scan of the **macro-environment** — the external forces an organisation cannot control but must respond to.

**DESTEP** factors: **D**emographic, **E**conomic, **S**ocial, **T**echnological, **E**cological/environmental, **P**olitical.

The lectures use the fuller **STIRDEEPER**: Society, Technology, Industry, Resources, Demographics, Economics, Environment, Politics, Energy, Religion.

- **Economic** — national income, inflation, employment; internationally, currency and interest rates.
- **Political** — includes the [[Forms of Economic Integration]] between countries.
- **Social** — ethics, individualisation, [[Corporate Social Responsibility]].
