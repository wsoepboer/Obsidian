# Outsourcing & Offshoring

- **Outsourcing** — having **others** perform activities (at home or abroad). Motives = **4 Cs**: **costs, capital, capability, capacity**. Forms: full, collaborative (joint venture), assigning activities (separate dept.), partial.
- **Offshoring** — transferring activities **across borders**.

**Social criticism** of moving to low-wage countries centres on **employment** and **working conditions** → responses like the Sustainable Clothing & Textile agreement and **Fairtrade**.
