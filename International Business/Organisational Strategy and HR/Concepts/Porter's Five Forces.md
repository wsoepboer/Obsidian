# Porter's Five Forces

Michael Porter's model of the **competitive pressure in an industry** — the higher the forces, the lower the potential profitability.

The five forces:
1. **Rivalry** among existing competitors
2. **Threat of new entrants**
3. **Threat of substitutes**
4. **Bargaining power of suppliers**
5. **Bargaining power of buyers**

Example contrast: **airlines** (high rivalry, low margins) vs **big pharma** (high entry barriers, patents → stronger position).
