# Uppsala Model

A **process theory**: firms internationalise **gradually**, in stages, increasing commitment as they gain knowledge and reduce perceived risk of foreign markets.

Contrast with [[Born Globals]], which skip the gradual path.

**See also:** [[Stages of Internationalisation]] · [[Theories of Internationalisation]]
