# Theories of Internationalisation

Why and how organisations go international:

- **Trade theories** — why trade occurs **between countries**.
- **Static theories** — why organisations undertake international activities.
- **Process theories** — **how** organisations internationalise → the [[Uppsala Model]] (stages) and [[Born Globals]].
