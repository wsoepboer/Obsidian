# Born Globals

Organisations that are **international from establishment**.

Characteristics: home market is irrelevant; founders have strong international experience; multiple markets developed **simultaneously**; **technology** is vital; close cooperation with foreign partners (networks). Examples: **Booking.com, Uber, Alibaba**.
