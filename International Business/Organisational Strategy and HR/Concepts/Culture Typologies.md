# Culture Typologies

Ways to classify organisational cultures:

- **Group-process** approach (affectivity, causality, hierarchy, change, cooperation, orientation to other groups).
- **Harrison & Handy** — power, role, task, person cultures.
- **Sanders & Neuijen**.
- **Professional culture** typology.
- [[Schein's Three-Layer Model]].
- [[Kets de Vries & Miller Typology]].
- **Quinn & Rohrbaugh** — the competing-values framework.
