# Strategy & Strategic Management

- **Strategy** — a plan setting out an organisation's intentions to achieve its goals.
- **Strategic management** — keeping the organisation properly **aligned to its environment** while developing the skills to change strategy when needed.

Two approaches:
- **Classical** — strategic management = strategic **planning** (formal, analytical).
- **Modern** — strategic management = strategic **thinking** (adaptive, creative).
