# Onboarding (5 Cs)

Integrating a new employee — the **5 Cs**:

1. **Compliance** — basic rules.
2. **Clarification** — mutual expectations.
3. **Culture** — vision, mission, values.
4. **Connection** — the social structure.
5. **Check-back** — feedback to the new employee.
