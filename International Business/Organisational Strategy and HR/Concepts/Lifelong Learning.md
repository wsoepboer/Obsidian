# Lifelong Learning

Voluntary, self-motivated acquisition of knowledge across a career — needed because people work longer and knowledge dates fast. The **Netherlands scores low** (little post-initial education; low participation of over-55s).

**Mindsets:** focus on growth · strive for serial mastery · stretch yourself · build your brand & network · own your development · do what you love · stay vibrant.
