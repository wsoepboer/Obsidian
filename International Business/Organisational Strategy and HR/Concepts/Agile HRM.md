# Agile HRM

An adaptive approach to HRM:

- **Performance** — continuous dialogue, not one yearly assessment.
- **Coaching** — managers coach more.
- **Teams** — focus on team performance ("team intelligence").
- **Reward** — shortly after desired behaviour.
- **Recruitment** — prioritise approved vacancies.
- **Training** — online modules, AI.
