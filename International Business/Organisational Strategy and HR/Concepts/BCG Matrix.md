# BCG Matrix

The Boston Consulting Group **portfolio** model — plots business units on **market growth** (vertical) × **relative market share** (horizontal):

- **Stars** — high growth, high share (invest).
- **Cash cows** — low growth, high share (harvest).
- **Question marks** — high growth, low share (decide).
- **Dogs** — low growth, low share (divest).

Used in [[Situation Analysis]] to balance a portfolio of activities.
