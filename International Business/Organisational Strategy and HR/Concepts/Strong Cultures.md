# Strong Cultures

Characteristics of a **strong** culture (Robbins & Coulter, 2015):

- Values enjoy **broad support**.
- Messages about what matters are **consistent**.
- Employees can tell stories about **history and heroes**.
- Employees **strongly identify** with the culture.
- Strong link between **shared values and behaviour**.
