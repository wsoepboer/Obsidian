# Porter's Competitive Strategies

Michael Porter's three **generic** ways to compete:

1. **Cost leadership** — be the lowest-cost producer.
2. **Differentiation** — offer something uniquely valued.
3. **Focus** — target a narrow segment (by cost or differentiation).

Being "stuck in the middle" erodes advantage.
