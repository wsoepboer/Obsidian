# Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions

Six dimensions comparing **national** cultures:

1. **Power distance** — acceptance of unequal power (high = hierarchy accepted).
2. **Individualism vs collectivism** — individual vs group focus.
3. **Masculinity vs femininity** — competition/achievement vs cooperation/quality of life.
4. **Uncertainty avoidance** — need for rules and predictability.
5. **Long- vs short-term orientation** — persistence/thrift vs tradition/quick results.
6. **Indulgence vs restraint** — free gratification vs suppressed gratification.
