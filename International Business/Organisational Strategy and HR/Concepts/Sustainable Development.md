# Sustainable Development

Meeting present needs without compromising the future — a central strategic theme.

- **UN Sustainable Development Goals** (2015–2030) and the **Paris Climate Treaty** (2015): limit warming to 1.5°C.
- Dutch **National Climate Agreement** (2019): −49% greenhouse gases by 2030, −95% by 2050 vs 1990.
- Trends: **agricultural innovation** (9bn people by 2050), **alternative nourishment** (synthetic meat, vegan), **ecovenience** (eco + convenience, less packaging), **reconomie** (recycle-economy).
