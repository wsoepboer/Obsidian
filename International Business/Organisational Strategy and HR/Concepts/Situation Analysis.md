# Situation Analysis

The diagnostic stage of strategy: assess **internal** strengths/weaknesses and **external** opportunities/threats (SWOT), then locate competitive advantage.

Competitive analysis asks: Who are the competitors? What must we know about them? How intense is rivalry? What **differential competitive advantage** do we hold in each market?

Tools used here: [[BCG Matrix]] (portfolio) and [[Porter's Five Forces]] (industry).
