# Internationalisation vs Globalisation

- **Internationalisation** — expanding activities across borders; rose sharply **after WWII** and again with the **internet**.
- **Globalisation** — broader: the worldwide **integration** of countries and continents, not only products and services.
- **Outsourcing** — relocating activities to low-income countries (India, Vietnam, China).
