# Treacy & Wiersema Value Strategies

Three **value disciplines** — pick one to lead on, be adequate on the others:

1. **Operational excellence** — best total cost / reliability.
2. **Product leadership** — best product, continuous innovation.
3. **Customer intimacy** — best total solution, tailored to the customer.
