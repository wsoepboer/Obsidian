# Human Resource Management

An **integrated, strategic** approach to the human qualities in an organisation. Core convictions:

- Top management recognises the **human factor**.
- HRM policy aligns with **strategic planning**.
- People are a **benefit, not a cost**; their qualities can be used better.
- Strategy realisation hinges on people's qualities and **motivation**.
- HRM systems are implemented professionally and by plan.
