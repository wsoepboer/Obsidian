# Diversity and Inclusion

- **Diversity** — the mix of **visible and invisible** differences between people.
- **Inclusion** — all employees are treated equally, fairly and with respect, and given equal opportunity and resources to contribute.

Differences can spark tension or exclusion because people tend to exclude the "strange" — inclusion counters this.
