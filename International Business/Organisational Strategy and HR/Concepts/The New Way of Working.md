# The New Way of Working

A vision (from the 2010s, driven by the [[Fourth Industrial Revolution]]) to make work more **effective, efficient and enjoyable** for organisation and employee — the prelude to **hybrid work**.

Four areas: **ICT · physical work environment · organisation · people (behaviour)**.

**Hybrid work** = combining place- and time-independent work. Hybrid leadership should be **clear, human, improvisational**; hybrid employees balance **growth, autonomy, connectivity**.
