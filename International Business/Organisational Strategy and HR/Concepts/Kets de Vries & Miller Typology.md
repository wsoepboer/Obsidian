# Kets de Vries & Miller Typology

Five cultures that **do not function optimally** ("clinical pictures"):

- **Depressive** — lack of self-confidence.
- **Compulsive** — fixation on control.
- **Dramatic** — attention-seeking, subjective.
- **Paranoid** — suspicion and mistrust.
- **Schizoid** — reserved, uninvolved.
