# Schein's Three-Layer Model

Edgar Schein's three levels of culture:

1. **Artefacts** — visible structures and behaviour.
2. **Espoused values** — stated strategies, goals, philosophies.
3. **Basic underlying assumptions** — unconscious, taken-for-granted beliefs (the real core).
