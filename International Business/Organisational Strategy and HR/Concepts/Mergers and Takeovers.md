# Mergers and Takeovers

Forms of collaboration for growth:

- **Merger** — amalgamation of firms on the basis of **equality**.
- **Takeover** — **unilateral**; the acquired party conforms to the acquirer's plans.

**Motives:** strategic reorientation (saturated markets), competitive position (one takeover triggers another), and **synergy** (the "1 + 1 = 3" effect).

**Reality:** **~40% of mergers fail** to get off the ground, and **half of acquisitions are reversed within six years** — scale advantages can flip into complacency once growth costs more than it benefits.
