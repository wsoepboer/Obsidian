# Four Ethical Stances

Four stances for making ethical decisions:

1. **Utilitarian** — judge by **results**; best for the majority. Founded by British philosopher **Jeremy Bentham** (1748–1832).
2. **Individual (liberal)** — the individual's **rights and freedom** are central.
3. **Principle of justice** — divide costs and benefits **proportionately and impartially** (e.g. equal pay for equal work).
4. **Moral** — **fundamental human rights** are central (privacy, free speech; UN Declaration of Human Rights, 1948).
