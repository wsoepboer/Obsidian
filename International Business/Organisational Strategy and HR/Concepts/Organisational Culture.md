# Organisational Culture

The collection of **shared views** about the job, about each other, about oneself and about the organisation. An organisation's **management style / policy** is the key ingredient of its culture.

Hofstede & Hofstede's widely-used definition: **"the collective mental programming that distinguishes one group of people from another"** — applies equally to organisations and to countries/national cultures.

Studied through models: [[Onion Model]], [[Iceberg Model]], and the [[Culture Typologies]].
