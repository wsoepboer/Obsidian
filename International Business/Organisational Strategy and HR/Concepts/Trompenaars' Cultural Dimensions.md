# Trompenaars' Cultural Dimensions

Seven dimensions of national culture (Trompenaars):

1. **Universalism vs particularism** — rules vs relationships.
2. **Individualism vs communitarianism** — individual vs group.
3. **Neutral vs affective** — controlled vs openly expressed emotion.
4. **Specific vs diffuse** — sharp vs blurred work/private separation.
5. **Achievement vs ascription** — status from performance vs position/background.
6. **Sequential vs synchronic time** — linear vs flexible/parallel time.
7. **Internal vs external control** — control the environment vs adapt to it.
