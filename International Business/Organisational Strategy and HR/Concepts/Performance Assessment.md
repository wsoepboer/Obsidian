# Performance Assessment

Rating how well an employee fills a role over a period (e.g. 1–5). Must be:

- **Fair** — substantiated, with a right of appeal.
- **Uniform** — common criteria, forms, procedures.
- **Diligent** — complete and accurate.

**Assessment interview** (hierarchical, backward-looking) ≠ **performance review** (equal, future-oriented). Modern principles (Van der Berg): focus on **talent**, ask for **feedback**, **continuous dialogue**, **enabling** management.
