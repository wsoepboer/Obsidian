# Industrial Revolutions

Four waves that reshaped work:

1. **First** (1760–1840) — the **steam engine**.
2. **Second** (1870–1914) — **mass production** / industrial production.
3. **Third** (1960–1990) — **automation** of work.
4. **Fourth** (from 2012) — see [[Fourth Industrial Revolution]].
