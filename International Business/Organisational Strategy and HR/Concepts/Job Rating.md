# Job Rating

Valuing a position to set pay. Methods:

- **Ranking** — order main positions by salary, slot the rest.
- **Factor comparison** — benchmark key positions against similar external roles.
- **Classification** — define job categories.
- **Points** — decompose a role into factors and score them.
