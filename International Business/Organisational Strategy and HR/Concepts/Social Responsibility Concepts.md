# Social Responsibility Concepts

Three concepts of an organisation's social responsibility:

1. **Traditional** — serves at the discretion of the **shareholders**.
2. **Social-responsibility** — serves the interests of **all parties involved**.
3. **Empowerment** — also takes initiative toward a **better society**.

Organisational (social) responsibility and individual (ethical) responsibility are extensions of each other.
