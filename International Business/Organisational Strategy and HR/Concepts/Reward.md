# Reward

All **tangible and intangible** things that direct behaviour and contribute to performance (wages, training, holidays, home-working, autonomy).

Two drivers: the **job** performed ([[Job Rating]]) and **how** it is performed (performance). Determination methods: **market-value** (external earning power) and **systematic** (external competitiveness + internal fairness). Trend: results-oriented, flexible terms. Note the **gender pay gap** (NL ~5%, UK ~22%, DE ~25%).
