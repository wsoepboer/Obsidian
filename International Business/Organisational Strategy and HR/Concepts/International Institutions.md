# International Institutions

Bodies that shape the global economy:

- **UN** (1945, 193 states) — peace, cooperation, human rights, SDGs; ICJ in The Hague.
- **World Bank** (1944) — loans to fight poverty in developing countries.
- **IMF** (1944) — monetary stability; fees scaled to GDP.
- **WTO** — promotes trade, settles disputes, removes barriers (states keep sovereignty).
- **OECD** (~35 market democracies) — sustainable growth, employment, the "Better Life Index".
- **WEF** — parties (not countries); annual **Davos** meeting.
- **BRICS** — NDB & CRA as a counterpart to the World Bank/IMF; plus **OPEC**.
