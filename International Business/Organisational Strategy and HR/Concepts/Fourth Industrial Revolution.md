# Fourth Industrial Revolution

From ~2012: **cyber-physical systems**, mobile internet, algorithms and **artificial intelligence**.

Consequences for jobs: technical skills date quickly; ~**65%** of today's primary pupils will hold jobs that don't yet exist; WEF estimate — ~**75M jobs lost** but ~**133M created** (positive net). Response: [[Lifelong Learning]] and [[The New Way of Working]].
