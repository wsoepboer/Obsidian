# Forms of Economic Integration

Five forms of integration between countries, in **increasing** depth:

1. **Free-trade zone** — remove mutual trade restrictions (EFTA, USMCA, ASEAN, AfCFTA).
2. **Customs union** — free-trade zone **+** common external tariffs (SACU).
3. **Common market** — customs union **+** free movement of goods, people, capital, services (the **EU** internal market).
4. **Economic & monetary union** — common market **+** shared currency (**19 of 27** EU states use the euro).
5. **Full political & economic union** — countries merge completely (e.g. formation of the USA).

**EU institutions:** European Commission (executive), European Council (political direction), Council of the EU + Parliament (legislation & budget), Court of Justice (compliance).
