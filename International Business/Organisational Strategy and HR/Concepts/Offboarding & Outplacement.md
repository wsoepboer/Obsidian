# Offboarding & Outplacement

Managing exits: retirement, end of contract, long-term illness, labour conflict, or leaving for another job.

**Outplacement** — systematic coaching of departing employees to find new suitable work (career analysis, psychological support, application techniques, tests). Part of a good offboarding process.
