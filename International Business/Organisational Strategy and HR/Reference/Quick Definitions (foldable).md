## Environment & strategy

> [!abstract]- DESTEP Analysis
> Scan of the macro-environment: **D**emographic, **E**conomic, **S**ocial, **T**echnological, **E**cological, **P**olitical (lectures use the fuller STIRDEEPER). → [[DESTEP Analysis]]

> [!abstract]- Porter's Five Forces
> Competitive pressure in an industry: rivalry, threat of entrants, threat of substitutes, supplier power, buyer power. Higher forces → lower profitability. → [[Porter's Five Forces]]

> [!abstract]- Corporate Social Responsibility
> Responsibility beyond profit — **people, planet, profit**; classic (Anglo-Saxon) vs social-economic (Rijnland) view. → [[Corporate Social Responsibility]]

> [!abstract]- BCG Matrix
> Portfolio model on market growth × relative share: **stars, cash cows, question marks, dogs**. → [[BCG Matrix]]

> [!abstract]- Ansoff Product-Market Matrix
> Growth strategies by product × market: penetration, product development, market development, diversification. → [[Ansoff Product-Market Matrix]]

> [!abstract]- Porter's Competitive Strategies
> Three generic strategies: **cost leadership, differentiation, focus**. → [[Porter's Competitive Strategies]]

> [!abstract]- Treacy & Wiersema Value Strategies
> Three value disciplines: **operational excellence, product leadership, customer intimacy**. → [[Treacy & Wiersema Value Strategies]]

## Internationalisation

> [!abstract]- Forms of Economic Integration
> Increasing depth: free-trade zone → customs union → common market → economic & monetary union → full union. → [[Forms of Economic Integration]]

> [!abstract]- Uppsala Model
> Firms internationalise **gradually**, in stages, as market knowledge grows. → [[Uppsala Model]]

> [!abstract]- Born Globals
> Firms **international from establishment** (Booking.com, Uber, Alibaba). → [[Born Globals]]

## HRM

> [!abstract]- HRM Instruments
> The cycle: recruitment → career development → learning & development → assessment → reward → offboarding. → [[HRM Instruments]]

> [!abstract]- Onboarding (5 Cs)
> Compliance, Clarification, Culture, Connection, Check-back. → [[Onboarding (5 Cs)]]

## Culture

> [!abstract]- Onion Model
> Hofstede's layers: **symbols → heroes → rituals → values**. → [[Onion Model]]

> [!abstract]- Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions
> Six: power distance, individualism/collectivism, masculinity/femininity, uncertainty avoidance, long/short-term, indulgence/restraint. → [[Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions]]

> [!abstract]- Trompenaars' Cultural Dimensions
> Seven, incl. universalism vs particularism, specific vs diffuse, achievement vs ascription. → [[Trompenaars' Cultural Dimensions]]
