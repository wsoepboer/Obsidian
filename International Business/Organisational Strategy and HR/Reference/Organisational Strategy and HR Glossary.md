# Organisational Strategy and HR Glossary

Key terms from the lecture strand. [[00 Organisational Strategy and HR MOC|↩ Lectures MOC]]

- **[[Agile HRM]]** — adaptive HRM: continuous dialogue, team focus.
- **[[Ansoff Product-Market Matrix]]** — expansion via product × market.
- **[[BCG Matrix]]** — portfolio by growth and market share.
- **[[Born Globals]]** — firms international from establishment.
- **[[Business Intelligence]]** — information/analysis/IT for decisions.
- **[[Corporate Social Responsibility]]** — people, planet, profit.
- **[[Culture Typologies]]** — ways to classify culture.
- **[[DESTEP Analysis]]** — macro-environment scan (STIRDEEPER).
- **[[Diversity and Inclusion]]** — mix of differences; equal treatment.
- **[[External Parties (Stakeholders)]]** — outside parties influencing the firm.
- **[[Forms of Economic Integration]]** — free-trade zone → full union.
- **[[Fourth Industrial Revolution]]** — cyber-physical systems, AI.
- **[[Four Ethical Stances]]** — utilitarian, individual, justice, moral.
- **[[GLOBE Model]]** — nine cultural dimensions.
- **[[HRM Instruments]]** — recruitment → offboarding cycle.
- **[[Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions]]** — six national-culture dimensions.
- **[[Human Resource Management]]** — strategic management of people.
- **[[Iceberg Model]]** — visible vs hidden culture (McClelland).
- **[[Industrial Revolutions]]** — four waves of change.
- **[[International Institutions]]** — UN, IMF, World Bank, WTO, OECD, WEF, BRICS.
- **[[Internationalisation vs Globalisation]]** — cross-border vs worldwide integration.
- **[[Job Rating]]** — valuing a position to set pay.
- **[[Kets de Vries & Miller Typology]]** — five neurotic cultures.
- **[[Lifelong Learning]]** — voluntary continuous learning.
- **[[Mergers and Takeovers]]** — M&A and synergy.
- **[[Onboarding (5 Cs)]]** — compliance, clarification, culture, connection, check-back.
- **[[Onion Model]]** — symbols, heroes, rituals, values.
- **[[Organisational Culture]]** — shared views shaping behaviour.
- **[[Organisational Lifespan]]** — three management commandments.
- **[[Outsourcing & Offshoring]]** — 4 Cs; moving activities abroad.
- **[[Performance Assessment]]** — fair, uniform, diligent rating.
- **[[Porter's Competitive Strategies]]** — cost, differentiation, focus.
- **[[Porter's Five Forces]]** — industry competitive pressure.
- **[[Recruitment and Selection]]** — right person, right position.
- **[[Responsibility and Integrity]]** — liability/task/virtue; moral self-determinism.
- **[[Reward]]** — tangible + intangible remuneration.
- **[[Schein's Three-Layer Model]]** — artefacts, values, assumptions.
- **[[Situation Analysis]]** — SWOT and competitive analysis.
- **[[Social Responsibility Concepts]]** — traditional, social, empowerment.
- **[[Stages of Internationalisation]]** — export → greenfield.
- **[[Strategy & Strategic Management]]** — plan + environmental alignment.
- **[[Strong Cultures]]** — broadly shared, consistent values.
- **[[Sustainable Development]]** — SDGs and sustainability trends.
- **[[Talent Management]]** — attract, develop, retain top staff.
- **[[The New Way of Working]]** — hybrid, flexible work.
- **[[Theories of Internationalisation]]** — trade, static, process.
- **[[Treacy & Wiersema Value Strategies]]** — operational, product, customer.
- **[[Trompenaars' Cultural Dimensions]]** — seven cultural dimensions.
- **[[Uppsala Model]]** — gradual internationalisation.
