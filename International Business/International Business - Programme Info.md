## Tests and exams

These are the rules, and they are the stable part of the site.

> [!tip] Course notes lead here too
> The rules below are the programme default. **A course's own resit and assessment arrangements override them.** Example: [[Family Business & Research - Course Info]] fixes the resit at the **examination weeks of term 3**, for a failing group or individual — that is the operative date for that course, whatever the generic period calendar implies. Check the Course Info note before planning around a period.

### Test chances — the expensive rule
- **Two test chances per academic year, per test.**
- **Enrolling consumes a chance.** If you enrol and then do not sit the test, you must cancel **before the test starts** — otherwise the chance is burned.
- **No enrolment, no test.** There is no walk-in.

### Enrolment
- Enrol in **Educator → My enrollments**, or **My Windesheim → Enrollments → Tests**.
- A confirmation email lands in your Windesheim mailbox. **Check that it arrived** — the site says so twice.
- **Interim tests** (during class weeks): the window opens 4 weeks before the test and closes **2 weeks before** it.
- Missed the deadline? Only four groups can still enrol: students not linked to the right programme, mid-year switchers, Kies-op-Maat and exchange students, and students with Examination Board permission. Email `bmr-studievoortgang@windesheim.nl` with your name, student number and reason.

> [!danger] The published enrolment windows are last year's — period 1 may be open right now
> The site lists these for the **central** test-taking weeks, on a page headed *2024-2025* and containing *2025-2026* dates:
>
> | Period | Window as published (stale) |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | Mon 1 Sep – Sun 28 Sep 2025 |
> | 2 | Mon 10 Nov – Sun 7 Dec 2025 |
> | 3 | Mon 2 Feb – Sun 8 Mar 2026 |
> | 4 | Mon 13 Apr – Sun 17 May 2026 |
> | 5.1 | Mon 13 Apr – Sun 17 May 2026 |
> | 5.2 | Mon 29 Jun – Wed 8 Jul 2026 |
>
> **Inference, not fact:** the 2025-2026 period-1 window opened on the Monday classes started. Classes for 2026-2027 started **Mon 31 August 2026**. If the pattern holds, the 2026-2027 period-1 window is open now and closes around **Sun 27 September 2026** — but no one has published that. **Check Educator directly; do not wait for this page to be updated.** A missed enrolment costs a whole test period, and on a custom route you are likelier than most to have units the standard year-group reminders do not cover.

### Sitting the test
- Bring a **student card valid for the current academic year** *(the site still says 2025-2026 — you need the 2026-2027 one)* **and** government ID (passport, driving licence or ID card).
- **Venues** are published no later than **three days** before the test starts. Date, time and venue can change until **two weeks** before the period begins. Checking is explicitly your own responsibility.
- **Start times:** 08:30, 11:30, 14:30, 18:00, 18:30. Tests of 3 hours or longer run only at **14:30 or 18:00**. Full-time tests may be held in the evening during test terms.
- Digital tests are at the **DTC**, first floor of the G Building.

### Periods 5.1 and 5.2
- **5.1:** if you have an unused test chance, enrol — even before period 4 results are published. If you passed in period 4, the 5.1 enrolment is cancelled automatically. The advice is unconditional: *always* enrol for 5.1 if you have chances left.
- **5.2:** gated. You need **≥40 EC in the year group**, or a programme switch, or Examination Board permission. Staff finalise eligible enrolments by mid-July.

### Schedule codes
The IB group code is **`IB.toets`**. Elective modules and minors use **`HKM.toets`**, **`PPR.toets`**, or `Minor<abbreviation>.toets`.

> [!tip] On a custom route, use My Windesheim rather than the group schedule
> The test-taking schedule page states that tests for programmes on the **Personal Learning Pathway** are found on **My Windesheim**, the personal learning environment — not the group schedule. With five units outside the major, `IB.toets` will not show you everything. Build the picture from My Windesheim and treat `IB.toets` as a cross-check.

## Examination Board and exemptions

Requests go to the Examination Board to **deviate from the regular curriculum** — exceptional work placements, resits of old subjects, extending a graduation deadline, a *Kies op Maat* minor. It also handles irregularities during an exam, and disputes with a lecturer over an assignment or exam that cannot be resolved directly.

> [!important] There are two boards, and the split matters on your route
> - **Propaedeutic-phase board** — anything concerning the **year-1** programme.
> - **Main-phase board** — everything else, per degree programme.
>
> Your route straddles both in the standard structure: Fundamentals of Business Economics and Organisational Strategy and HR are year-1 units. Sending a request to the wrong board costs time. **Confirm which phase your exemptions placed each unit in** before filing anything.

**Forms available on the site:** Request extra exam opportunity term 5.2 · Disability form · **Exemption form IB** · Portfolio Regulations.

> [!note] You already know someone on a board
> From [[Family Business & Research - Course Info]]: **Henk Jager** — product owner of that course — is a **member of the examination board GPCM_IB_LM**, **chair of its assessment committee**, and **graduation coordinator**. Useful for an informal read on whether a request is worth filing and to which board, though not a substitute for the formal route.

The governing document is *Regulations on Testing and Examination at Business, Media and Law*, published as `Besluit 2025-010 Regels Tentaminering studiejaar 2025-2026` — again, last year's edition at time of scan.

## Timetable and class lists

- The academic year runs as **two semesters**, each normally **two terms** of teaching weeks followed by test weeks.
- **The class timetable is published four times a year, two weeks before each new term.**
- Load your timetable into the **Windesheim Information Portal (WIP)** and the Windesheim App via WIP, using the class code from the class lists.
- **Class lists 2026-2027, semester 1** are published for all four years, maintained by **Marianne Termeulen**. Year 3 was last modified **31 August 2026**; year 1 is still marked *concept*. All four are mis-tagged `2025-2026` in the library metadata — a metadata slip, not a content one.
- Year-1 class placement is **by language choice** and only final after the September kick-off. Not assigned? → `BMR-studievoortgang@windesheim.nl`, with your programme in the subject line.

> [!question] Does a class code even apply to you?
> Class lists assume you move through a year group as a block. With units spread across three year levels plus electives, a single year-3 class code will not generate a correct timetable. Worth checking whether you have a class code at all, or whether your schedule is assembled per unit in My Windesheim.
