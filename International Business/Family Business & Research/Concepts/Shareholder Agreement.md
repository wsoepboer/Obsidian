# Shareholder Agreement

The **legally binding** agreement between the owners of a family business — named in the [[S3 - Organising Ownership and Decision-Making]] learning objectives alongside the [[Family Charter]].

## Charter vs agreement
| | [[Family Charter]] | Shareholder agreement |
|---|---|---|
| **Binds** | Morally, by consent | **Legally** |
| **Covers** | Values, family employment, ownership expectations, conflict procedures | Share transfers, voting, dividends, minority rights, exit |
| **Governs** | How the **family** relates to the business | How the **owners** relate to each other |

The two are complements, not alternatives: the charter says what the family has agreed it wants; the agreement makes the parts that must hold under disagreement enforceable.

## Where it becomes decisive
The [[S6 - Remaining Successful for Future Generations]] private-equity deliverable turns on it. A **20% minority** stake does not take control — so the real question is what **minority rights** the agreement grants: board seat, veto rights, information rights, exit and drag/tag clauses. That is where a family either keeps or loses its freedom to act on [[Long-Term Orientation]] and [[Patient Capital]].
