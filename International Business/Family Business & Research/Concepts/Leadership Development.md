# Leadership Development

**Identifying, preparing and supporting individuals who have the potential to lead the organisation in the future** — family members **and non-family employees**.

Every family business depends on leadership. The course sharpens the question: *not whether leadership matters, but **where future leaders will come from***. Historically the answer was family members; today many family businesses take a broader view.

## How successful firms do it
- Experience across **different departments**
- Work on **challenging projects**
- **Coaching and mentoring**

And not only technical skills. It includes **communication, decision-making, emotional intelligence, and the ability to inspire others**.

> [!important] The principle
> Future leadership should be based on **capability rather than entitlement**.

## Relation to successor readiness
[[Successor Readiness]] applies the same test to **one named successor**; leadership development applies it to a **pipeline** — and opens that pipeline to non-family employees. It is therefore also the structural answer to [[Nepotism]].
