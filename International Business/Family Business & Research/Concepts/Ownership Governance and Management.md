# Ownership, Governance and Management

The three-role separation that resolves [[Role Ambiguity]]:

| Role | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Ownership** | **Who owns** the business? |
| **Governance** | Who provides **oversight and strategic direction**? |
| **Management** | Who is responsible for **daily operations**? |

Clear role definitions **improve decision-making, reduce conflict and increase accountability** — and matter more as the organisation grows more complex.

> [!tip] Recognise the model
> This is the [[Three-Circle Model]] re-cut for a different purpose.
>
> - *Family / ownership / business* explains **why family firms are complex** — descriptive, [[S1 - What Makes Family Businesses Unique]]
> - *Ownership / governance / management* explains **how to make them manageable** — prescriptive, [[S5 - Professionalisation and Leadership Development]]
>
> Same instinct — separate the systems — applied first to diagnosis, then to design.

## Using it
The separation is also how [[Family Logic vs Business Logic]] gets resolved in practice: business logic governs **management** appointments, family logic governs **ownership** and its returns, and **governance** is the table where the two negotiate.
