# Family Charter

A **document capturing agreements about how the family wants to interact with the business**.

What it may contain:

- **Family values** → [[Family Values]]
- **Rules regarding family employment** — the structural answer to [[Nepotism]]
- **Ownership expectations**
- **Procedures for resolving conflicts** → [[Family Conflict]]

The course's summary claim: **family charters help align expectations.**

## Why writing it down matters
The charter's power is that it is agreed **before a specific person is in the frame**. Rules on family employment written when nobody is applying are rules; the same rules written when a named nephew wants a job are a verdict.

The charter is the **document**; the [[Family Council]] is the **forum** that produces it. It is a moral and relational agreement — its legal counterpart is the [[Shareholder Agreement]].
