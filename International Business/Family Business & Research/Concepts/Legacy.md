# Legacy

People hear *legacy* and think **inheritance**. In family business research it is broader:

> [!important] Definition
> **Legacy refers to what future generations receive and remember.**

Which may include:

- **Financial wealth**
- **Family values** → [[Family Values]]
- **Reputation**
- **Relationships**
- **Social impact**
- **Entrepreneurial spirit**

The strongest legacies **combine economic success with positive societal impact**.

## Why the distinction matters
A family's legacy shapes how future generations **view the business and their own role within it**. So legacy is not only backward-looking sentiment — it is an input to [[Successor Readiness]]: a next generation that inherits a legacy it believes in is far more likely to bring the **commitment** the role requires.

It is also the frame that makes [[Professionalisation]] and [[Founder Dependency|letting go]] acceptable to a founder — *a business that can thrive beyond you* is a legacy, where *a business that needs less of you* is a demotion.
