# Future-Proofing

The organising idea of [[S6 - Remaining Successful for Future Generations]], reached through one question asked of an apparently healthy company:

> **Will this business still be successful twenty years from now?**

Markets change, technology evolves, customer expectations shift, new regulations emerge. Organisations that fail to adapt may not survive. Family businesses carry an **additional** burden: responsibility not only for current performance but for **protecting the business for future generations**.

> [!important] The central tension
> Future-proofing requires **balancing continuity with change**.

That tension recurs in every part of the session: innovation vs identity ([[Innovation Across Generations]]), stewardship vs ownership ([[Stewardship]]), legacy vs inheritance ([[Legacy]]).

## The family business of the future
The firms that thrive will be **adaptable · innovative · sustainable · professionally governed · purpose-driven**, and defined by three refusals:

| Rather than… | They will… |
|---|---|
| Choose between tradition and innovation | **Combine both** |
| Focus only on profit | **Create value for multiple stakeholders** |
| Think in years | **Think in generations** |

> [!tip] The list is a summary of the course
> *Professionally governed* is sessions 3 and 5 · *purpose-driven* is the family values of session 1 · *adaptable* and *innovative* are session 6 · *think in generations* is [[Long-Term Orientation]]. If you need one slide that closes the report, this is it.
