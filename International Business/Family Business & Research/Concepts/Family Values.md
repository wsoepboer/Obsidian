# Family Values

Many family firms are guided by a clear, explicit set of values. The five the course names:

- **Trust**
- **Responsibility**
- **Stewardship** → [[Stewardship]]
- **Reputation**
- **Commitment**

These are not decoration. They **influence decisions about employees, customers and communities**, and they are one of the three reasons family firms often survive longer (with [[Long-Term Orientation]] and [[Patient Capital]]).

## Why they matter later in the course
- One of the four **additional goals** of family firms, alongside preserving [[Legacy|the family legacy]], creating opportunities for future generations, and long-term continuity
- They create the **strong organisational culture** listed as an advantage of family ownership
- They are what [[Professionalisation]] must not destroy: *professional systems provide structure, family values provide purpose*
- They are what a [[Family Charter]] writes down
- They are what a successor's **character** is tested against → [[Successor Readiness]]
