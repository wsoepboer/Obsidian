# Professionalisation

> [!important] Definition
> Professionalisation is **not about becoming less entrepreneurial**. It is about creating the **structures, leadership capabilities and management practices** that allow the business to keep growing **without becoming dependent on a few individuals**.

## The symptoms that trigger it
A firm that has grown from a founder and a few employees into hundreds of employees across several countries starts to show:

- Employees **no longer sure who is responsible for what** → [[Role Ambiguity]]
- Important decisions **depending on a small number of family members** → [[Founder Dependency]]
- Recruitment, performance management and leadership development happening **informally** → [[Leadership Development]]
- Processes that worked when small now **creating bottlenecks**

Which produces the question of [[S5 - Professionalisation and Leadership Development]]: *how can we maintain our entrepreneurial spirit while becoming more professional?*

## The four moves
1. **Founder-centred → organisation-centred** → [[Founder Dependency]]
2. **Clarify roles** → [[Ownership Governance and Management]]
3. **Develop future leaders** → [[Leadership Development]]
4. **Keep the values** — see below

## Structure and purpose
> **Professional systems provide structure. Family values provide purpose.**

Together they create organisations that are both **effective and authentic**. The balance is two-sided:

| Too little structure | Too much bureaucracy |
|---|---|
| Inefficiency | Reduced entrepreneurial flexibility |

So professionalisation does not require abandoning [[Family Values]] — many successful family businesses combine strong professional management with a strong family identity.
