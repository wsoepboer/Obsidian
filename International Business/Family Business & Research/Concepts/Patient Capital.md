# Patient Capital

Family owners are **willing to wait longer for returns on investments**, which allows the firm to invest in innovation and long-term growth.

One of the three reasons the course gives for the longer survival of family firms, with [[Long-Term Orientation]] and [[Family Values]].

## Orientation vs capital
Worth holding the distinction: [[Long-Term Orientation]] is a **mindset** about goals; patient capital is a **financial behaviour** about returns. A family can hold the mindset and still be forced into impatience by debt covenants or a dividend-hungry branch of the family — which is exactly what an external shareholder introduces.

## Where it is tested
The [[S6 - Remaining Successful for Future Generations]] private-equity scenario runs directly against it: a fund with a defined holding period and return target is **structurally impatient**, whatever it says at the negotiating table.
