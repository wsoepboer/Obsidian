# Stewardship

**Acting as a guardian of something valuable and ensuring that it remains healthy for future generations.**

The framing question of [[S6 - Remaining Successful for Future Generations]]: imagine receiving a family business from previous generations. How would you view your responsibility — **as the owner, or as a temporary caretaker?**

| Owner mindset | Caretaker mindset |
|---|---|
| The business is mine to use | The business is mine to **hand on** |
| Returns accrue to me | Returns must also fund the next generation |
| A ten-year investment competes with my own horizon | A ten-year investment **is** the horizon |

Stewardship is also one of the five [[Family Values]] the course names — so it appears twice: as a value families claim, and as a mindset the final session asks them to adopt deliberately.

## Why it fits family firms
The perspective aligns naturally with [[Long-Term Orientation]] and [[Patient Capital]], and it is the reason [[Sustainability in Family Business|sustainability]] lands more easily in a family firm than in a listed one.
