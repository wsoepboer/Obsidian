# Sustainability in Family Business

In this course sustainability is **not limited to environmental issues**. It includes:

- **Social responsibility**
- **Employee wellbeing**
- **Ethical behaviour**
- **Community impact**
- **Long-term value creation**

## Why family firms adopt it
Not as compliance or PR. Many family businesses adopt sustainability initiatives **because they align closely with their long-term orientation and family values** — see [[Long-Term Orientation]], [[Patient Capital]], [[Family Values]], [[Stewardship]].

> [!tip] A usable argument for an advice report
> Sustainability is **easier to sell to a family firm than to a listed one**, because the time horizons already match. A listed company must justify a long-payback sustainability investment against quarterly expectations; a family owner thinking in generations does not have that problem. Lead with the alignment, not with the obligation.
