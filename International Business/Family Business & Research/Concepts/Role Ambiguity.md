# Role Ambiguity

People struggling to **separate family roles from business roles**. Introduced as *role confusion* among the challenges in [[S1 - What Makes Family Businesses Unique]], and treated at length in [[S5 - Professionalisation and Leadership Development]] as the most common source of confusion in family businesses.

One family member may simultaneously be:

- A **shareholder**
- A **board member**
- A **manager**
- A **son or daughter**

Each role carries different responsibilities and expectations. The trouble starts when they mix. The three questions that expose it:

- Should a promotion be based on **family status or job performance**?
- Should a **shareholder** influence **operational** decisions?
- Should **family disagreements** be discussed in **management meetings**?

## The remedy
Separate the roles explicitly — [[Ownership Governance and Management]]. Clear role definitions improve decision-making, reduce conflict and increase accountability, and matter more as the organisation grows more complex.
