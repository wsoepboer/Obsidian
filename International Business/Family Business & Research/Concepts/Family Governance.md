# Family Governance

The half of [[Governance]] that focuses on **the family itself**. The reason the distinction from [[Corporate Governance]] matters:

> Many family business challenges do not originate from business operations. Instead, they arise from **misunderstandings, differing expectations or unresolved family issues**.

As families grow across generations, communication becomes more complex: new members enter the system, ownership fragments, views on the future diverge. Family governance creates **structured opportunities for dialogue**.

## The instruments
- **[[Family Council]]** — the forum
- **[[Family Charter]]** — the document
- **[[Shareholder Agreement]]** — the legal counterpart to the charter's moral agreement

## The purpose
Family governance **keeps the family aligned**. When family members share a common understanding of their values, responsibilities and expectations, they are better equipped to decide together and navigate future challenges.

> [!important] Not bureaucracy
> Family governance is not about creating bureaucracy. It is about **creating clarity and preserving relationships** that support the continuity of the family enterprise.

It is also the machinery that makes [[Constructive vs Destructive Conflict|constructive conflict]] possible — a safe place to raise difficult topics before they escalate.
