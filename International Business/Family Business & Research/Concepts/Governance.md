# Governance

The **structures, processes and agreements** that help people make decisions and work together effectively. In family businesses, governance provides a framework that **balances family interests with business interests**.

Brightspace's metaphor: governance provides the **"rules of the game"** that allow a family business to function effectively across multiple generations.

| Without governance | With governance |
|---|---|
| Decisions become unclear | **Transparency** |
| Conflicts escalate | **Accountability** |
| Continuity is threatened | **Trust** |

## Why it becomes necessary
Most family businesses start **informally**, and informal agreements stop working as the firm grows. Three things happen at once:

- More family members become involved
- **Ownership becomes fragmented**
- Decision-making becomes more complex

So governance is not a bureaucratic add-on — it is **the answer to growth**. See [[Generational Stages]] for why the need rises with each generation.

## The three questions governance answers
- Who has the authority to make decisions?
- How are disagreements resolved?
- Who determines the future direction of the company?
