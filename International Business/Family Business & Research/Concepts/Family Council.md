# Family Council

A **formal forum** where family members discuss topics such as **ownership, family values, succession and long-term ambitions**.

The defining feature: these discussions take place **outside the daily management of the business**. That separation is the whole point — it gives the family somewhere to argue about the future that is not a management meeting, which is exactly the failure mode [[Role Ambiguity]] describes.

One of the two governance instruments the course names, with the [[Family Charter]]. The council is the **forum**; the charter is the **document** it produces.

## What it is for
- Structured dialogue as the family grows and fragments → [[Generational Stages]]
- A safe space where difficult topics can be raised **before they escalate** → [[Constructive vs Destructive Conflict]]
- Keeping the family aligned on values, responsibilities and expectations
