# Family Conflict

Personal disagreements influencing business decisions — one of the five challenges of family ownership in [[S1 - What Makes Family Businesses Unique]].

The course does **not** treat conflict as something to eliminate. [[S4 - How Family Relationships Influence Success]] splits it in two: see [[Constructive vs Destructive Conflict]]. Conflict is a natural part of working together; the goal is conditions in which disagreement can be addressed respectfully and productively.

## Where it comes from
- **Unspoken expectations** — when they remain unspoken, assumptions fill the gap → [[Communication in Family Businesses]]
- **[[Family Dynamics]]** — loyalty that prevents honest feedback, sibling rivalry carried into the firm, conflict avoidance that lets small problems grow
- **[[Family Logic vs Business Logic]]** — fairness against competence, most sharply during [[Succession]]
- **Fragmenting ownership** — more owners, more divergent preferences → [[Generational Stages]]

## What contains it
Structures, not goodwill: family meetings, a [[Family Council]], and governance agreements provide safe spaces where difficult topics can be raised **before they escalate**. A [[Family Charter]] sets out conflict-resolution procedures in advance.
