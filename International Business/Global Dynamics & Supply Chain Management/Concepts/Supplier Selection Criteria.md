# Supplier Selection Criteria

Pillar 2 names the criteria and then names the tension: *"cost, quality and reliability considerations."*

Three criteria that cannot be maximised together. The cheapest supplier is rarely the most reliable; the most reliable is rarely the cheapest; and quality that must be inspected on arrival is quality you are paying for twice. Selection is therefore a **weighting decision**, and a defensible weighting is one derived from what the company competes on.

For international expansion the list extends: capacity to scale, ability to serve the target region, certification and compliance status → [[Quality Standards and Certification]], sustainability and labour practice → [[Ethical Sourcing]], financial stability, and whether the supplier can be audited at all.

In a report, state the criteria and their weights **before** naming a preferred supplier. The rubric rewards the reasoning, not the conclusion.
