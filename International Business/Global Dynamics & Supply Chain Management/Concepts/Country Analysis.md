# Country Analysis

**Part 2 of the assessed learning outcome**, and half the grade. The rubric's wording:

> the student conducts a country analysis in which geopolitical and macro-economic factors are translated into strategic implications for organisations

Two inputs, one output. **Geopolitical** — stability, government and policy direction, alliances and trade relations, sanctions and export controls, rule of law, corruption. **Macro-economic** — growth, inflation, currency, labour market and wage levels, interest rates, infrastructure, sector structure. The output is not a profile: it is **[[Strategic Implications]]** for a specific organisation.

The assignment ties it to the chain: *"at least one country within the supply chain network should be examined in greater depth."* So the country you choose should be one where the chain is actually exposed — a critical supplier's location, a chokepoint, the target market.

> Each part of the learning outcome must reach a 6 independently, and Brightspace publishes no Global Dynamics teaching material. **Ask early.**
