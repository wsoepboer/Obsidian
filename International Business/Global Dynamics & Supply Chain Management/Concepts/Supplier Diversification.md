# Supplier Diversification

Pillar 3's answer to dependency: spread the supply so that one failure is not the company's failure.

The mechanics are simple — a second qualified supplier, ideally in a different country and not sharing the same upstream tier or the same shipping route. The cost is equally simple: less volume leverage on price, more qualification and audit work, more complexity to manage.

Diversification only reduces risk if the alternatives are genuinely independent. Two suppliers drawing on the same scarce raw material, the same port, or the same politically exposed region are one supplier with extra paperwork → [[Raw Material Scarcity]].

For the assignment this is one of the most common defensible recommendations — which is why the rubric's *Good* level matters: state what it costs, whether the company can bear that cost, and why you chose it over holding more [[Safety Stock]] instead.
