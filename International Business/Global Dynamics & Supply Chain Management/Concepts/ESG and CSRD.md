# ESG and CSRD

Pillar 7's fifth bullet, and the only place the lecture names a specific regulatory regime — *"ESG and CSRD requirements."*

- **ESG** — environmental, social and governance: the framework through which investors, customers and regulators assess non-financial performance.
- **CSRD** — the EU **Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive**: sustainability reporting obligations for in-scope companies, on assured, comparable, standardised information covering the **value chain**, not just the entity.

The supply chain consequence is the important part. Reporting on the value chain means **collecting data from suppliers**, which requires traceability, audit rights and systems that most chains do not yet have → [[Product Traceability]], [[Blockchain and Traceability]]. And the obligation cascades: a company outside scope that supplies one inside scope will be asked for the data anyway.

For the rubric this is what *compliant* means in *"viable, feasible and compliant"* → [[Viable Feasible Compliant]]. Check current scope and timetable before citing specifics — this is fast-moving legislation.
