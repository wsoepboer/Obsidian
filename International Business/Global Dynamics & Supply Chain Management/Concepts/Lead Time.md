# Lead Time

Pillar 10 KPI, and arguably the most load-bearing number in the whole framework: the elapsed time from placing an order to receiving the goods.

It shows up in nearly every other pillar. It is set by sourcing distance ([[Local vs Global Sourcing]]) and mode ([[Transportation Modes]]), inflated by borders ([[Cross-Border Logistics]]), and it drives how much [[Safety Stock]] is required and whether [[JIT vs Just-in-Case|JIT]] is viable at all.

The point most reports miss: **variability matters more than length**. A reliable 40-day lead time can be planned around; a 20-day lead time that is sometimes 45 cannot, and the difference has to be covered by stock or paid for in lost service. So report a mean *and* a spread, and treat reducing variability as a legitimate recommendation in its own right — often a cheaper one than reducing the mean.
