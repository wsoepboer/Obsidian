# Transportation Modes

Pillar 4's first bullet: **road, rail, sea, air.** Mode choice is the single biggest lever on the speed–cost–carbon triangle, and it is a tactical decision with daily operational consequences.

| Mode | Speed | Cost | Carbon | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Air** | Fastest | Highest | Highest per tonne-km | Urgent, high-value, low-weight |
| **Road** | Fast, flexible | Medium | Medium | Regional, door-to-door, last leg |
| **Rail** | Moderate | Lower | Low | Long overland, bulk, containers |
| **Sea** | Slowest | Lowest | Lowest per tonne-km | Intercontinental volume |

Two things follow. First, mode and inventory are the same decision seen twice: slow modes buy cheap freight with capital tied up in stock → [[Safety Stock]]. Second, chains are usually **multimodal**, so the handover points — ports, terminals, customs — are where delay and cost actually accumulate.
