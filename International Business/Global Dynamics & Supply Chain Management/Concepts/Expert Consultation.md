# Expert Consultation

A graded requirement, not a research tip. The learning outcome demands recommendations formulated *"in consultation and collaboration with experts from various disciplines"*, and the assignment is specific: *"interviews with senior experts in supply chain management."*

**Various disciplines** is the operative phrase. A supply chain redesign touches logistics, procurement, finance, law and compliance, IT, HR and sustainability — so a report built on three interviews with logistics managers has met the letter and missed the criterion.

The rubric levels again separate incorporating from weighing:

- **Pass** — *"relevant insights from experts across various disciplines have been incorporated"*
- **Good** — insights *"consciously weighed and integrated"*, with clarity on **why** the chosen recommendation is most appropriate

Which means experts who disagree are more useful than experts who confirm: the disagreement is what you weigh.

Practically — start now, because access is the bottleneck and the GO meeting on 11 September already asks which experts could substantiate your recommendations. Record every interview in the [[Personal Logbook]]: who, when, what changed as a result.
