# Climate and Disaster Risk

Pillar 6's fifth bullet: *"natural disasters and climate risks."* Two different time horizons filed together, and separating them makes the analysis better.

- **Acute** — floods, storms, earthquakes, drought, fire. Sudden, local, and disruptive out of proportion to their footprint because chains concentrate on a few nodes and corridors. Low water on a river or a blocked canal reroutes an entire region's freight.
- **Chronic** — the slow shifts: water scarcity where crops or processes depend on it, heat affecting labour and yields, rising sea levels at port and warehouse sites, and the **regulatory** climate response, which changes costs predictably rather than randomly.

Acute risk is managed with buffers, alternatives and visibility. Chronic risk is managed by *where you put things* — siting, sourcing regions, network design — which means it belongs in a redesign recommendation, not a risk appendix.
