# Supply Chain Structure

**Pillar 1 of the ten.** The as-is picture: who is in the chain, in what order, and how the goods and money actually move. The lecture lists it as *"overview of the current supply chain"* plus *"key suppliers, manufacturers, distributors and customers"*.

> **Key question: Is the current supply chain suitable for change?**

That question is doing real work. Structure is not described for its own sake — it is described to establish whether the chain *can absorb* the redesign you are about to recommend. A chain with one supplier, one route and no slack is structurally hostile to change, and saying so is already part of the analysis.

Where it lands in the assignment: *"select a company and map its supply chain network, or a specific part of it."* Note the permission to take **part** of it — a well-bounded slice mapped properly beats the whole network mapped vaguely.
