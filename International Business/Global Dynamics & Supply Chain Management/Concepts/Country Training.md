# Country Training

The **delivery format** required for part 2 of the learning outcome: the [[Country Analysis]] is *"presented professionally in the form of a country training."*

A training is not a presentation of findings — it is designed so that an audience can **do something afterwards**. That framing shapes everything: who is being trained (a management team? colleagues about to operate there?), what they must be able to do at the end, and which of your findings are decision-relevant rather than merely interesting.

The rubric's two levels make the standard explicit:

- **Pass** — the training is *aligned with the analysis conducted*
- **Good** — the analysis and training *demonstrably strengthen the substantiation* of the proposed organisational change

So a country training that stands apart from the supply chain advice scores Pass at best. It has to make the redesign more convincing.
