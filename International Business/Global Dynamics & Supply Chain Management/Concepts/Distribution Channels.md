# Distribution Channels

Pillar 4's third bullet: how the product reaches the end customer once it exists — direct, via wholesalers, via retailers, via agents or distributors, or online.

Each channel is a bundle: reach and local knowledge come with margin loss and distance from the customer; direct and online keep margin and data but require the company to carry service, returns and last-mile cost itself. Multi-channel chains have to keep the promise consistent across all of them.

The channel decision is also a **data** decision. Selling through an intermediary means the demand signal arrives filtered and late, which degrades [[Demand Forecasting]] and worsens the very stock problems the company is trying to fix.

This is also the pillar where this course meets [[00 Global Sales and Accountmanagement MOC|Global Sales and Accountmanagement]], whose rubric asks for advice on market-entry mode, sales-team composition and online strategy — the commercial face of the same choice.
