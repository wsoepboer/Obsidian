# Demand Forecasting

Pillar 5's second bullet. Every inventory, capacity and sourcing decision runs on a forecast, so forecast error propagates into all of them — which is the practical reason forecasting sits in the *inventory* pillar rather than in marketing.

Inputs: historical sales, seasonality, promotions and price changes, customer intelligence, and market or macro indicators. Methods run from simple time-series extrapolation to the [[AI and Predictive Analytics]] the deck names in pillar 8.

Three cautions worth stating in a report. A forecast is a distribution, not a number — the useful output is a range with an error estimate. Forecast quality degrades with distance from the demand signal, so long channels and intermediaries make it worse → [[Distribution Channels]]. And a **new market has no history**, so entry decisions rest on analogy and judgement, and should be planned to be revised.
