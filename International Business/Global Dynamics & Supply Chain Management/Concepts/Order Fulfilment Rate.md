# Order Fulfilment Rate

Pillar 10 KPI. The proportion of demand that can be met from available stock — the fill rate.

It is the direct output of the inventory policy: raise [[Safety Stock]] and it rises, cut stock and it falls. That makes it the honest partner to [[Inventory Turnover]], and the pair is how you tell a genuine efficiency gain from a service cut wearing its clothes. Turnover up with fulfilment held is an improvement; turnover up with fulfilment down is a transfer of cost to the customer.

Two refinements: measured by **line, order or value**, the same chain scores differently — an order missing one cheap component fails on order-fill but barely dents value-fill. And an aggregate figure hides the distribution, so a healthy overall rate can conceal chronic shortfalls on a few critical items.

It differs from [[On-Time-In-Full]] in what it blames: fulfilment rate is about *having* the goods, OTIF about *delivering* them as promised.
