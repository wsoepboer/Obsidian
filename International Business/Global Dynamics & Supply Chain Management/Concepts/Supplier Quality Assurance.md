# Supplier Quality Assurance

Pillar 9's second bullet: making sure incoming goods meet specification, and preferably making sure they were **made** to specification.

There is a hierarchy here, and moving up it is a recognisable recommendation:

1. **Inspect on arrival** — catches defects, pays for them twice, and cannot catch what inspection does not see.
2. **Statistical control at the supplier** — the supplier measures its own process and shares the data.
3. **Qualify the process, not the batch** — approve the supplier's method, then monitor by exception.
4. **Joint improvement** — work on the root cause together, which requires a relationship rather than a purchase order → [[Supplier Relationships and Contracts]].

Distance pushes companies down this hierarchy and cost pushes them up it: rejecting a container that has already crossed an ocean is far more expensive than preventing the defect, and the delay lands on [[On-Time-In-Full]] and customer service.
