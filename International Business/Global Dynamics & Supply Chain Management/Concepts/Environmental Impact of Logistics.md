# Environmental Impact of Logistics

Pillar 4 closes with *environmental impact* — put inside the logistics pillar rather than left to the sustainability pillar, which is the right place for it: transport is where a large share of a chain's emissions is actually created.

The levers are concrete and mostly familiar: mode shift from air and road to rail and sea, higher load factors and fewer empty runs, network redesign so goods travel less, fuel and fleet choices, and reduced packaging. Most of them trade speed for emissions, which is why this cannot be decided separately from [[Transportation Modes]] and [[Lead Time]].

It is measurable, and the deck gives the measure: **carbon emissions per shipment** is one of the pillar-10 KPIs → [[Carbon Emissions per Shipment]]. That is what lets a sustainability recommendation be monitored rather than asserted.
