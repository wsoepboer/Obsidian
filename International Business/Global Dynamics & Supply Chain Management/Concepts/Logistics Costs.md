# Logistics Costs

Pillar 10 KPI. The total cost of moving and holding goods — freight, warehousing, handling, customs and duties, packaging, insurance, and the capital tied up in inventory.

Usually expressed as a percentage of revenue or a cost per unit or shipment, so it can be compared over time and across routes. The comparison is the useful part: an absolute figure says little, while a rising cost-to-serve on one channel or one market says exactly where to look.

The mistake to avoid is optimising a component. Cheaper freight bought with slower transit raises inventory and worsens service; a closed regional warehouse cuts fixed cost and raises delivery cost and lead time. Only the **total** moves in a meaningful direction, which is why this KPI belongs beside [[On-Time-In-Full]] and [[Inventory Turnover]] rather than on its own.

For the assignment's *"more cost-efficient"* option, this is the number the recommendation has to move — and the one a reader will check against the service KPIs.
