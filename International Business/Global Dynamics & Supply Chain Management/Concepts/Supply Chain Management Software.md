# Supply Chain Management Software

Pillar 8's second bullet, listed separately from [[ERP Systems]] — and the separation is the point. ERP runs the transactions inside the company; SCM software plans and coordinates **across** companies.

Typical functions: demand and supply planning, distribution and transport planning, warehouse management, supplier collaboration portals, and network visibility across tiers. Where ERP asks *what do we have and what did we commit to*, SCM software asks *what should we do next, given the whole network*.

Two practical notes for a recommendation. Best-of-breed planning tools give better decision support than ERP planning modules but add an integration burden — data has to flow reliably in both directions. And the software's output is only as good as its inputs: a planning system fed by a distorted demand signal produces confident nonsense → [[Demand Forecasting]].
