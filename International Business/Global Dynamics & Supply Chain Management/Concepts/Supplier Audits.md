# Supplier Audits

Pillar 3 pairs *supplier audits* with *quality control* — measurement tells you the output; the audit tells you whether the process behind it is sound.

An audit inspects what a KPI cannot see: the production process, quality systems and certifications, working conditions and labour practice, environmental controls, sub-tier suppliers, and records. It is the mechanism behind almost every sustainability claim a company makes about its chain, and behind the due-diligence expectations of [[ESG and CSRD]].

Distance is the complication. The further and deeper the chain, the more expensive auditing becomes and the more companies substitute certificates for inspection — which is exactly where claims fail. Practical forms scale: second-party audits, accredited third-party audits, and increasingly data-based verification via [[Blockchain and Traceability]].

Right of audit is a **contract** term. If it is not written in, it does not exist.
