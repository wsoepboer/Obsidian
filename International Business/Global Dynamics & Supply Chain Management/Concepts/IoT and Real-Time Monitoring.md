# IoT and Real-Time Monitoring

Pillar 8's fifth bullet. Connected sensors on goods, vehicles, containers and equipment reporting location and condition continuously.

What it buys: **visibility**, which is the scarce commodity in a long chain. Live position and ETA instead of a status guess, condition monitoring for temperature- or shock-sensitive goods, cold-chain evidence, equipment telemetry for predictive maintenance, and early warning that a shipment is going wrong → [[Supply Disruption]].

Two consequences worth drawing out. Visibility narrows the uncertainty that [[Safety Stock]] exists to cover — better information can substitute for inventory, and that trade is a defensible recommendation. And the value is realised only if the data reaches a **decision**: alerts with no owner and no response procedure change nothing, which puts this back in the *people and processes* column.
