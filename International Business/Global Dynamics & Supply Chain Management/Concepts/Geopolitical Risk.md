# Geopolitical Risk

**Pillar 6's first bullet**, and the concept that joins the two halves of this unit — it is a supply chain risk *and* a [[Country Analysis]] finding.

It covers state action and instability that changes the economics or legality of a chain: sanctions and export controls, conflict and closed routes, nationalisation or ownership restrictions, sudden regulatory change, and the use of trade as leverage. Its distinguishing feature is that it is **not diversifiable within a country** — no supplier choice inside a sanctioned or blockaded state helps.

Concentration is what makes it bite: chains that meet at one region, one strait or one dominant producer are exposed however many suppliers appear on the map → [[Supplier Diversification]], [[Raw Material Scarcity]].

> **Pillar key question: What supply chain risks arise in the target country?**

That question is, word for word, the assignment's instruction to examine at least one country in depth.
