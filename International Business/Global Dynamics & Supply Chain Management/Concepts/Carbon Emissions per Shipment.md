# Carbon Emissions per Shipment

Pillar 10's last named KPI, and a telling inclusion: emissions sit in the same list as cost and service, which makes sustainability a **performance measure** in this course rather than a policy statement.

Per shipment (or per tonne-kilometre, or per unit delivered) it is comparable across routes, modes and periods, so it can be improved deliberately: mode shift, higher load factors, fewer and shorter movements, network redesign → [[Environmental Impact of Logistics]].

Its second function is evidentiary. Value-chain sustainability reporting needs numbers with a method behind them, not estimates → [[ESG and CSRD]], so the measurement itself is often the first recommendation.

Use it as the counterweight when the improvement direction is speed or cost: an air-freight fix that meets a service target and doubles emissions has traded one graded criterion for another, and the rubric's word *responsible* means you have to say so.
