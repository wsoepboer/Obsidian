# Supply Chain KPIs

**Pillar 10**, and the pillar that makes the other nine auditable. The deck lists the typical KPIs:

| KPI | Measures | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **On-Time-In-Full (OTIF)** | Delivery reliability | [[On-Time-In-Full]] |
| **Lead time** | Speed and its variability | [[Lead Time]] |
| **Inventory turnover** | Capital efficiency in stock | [[Inventory Turnover]] |
| **Order fulfilment rate** | Ability to serve from stock | [[Order Fulfilment Rate]] |
| **Logistics costs** | Cost to move and hold | [[Logistics Costs]] |
| **Supplier performance** | Upstream reliability | [[Supplier Performance Measurement]] |
| **Carbon emissions per shipment** | Environmental impact | [[Carbon Emissions per Shipment]] |

> **Key question: How will the organisation monitor supply chain performance internationally?**

Two rules for using them. **Never read one alone** — cost down with OTIF down is not an improvement, and turnover up with fulfilment down means the chain has been cut too thin. And **attach a KPI to every recommendation you make**: the learning outcome includes *monitoring* the implementation, so an unmeasurable recommendation is an incomplete one.
