# Supply Disruption

Pillar 6's second bullet: an interruption in the flow of goods, from a single late shipment to a chain-wide stoppage.

Causes are the rest of the pillar — geopolitics, disaster, a failed supplier, a closed route, a cyber incident. What matters analytically is not the cause but the **shape** of the exposure: how likely, how long, and how much it costs per day. That framing is what turns a risk list into a prioritised one.

Two properties decide severity. **Concentration** — one supplier, one plant, one route, one port. And **thinness** — no buffer stock, no qualified alternative, no visibility far enough upstream to see it coming.

The responses are all elsewhere in the ten pillars, which is the useful insight: [[Supplier Diversification]], [[Safety Stock]], [[JIT vs Just-in-Case]] positioning, and [[IoT and Real-Time Monitoring]] for early warning. Resilience is not a separate programme — it is a set of deliberate choices in the other pillars.
