# Supplier Performance Measurement

Pillar 3's first bullet, and also one of pillar 10's KPIs — the deck lists *supplier performance* among the typical supply chain KPIs, which is a hint that the two pillars close a loop.

Measuring a supplier means agreeing, in advance, what "good" is: delivery reliability ([[On-Time-In-Full]]), lead-time variability, defect and rejection rates, responsiveness on exceptions, invoice accuracy, and increasingly emissions and compliance evidence.

Two properties separate a real system from a scorecard nobody reads: the measures are **agreed with the supplier** and tied to the contract, and they are **acted on** — reviewed on a cycle, with consequences ranging from improvement plans to re-tendering.

> **Pillar key question: Are there supply risks that could hinder international operations?**

You cannot answer that question about a supplier you do not measure.
