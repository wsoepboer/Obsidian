# Carbon Footprint

Pillar 7's third bullet: the greenhouse gas emissions attributable to the chain.

The distinction that matters is scope. Emissions from a company's own operations and purchased energy are small next to those embedded in the goods and services it buys and in the transport, use and disposal of what it sells — the chain, in other words. For most companies, decarbonising means changing suppliers, materials, modes and design, not changing the lights.

Which is why this concept is measured, not asserted: the deck gives **carbon emissions per shipment** as a pillar-10 KPI → [[Carbon Emissions per Shipment]], and the operational levers sit in pillar 4 → [[Environmental Impact of Logistics]].

The honest tension for a report: lower emissions and lower cost sometimes align (fewer empty runs, less packaging, shorter distances) and sometimes do not (sea over air trades emissions for [[Lead Time]] and [[Safety Stock]]). Say which case you are in.
