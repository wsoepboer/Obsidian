# Implementation - People Processes Information

The third paragraph of the learning outcome, and the part students most often omit: after the recommendation, *"design, plan, coordinate and/or monitor the operational implementation process of these changes, **in terms of people, processes and information**, making effective use of current digital and technological tools."*

Three dimensions, and each has to be addressed:

- **People** — who does what differently; whose approval is needed (the rubric names *decision makers*); what skills, training and roles change; who resists and why.
- **Processes** — the actual sequence of work that changes, at [[Tactical and Operational Level|tactical and operational]] level; the new steps, controls and hand-offs.
- **Information** — what data has to flow, to whom, when, and through which system → [[ERP Systems]], [[Supply Chain Management Software]].

> [!important] Pass vs Good is the word "interconnected"
> **Pass:** the three are *"given due consideration"*. **Good:** the activities are *"integrated into a coherent whole"* and people, processes, information and the digital tools are *"clearly interconnected"*.
>
> So do not write three sections. Show how the process change requires the data, which requires the system, which requires the training, which requires the decision-maker's approval — one chain of dependency, with a sequence and an owner. Add a KPI to monitor it → [[Supply Chain KPIs]].
