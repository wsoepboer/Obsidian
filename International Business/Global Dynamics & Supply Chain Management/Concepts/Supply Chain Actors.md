# Supply Chain Actors

The four roles pillar 1 names: **key suppliers · manufacturers · distributors · customers**. The chain is the sequence, and each role has its own logic and its own failure mode.

| Role | What it controls | Where it goes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **Suppliers** | Inputs, cost base, ethical exposure | Dependency, scarcity, poor practice upstream |
| **Manufacturers** | Capacity, quality, flexibility | Bottlenecks, defects, rigid changeovers |
| **Distributors** | Reach, speed, cost to serve | Long lead times, poor visibility |
| **Customers** | Demand signal, service expectation | Distorted or misread demand |

Two refinements worth carrying: the word **key** is a filter — not every supplier belongs in the analysis — and real chains are **tiered**, so the supplier of your supplier can be the actual constraint.
