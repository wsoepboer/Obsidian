## The assignment
Given verbatim on the deck and repeated in the `Extra explanation` hand-out. Full brief: [[The Supply Chain Assignment]]. In summary:

- Working as a team, **select a company and map its supply chain network**, or part of it
- Investigate how it could become **more sustainable, faster, more effective or more cost-efficient**
- Address **both tactical and operational levels** → [[Tactical and Operational Level]]
- **Clearly identify the problem** you intend to solve
- Substantiate with **academic literature, relevant models, interviews with senior supply chain management experts** and the latest insights
- Develop **different scenarios** and evaluate the recommendations **from multiple perspectives**
- Propose **how the change could be implemented** — people, processes, information → [[Implementation - People Processes Information]]
- Incorporate the knowledge from **Global Dynamics** → [[Country Analysis]]
