## What happens in the slot
From the hand-out: *"a short presentation, followed by an individual component conducted by two assessors."* The group presents; you are then questioned individually. Both parts of the learning outcome — the redesign **and** the country training — are in scope, and each must reach a 6 on its own.

Report due **Fri 16 Oct, 12:00** — that leaves as little as **one weekend** before the first assessment slot. Prepare the defence before the report goes in, not after.
