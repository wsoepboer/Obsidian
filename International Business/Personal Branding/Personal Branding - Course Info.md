## Course description
*Verbatim from Brightspace:*

> During this course, you will build a digital personal branding portfolio. You will present your brand whilst using storytelling techniques in order to captivate your audience and make an impact on them.

## Learning outcome
*Verbatim from Brightspace:*

> You develop a creative, visually appealing and compelling personal brand, in which you showcase your development, values, strengths, goals and ambitions as an independent as well as interdependent International Business professional. You reflect back on your actions, evaluate major choices you have made, pitch yourself, your skills and your knowledge, and present your plans for future development relating to self- and relationship management effectively to an audience. In addition, you connect your story to a larger societal debate and present your point of view on this topic convincingly.

Unpacked, that is **five** deliverables, not one:

1. A personal brand that is creative and *visually appealing* — the artefact is judged on how it looks, not only on content.
2. Showcased **development, values, strengths, goals and ambitions**, as both an *independent* and an *interdependent* professional.
3. **Reflection** on your actions and evaluation of major choices already made.
4. A **pitch** of yourself delivered to an audience — self- and relationship management.
5. A connection to a **larger societal debate**, with your own point of view argued convincingly.

Point 5 is the one most easily missed: the portfolio is not purely inward-looking.

## Assessment
**Type:** Portfolio Assessment — a professional, authentic personal branding portfolio presenting yourself as an in(ter)dependent professional, plus a presentation of your personal story.

The portfolio must contain:

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Personal branding story** | Origin story · major obstacle(s) · solutions · future outlook |
| **Your WHY** | Sinek's Golden Circle — introduced in Workshop 1 |
| **T-shaped profile** | Breadth across disciplines plus one deep specialism |
| **Process reflection** | First, second and final draft of the story; how you uncovered your WHY; **how you used AI for this assignment** |
| **Professional presentation** | *"The portfolio should look professional"* — stated as a requirement in its own right |

- **Rubric:** Brightspace → *Assessment* page (detailed grading criteria) — **not yet extracted into the vault**
- **Hand-in:** Brightspace → *Assessment* > *Assignments*
- The three story drafts are **assessed artefacts**, not working files — keep every version.

> [!important] The AI declaration is graded
> Process reflection explicitly asks *how did you use AI for this assignment?* Programme-wide, the GenAI declaration is a mandatory/conditional rubric criterion — miss it and the grade is capped at a near pass (5). See [[AI Policy - IB Programme]].
