## Self-test
- What does each of *action*, *colour* and *emotion* add to a story, and what is lost when one is missing?
- Take one flat sentence from your first draft and rewrite it three times — once for action, once for colour, once for emotion.
- What changed between your draft 1 and draft 2, and can you explain *why* it changed? Process reflection will ask.
- Does the emotion in your story serve the point you are making, or replace it?
