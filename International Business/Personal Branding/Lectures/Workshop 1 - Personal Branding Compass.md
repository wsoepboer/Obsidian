## Why this session matters
It sets up two of the five things the portfolio must contain: your **WHY** (the Golden Circle) and your **T-shaped profile**. Both are named explicitly in the assessment brief, so treat the models introduced here as examinable rather than as warm-up.

The *independent as well as interdependent* framing comes straight from the learning outcome — the portfolio has to show you as both, not just as a capable individual.

## Self-test
- What are the three rings of the Golden Circle, and why does Sinek argue you should start from the inside out?
- What does the vertical bar of a T-shaped profile represent, and what does the horizontal bar represent? Which is yours?
- What is the difference between an *independent* and an *interdependent* professional skill? Give one example of each from your own experience.
- How would you articulate your WHY in one sentence — without describing what you do?
