## Why this session matters
The **first draft** produced here is an assessed artefact. Process reflection in the portfolio requires the first, second and final drafts of the story, so whatever you write in this session must be kept, not overwritten.

The story itself has a prescribed shape, given in the assessment brief: **origin story → major obstacle(s) → solutions → future outlook**. Draft against that structure from the start.
