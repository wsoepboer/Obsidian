## Why this session matters
The last workshop before hand-in, and the only scheduled point at which you get feedback on the story in front of an audience. What you learn here feeds the **final draft** — the third of the three assessed versions — and the process reflection that explains how the story changed.

Come with a story that is finished enough to be criticised. A session spent drafting is a session of feedback wasted.
