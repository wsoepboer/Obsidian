## Why this session matters
The learning outcome requires you to *present your plans... effectively to an audience* and to argue a point of view *convincingly*. Delivery is therefore graded, not just the written portfolio — this session is where the story stops being a document and becomes a performance.

## Self-test
- What delivery techniques were given for holding an audience, and which one is weakest for you?
- How long is your story when spoken aloud, and where does it sag?
- How do you open and close so that the WHY, not the biography, is what the audience remembers?
- What will you do with your hands, your pauses and your eye contact — deliberately, rather than by default?
