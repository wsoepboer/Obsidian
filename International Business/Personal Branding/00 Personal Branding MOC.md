Sketch of the territory this course covers; each becomes a linked concept note once material arrives.

- **Foundations:** personal brand · brand identity vs brand image · authenticity · reputation · positioning

- **Direction:** personal vision & mission · personal development plan (PDP) · SMART goals · career anchors

- **Expression:** storytelling · story structure · action, colour and emotion · elevator pitch · visual identity · tone of voice · CV & letter of motivation
- **Channels:** digital portfolio · LinkedIn profile · digital footprint · networking · offline presence
