## Mandatory literature
**None.** Brightspace states *N.A.* — there is no prescribed book or reader for this unit.

> [!warning] No mandatory literature is not the same as no theory
> The portfolio still requires named models — the Golden Circle and the T-shaped profile — and the learning outcome requires you to argue a position in a **societal debate**, which needs sources. With nothing prescribed, sourcing is on you. The two books below are the only steer given.

## Non-mandatory literature
- Szuplat, T. (2024). *Say it well: Find your voice, speak your mind, inspire any audience.* Ebury Edge.
- Wong, H. (2023). *Telling your story, building your brand: A personal and professional playbook.* Business Expert Press.

Szuplat maps onto [[Workshop 4 - Presentation Skills]]; Wong onto [[Workshop 2 - Storytelling 1]] and [[Workshop 3 - Storytelling 2]].
