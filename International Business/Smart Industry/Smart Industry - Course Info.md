## Learning outcome
Analyse the **macro, meso and internal environment** of an international organisation, formulate **strategic advice**, and assess its impact using **desk and field research** and critical thinking.

## His stated assessment expectation
> "If I mention a theory, you are able to explain it back to me and apply it for yours."

Define the concept **and** locate your own case within it. Definition alone does not score.

> "A well-argued plan doesn't consist of concepts — it consists of arguments."

The rubric backs this: "sufficient" already requires theories and models **applied**, and "excellent" requires them **justified** with international sources.

## Industry/company choice — his advice
- **Be original.** Last year produced a glut of **e-commerce and retail** cases; he says they converge on the same solutions.
- Broaden the scope: **shipping, agriculture, transport, health, B2B**.
- Pick something you would genuinely want to work in.
- Work **industry → company** rather than the reverse, though either is allowed.
- **No nationality or country constraint** on the company. Confirmed.
