# Smart Industry Glossary

Key terms for the course. [[Smart Industry - Course Info|↩ Course]]

## Waves and adoption
- **[[Kondratieff Waves]]** — long economic cycles of 40-60 years, each opened by a cluster of general-purpose technologies.
- **MBNRIC** — med-bio-nano-robo-info-cognitive technologies; Grinin's driver of the sixth K-wave in the 2030s-2040s.
- **Cybernetic Revolution** — Grinin's production revolution starting in the 1950s, ending in a phase of self-regulating systems.
- **[[Technology Adoption]]** — the move from a technology being available to being routinely used; invention is not adoption.
- **[[Diffusion of Innovation]]** — Rogers' S-curve, adopter categories, and the five constructs that drive adoption.
- **[[Gartner Hype Cycle]]** — perceived value against maturity: trigger, peak, trough, slope, plateau.
- **[[Fourth Industrial Revolution]]** — the current fusion of physical, digital and biological systems.

## Trends
- **[[Trend]]** — a directional change already underway but not yet fully adopted.
- **Hype** — fast rise on buzz, short duration, no lasting change.
- **[[Trend Classification]]** — sorting a phenomenon into hype, micro trend, trend, megatrend or adopted norm.
- **[[DESTEP Analysis|DESTEP]]** — demographic, economic, socio-cultural, technological, ecological, political scan of the macro environment. Conclude per letter, never sum up.

## Industry and organisation
- **[[GICS]]** — MSCI/S&P four-level classification: 11 sectors down to 163 sub-industries.
- **[[Industry Life Cycle]]** — introduction, growth, maturity, decline, each with a different competitive and HR logic.
- **[[Porter's Five Forces]]** — new entrants, supplier power, buyer power, substitutes, rivalry. Rivalry is driven by the other four.
- **[[Industry Technology and Competitiveness Scan]]** — the course worksheet scoring the five forces 1-5 out of 25.
- **[[STP Framework]]** — segmentation, targeting, positioning.
- **[[Digital Innovation Strategy]]** — Nylén & Holmström: user experience, value proposition, digital evolution scanning, skills, improvisation.
- **Merciless consumer** — the digitalised shopper: price-transparent, in control, expecting extreme ease, less loyal.

## People
- **[[Human Resource Function]]** — how an organisation acquires, develops, deploys and releases people; the course's outcome variable.
- **[[Employment Cycle]]** — establishment (IN), maintenance (THROUGH), termination (OUT).
- **Job rotation / enlargement / enrichment** — more variety, more tasks, more control respectively.
- **[[Machine Augmentation of Work]]** — technology raising a worker's output rather than replacing the worker; task-level, not job-level.
- **[[Human Resource Management]]** — the broader discipline (cross-course).

## Consequences and craft
- **[[Eco-Design]]** — minimising environmental impact across the whole product life cycle by design.
- **[[Cognitive Sustainability]]** — treating human attention and mental capacity as a finite resource that design can deplete.
- **[[Pyramid Principle]]** — situation, complication, question, answer; then arguments, then data.
- **[[Double Diamond]]** — discover, define, develop, deliver.
