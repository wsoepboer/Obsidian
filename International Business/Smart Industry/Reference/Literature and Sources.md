## What each one gives you
- **Second Machine Age** — the framing sentence: digital technology does for **mental power** what the steam engine did for muscle power. Three claims: rapid progress, profound benefit, tough challenges (inequality).
- **Grinin & Grinin** — the sixth K-wave and MBNRIC technologies → [[Kondratieff Waves]].
- **PwC "Sizing the prize"** — the AI-and-GDP numbers, and the automated/assisted/augmented/autonomous ladder. Marked **must use**.
- **WEF Future of Jobs 2025** — the job creation and destruction figures, the skills lists, the reskilling gap. The richest single source for quantitative claims about work.
- **WEF Leveraging GenAI (2024)** — augmentation scenarios, early-adopter cases, a framework for action → [[Machine Augmentation of Work]].
- **Nylén & Holmström** — the digital innovation diagnostic → [[Digital Innovation Strategy]].
- **The Human Resource Function** — the employment cycle chapter → [[Employment Cycle]].
