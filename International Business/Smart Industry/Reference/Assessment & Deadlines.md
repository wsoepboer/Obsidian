## Format
Teams of **max 3** (trios) within your own class. Two assessments, **each worth half the grade**, both presented and defended individually.

1. **Analysis** — industry, technology and organisation scan for your chosen company.
2. **Advice** — how the organisation adapts to be future proof: business rationale, impact on the HR department, brief implementation plan.

Joint grade, but **both parts must be sufficient** to pass.

## What "good" looks like — criterion 1 (Analysis)
Sufficient already requires: trend analysis covering **the past ~50 years**, current and future state, **on a timeline**, with emphasis on technologies adopted; **quantitative data**; evidence that several developments were explored before one trend was selected, including effects on consumers and society; an organisation analysis including **HRM aspects and a consumer analysis**; theories and models applied with sources.

To reach **good**, add: how HRM **within the industry** changed over time and is reasoned forward, lots of quantitative data, models justified in detail, international sources.

**Excellent** adds: technologies adopted **within and beyond** the industry, an explicit link between HRM change and technology adoption, critical justification with international sources for why *this* trend is most influential, a clear HRM description of the organisation, and an argument that already points to where the advice will land.

## What "good" looks like — criterion 2 (Advice)
Sufficient: a relevant trend the company must adjust to, practical advice at **strategic, tactical and operational** level, with time, budget and feasibility considered; strategic HR advice covering elements of the employment cycle (**IN, THROUGH, OUT**) and linked to the analysis.

Good: well-grounded advice, the **full** employment cycle, explicit strategic implications tied to long-term objectives.

Excellent: **all processes** in all three phases, concrete and directly applicable, plus a justified **implementation plan** with international sources, and a critical reasoning of the impact at strategic, tactical and operational level.

## Presentation and defense criteria
- **Presentation.** Slides with **too much text** are explicitly marked down. Aim for keywords, visualised models, sources woven into the storyline, eye contact, engagement. Excellent uses advanced visual technique (animation, transitions, imagery).
- **Defense.** Specific, well-structured answers with references to sources; professional posture; excellent means nuanced critical thinking that goes beyond the assignment scope.
