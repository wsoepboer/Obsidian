# AI Policy

[[Smart Industry - Course Info|↩ Course]] · [Brightspace module](https://leren.windesheim.nl/d2l/le/lessons/203043/lessons/1698482)

## Allowed
AI as a **tool**: sparking ideas, creating visuals, designing slides, finding information, checking grammar and spelling. Treat it as a brainstorming partner and always double-check quality and accuracy.

## Not allowed
- **AI as a source.** Every argument must be supported by reliable, traceable authors, theories and data.
- Over-reliance on AI for creative ideas. The lecturer's warning: if many teams do this the advice converges, becomes unoriginal and less valuable to the client — which can cost you marks.

## Mandatory
If you use GenAI you must complete the **AI statement** from paragraph 7.5 of the Windesheim AI Policy **for every tool used**, and attach the statement(s) to your work.
