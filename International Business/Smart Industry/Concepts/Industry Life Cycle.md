# Industry Life Cycle

Stages an industry passes through, each with a different competitive logic and a different HR profile:

| Stage | Demand | Competition | Typical HR focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Tiny, uncertain | Few players | Scarce specialists, high autonomy |
| Growth | Rising fast | Entrants pour in | Volume hiring, [[Recruitment and Selection]] |
| Maturity | Flat, replacement | Consolidation, price pressure | Efficiency, retention, reskilling |
| Decline | Falling | Exit and niching | Restructuring, [[Offboarding & Outplacement]] |

Pair it with [[Porter's Five Forces]]: the same force reads differently depending on the stage.
