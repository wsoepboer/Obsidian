# Trend

A directional change in behaviour, technology or market structure that is **underway but not yet fully adopted**. The core analytical object of this course: you identify the trends acting on your industry, pick the single most impactful one, and build your conclusion on it.

> [!important] The lecturer's rule
> **AI is not a trend.** It is too unspecific and its applications are already omnipresent. In a forecast trend analysis, focus on phenomena **not yet adopted by the majority**. Once almost everyone has adopted something, it is no longer a trend.

Levels to keep apart when you write: **macro** (society, [[DESTEP Analysis|DESTEP]]), **meso** (your industry), **micro** (your organisation).
