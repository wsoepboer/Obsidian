# Technology Adoption

The process by which a technology moves from first availability to routine use inside a firm or market. Adoption is **not** invention: the gap between the two is where competitive advantage and HR disruption actually live.

Two levels the course keeps separate:
- **Market adoption** — how fast users take it up ([[Diffusion of Innovation]]).
- **Organisational adoption** — whether the firm restructures work, roles and skills to use it ([[Human Resource Function]]).

The historical lesson from week 1: firms that were **not agile enough** to integrate steam, telecoms or computing into their processes did not survive the wave.
