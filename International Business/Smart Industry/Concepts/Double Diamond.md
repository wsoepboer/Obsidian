# Double Diamond

A four-step design process of alternating divergence and convergence: **Discover → Define → Develop → Deliver**.

The lecturer offers it as an **optional but encouraged** structure for the trend work:
- **Discover** — broad trend exploration.
- **Define** — selecting and sharpening one key trend.
- **Develop** — exploring implications and opportunities.
- **Deliver** — translating the trend into strategic direction.

It maps neatly onto the two assessments: Discover and Define are part 1, Develop and Deliver are part 2.
