# Eco-Design

Designing a product, service or workplace so that its environmental impact is minimised **across the whole life cycle** — materials, production, distribution, use, end of life — rather than cleaned up afterwards. Principles: durability, repairability, recyclability, material and energy reduction, design for disassembly.

The course's twist: eco-design is examined not only for its environmental effect but for its link to **[[Cognitive Sustainability]] and employee well-being**. Answer that link before week 5.
