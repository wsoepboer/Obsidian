# Employment Cycle

The employee's journey through the organisation, in **three phases**. The course text is *The Human Resource Function* (Chapter 5, "The human resource management function — the employment cycle"), in `Files/`.

| Phase | Rubric shorthand | Processes in the text |
|---|---|---|
| **Establishment** | **IN** | Human resource planning related to business strategy · job analysis and job design · recruitment methods and selection processes · employment arrangements and remuneration |
| **Maintenance** | **THROUGH** | Induction · training and development · recognition and reward · performance management |
| **Termination** | **OUT** | Termination management, including entitlement and transition issues |

Job design options covered: **job rotation**, **job enlargement** (more things to do within the same job), **job enrichment** (more control and independence over how the work is done).

## Why it matters for the grade
Part 2 of the assessment is graded on strategic HR advice **across IN, THROUGH and OUT**. "Excellent" requires **all processes in all three phases**, made concrete and directly applicable, with an implementation plan. Technology hits each phase differently — that difference is the substance of the advice.
