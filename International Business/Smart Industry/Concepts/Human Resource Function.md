# Human Resource Function

The set of activities through which an organisation acquires, develops, deploys and releases people. In this course it is the **outcome variable**: technology and trends are the input, what happens to the HR function is the answer. The lecturer's recurring prompt over the history slides is *what does this mean for management, thinking HR?*

## Two levels, and the distinction is graded
- **Meso — the INDUSTRY.** How has the HR function in your industry changed over the **last 50 years** because of trends, and how will it change over the **next 3-5 years**?
- **Micro — the ORGANISATION.** The same question for your company, plus a description of its current people strategy and HR department.

"Excellent" on criterion 1 requires an explicit link between **HRM change and technology adoption** in the industry.
