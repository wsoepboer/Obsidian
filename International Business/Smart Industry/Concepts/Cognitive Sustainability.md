# Cognitive Sustainability

Treating **human attention and mental capacity as a finite resource** that design decisions can deplete or protect. Where environmental sustainability asks whether a system exhausts materials, cognitive sustainability asks whether it exhausts people: notification load, context switching, always-on availability, interfaces that externalise complexity onto the user.

The week 5 question to answer in advance: **how is eco-design related to cognitive sustainability and employee well-being?** The bridge is that both are life-cycle thinking applied to a scarce resource, and that badly designed digital tools raise cognitive load exactly as badly designed products raise material waste.
