# Industry Technology and Competitiveness Scan

The course worksheet that turns [[Porter's Five Forces]] into a scored assessment of how competitive **and** how technologically advanced an industry is. **Industry level, not company level.** → [[Industry_Tech_Competitiveness_Scan_Worksheet|source text]]

Porter's five forces as Boris draws them:

![[destep-21.jpg]]

## Steps
1. **Identify the industry** — name and brief description.
2. **Current use of technology** — common technologies, platforms, automation, AI, digital tools.
3. **Five forces with a technology and innovation lens** — for each of threat of new entrants, supplier power, buyer power, threat of substitutes, rivalry: an explanation plus a **score of 1-5**.
4. **Total score out of 25**:

| Total | Reading |
|---|---|
| 0-10 | Low technology intensity, slow innovation |
| 11-17 | Medium technology intensity, developing innovation |
| 18-25 | High technology intensity, highly innovative industry |

5. **Short conclusion** — how competitive is the industry, how advanced technologically, biggest technology opportunities, biggest risks or challenges.

![[destep-27.jpg]]

Boris' rule from the DESTEP session applies here: **conclude per force, do not sum up**, and remember rivalry is the force the other four drive.
