# STP Framework

**Segmentation → Targeting → Positioning.** The standard route from a heterogeneous market to a defensible position.

1. **Segmentation** — split the market on meaningful criteria (geographic, demographic, psychographic, behavioural). A segment must be measurable, substantial, accessible, differentiable and actionable.
2. **Targeting** — choose which segments to serve, judged on size, growth, structural attractiveness and fit with company resources.
3. **Positioning** — decide the place your offer occupies in the target's mind relative to alternatives, and express it as a value proposition.

In this course it is the **micro-level** counterpart to the industry analysis: it tells you who the organisation actually serves before you advise it on technology and HRM.
