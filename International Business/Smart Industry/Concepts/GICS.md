# GICS

The **Global Industry Classification Standard**, developed by MSCI and S&P. A four-level hierarchy used to classify listed companies: **11 sectors → 25 industry groups → 74 industries → 163 sub-industries**. Each company gets one sub-industry, assigned by its principal business activity (usually the largest source of revenue).

Used in week 2 to fix **which industry you are actually analysing** before you run [[Porter's Five Forces]] or the [[Industry Life Cycle]] on it. Getting this wrong invalidates everything downstream — "tech" is not an industry.
