# COGNITIVE SUSTAINABILITY

> Raw text extracted from the Brightspace file on 2026-08-31. Layout is approximate; the original is in `Files/`.

COGNITIVE SUSTAINABILITY
Prepare a short and concise slide deck consisting only of pictures in which you answer the questions below in a creative way. 
What is cognitive sustainability for you? 
Discuss in what way technologies made us more cognitively sustainable? Possibly refer to the table you created on trends and technologies. Focus on the past, present and the future. 
Which very concrete effects does impairment of cognition have on our current and future generations?
How can we ensure cognitive sustainability for the current generation, your children and our children’s children? 
Which skills and abilities maybe obsolete in the future?As an example: some people may say that we will not need to teach our children to write. In what way do you agree/disagree with this?
