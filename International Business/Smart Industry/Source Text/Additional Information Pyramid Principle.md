THE PYRAMID PRINCIPLE
LOGIC IN WRITING AND
THINKING
• Written by Barbara Minto in 1987
• She was working as a consultant at McKinsey at that time
• Consultants are often hired by companies to investigate and
analyse (strategic) problems and to make recommendations as
neutral outsiders.
• Consultants frequently have to persuade others to change,
they do this through well structured and logical arguments that,
together, tell a convincing story
• Minto’s objective was that her colleagues improved their
(written) communication and used more persuasive arguments
• The Pyramid Principle is a method to utilize lots of information
and structure that in a story, message or presentations without
losing the details or the power of the message
