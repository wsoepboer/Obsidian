```
EFFECTS OF TRENDS AND TECHNOLOGIES ON ENVIRONMENT AND PEOPLE
Make a list of trends and accompanied technologies adopted. Come up with three past trends, three current trends and three future trends. 
TIME
TECHNOLOGY
TREND
MAGNITUDE
PREVALENCE
EFFECTS
PEOPLE AFFECTED
SOURCES
Notes on columns.Time refers to when the trend was, is or will happening. So here you should put past, present, future. Technology refers to any kind of technology that is related to the trend. The trend should not be a hype or a fad. The magnitude can be described as severe, mediocre, small or negligible. Prevalence maybe related to a particular continent, country, region or village but may also be worldwide.People affected refers to the different layers of people affected by the trend or technology in which way. Try to also describe HOW it is affecting different layers of our population. You may for example have different effects of a trend on people with high vs low economic status or based on gender, age and other attributes. Lastly, in the column sources provide links to the sources used. 
!!!! Be as specific as possible and use critical thinking when presenting your table. !!!!
```
