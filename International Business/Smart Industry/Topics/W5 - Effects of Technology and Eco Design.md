## Assignment 1 — Cognitive sustainability
> Prepare a short, concise slide deck **consisting only of pictures**, answering creatively:
> 1. What is cognitive sustainability **for you**?
> 2. In what way have technologies made us more cognitively sustainable? Refer to the table you created on trends and technologies. Cover **past, present and future**.
> 3. Which very concrete effects does impairment of cognition have on current and future generations?
> 4. How can we ensure cognitive sustainability for this generation, your children, and their children?
> 5. Which skills and abilities may be obsolete in the future? Example: some say we will not need to teach children to write. Agree or disagree, and why?

## Assignment 2 — Effects of trends and technologies table
> List trends and the technologies adopted with them: **three past, three current, three future**. Columns:
>
> `TIME | TECHNOLOGY | TREND | MAGNITUDE | PREVALENCE | EFFECTS | PEOPLE AFFECTED | SOURCES`
>
> - **Time** — past, present or future.
> - **Technology** — any technology related to the trend.
> - **Trend** — must not be a hype or a fad → [[Trend Classification]].
> - **Magnitude** — severe, mediocre, small or negligible.
> - **Prevalence** — continent, country, region, village, or worldwide.
> - **People affected** — which layers of the population, and **how**. Differentiate by economic status, gender, age and other attributes.
> - **Sources** — links to what you used.
>
> **Be as specific as possible and use critical thinking when presenting the table.**
