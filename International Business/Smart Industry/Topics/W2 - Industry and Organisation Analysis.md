## Week 2 deliverable — organisation analysis
1. **Basic profile** — name, sector, size, markets.
2. **Mission, vision, strategic direction**, aims and objectives.
3. **Segmentation, target groups, positioning** → [[STP Framework]].
4. **Value proposition and main customer benefits** — Business Model Canvas, value proposition canvas.
5. **Technology & innovation readiness scan** — use of digital tools and platforms, openness to innovation, recent innovations (product, service, process), technological maturity (basic / developing / advanced).
6. **Current state of the people strategy and HR department**, using the model from class → [[Human Resource Function]].
7. **Conclusion with 3 strengths and 3 weaknesses.**

- Any model is a **simplified version of reality**, and the six DESTEP letters are **always intertwined**.
- **Do not sum up findings per letter. Conclude per letter**, relate it to your organisation, and use it to find **opportunities and threats** in the macro environment.
- Same for Porter: **rate every force low / medium / high** and conclude per force. **Rivalry is the most important force** and is driven by the other four.
- Whether a factor is a threat or an opportunity depends on the business and industry. **Every threat and opportunity needs research and reasoning behind it.**
- The session ends in **SWOT** — internal vs external environment — and next-meeting topics: Ansoff matrix, confrontation matrix, strategic options.

## Self-test
- Classify your company through all four GICS levels. Which sector, industry group, industry, sub-industry?
- Run the five forces and *conclude* per force, low/medium/high, with reasoning. Then say what that does to rivalry.
- Which life-cycle stage is your industry in, and what data says so?
- Name the three phases of the employment cycle and one process in each. Map them onto IN / THROUGH / OUT.
- Berlin, India, Silicon Valley: one opportunity and one threat each, for *your* company specifically.
