# Study Tips

Strategies for effective language learning using this vault.

---

## Study Path (Beginner)

### Week 1: Foundation
**Goal**: Master particles and formal speech

1. Read [[00-Home/Quick Reference|Quick Reference]] for overview
2. Study [[20-Grammar/Particles/Topic & Subject (은는 이가)|topic/subject particles]]
3. Learn [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Formal Polite|formal polite ending (-습니다)]]
4. Do [[40-Review/Conjugation Drills|conjugation drills]] (10 min/day)

### Week 2: Verb Mastery
**Goal**: Understand verb conjugation patterns

1. Study [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite|informal polite (-어요/-아요)]]
2. Learn irregular patterns: [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite#Exception 2: ㅂ Irregular|ㅂ irregular]], [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite#Exception 3: 하다 Verbs|하다 verbs]]
3. Practice drills (15 min/day)
4. Start [[40-Review/Practice Questions|practice questions]]

### Week 3: Expansion
**Goal**: Build vocabulary and sentence-making

1. Pick vocabulary categories ([[30-Vocabulary/People & Family|People]], [[30-Vocabulary/Food & Animals|Food]], [[30-Vocabulary/Actions & Verbs|Verbs]])
2. Learn [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/좋다 vs 좋아하다|좋다 vs. 좋아하다]]
3. Study [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/Negation|negation patterns]]
4. Review [[40-Review/Common Mistakes|common mistakes]]

## Daily Routine (30 min)

- **5 min**: Review [[00-Home/Quick Reference|quick reference]]
- **10 min**: [[40-Review/Conjugation Drills|Conjugation drills]] (rotate topics)
- **10 min**: Read grammar, watch examples
- **5 min**: [[40-Review/Practice Questions|Do practice questions]] or make sample sentences

## Active Learning Techniques

### 1. **Spaced Repetition**
- Review topics from 1 week ago, 3 weeks ago, etc.
- Obsidian search: find all files mentioning "particle" or "conjugation"

### 2. **Write Sample Sentences**
- After learning grammar, create 5 sentences using it
- Use [[30-Vocabulary/📌 Vocabulary Index|vocabulary]] you've learned
- Compare with examples in the notes

### 3. **Teach-Back Method**
- Explain a grammar point out loud without looking at notes
- Check [[20-Grammar/📌 Grammar Index|grammar section]] for what you missed

### 4. **Minimize Context Switching**
- Spend 20–30 min on one topic per session, not jumping around
- Read the entire particle file before moving to the next

### 5. **Link Concepts**
- When studying particles, look at [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/📌 Verb Endings Overview|verb endings]] too
- Click internal links to explore connections
- Use [[00-Home/Quick Reference|quick reference table]] to see how concepts relate

## Integration with Other Tools

### With Flashcard Apps (Anki, Quizlet)
- Create cards from [[30-Vocabulary/📌 Vocabulary Index|vocabulary lists]]
- Add grammar rules as cloze deletions
- Example: "Verb + ___\___ = past tense" (answer: 었어요)

### With Language Learning Apps (Duolingo, etc.)
- **Before**: Read the grammar here to understand WHY
- **During**: Practice sentences in the app
- **After**: Return here to cement understanding

### With Native Speakers / Tutors
- Bring specific confusions from [[40-Review/Common Mistakes|Common Mistakes]]
- Ask for more examples of grammar patterns
- Show sentences you've written for feedback

## Progress Tracking

### Self-Assessment Questions

- [ ] Can I identify particles in a sentence?
- [ ] Can I conjugate regular verbs in both formal and informal speech?
- [ ] Can I recognize and apply irregular verb patterns?
- [ ] Can I form a basic S-O-V sentence?
- [ ] Can I express negation correctly?
- [ ] Can I distinguish 좋다 vs. 좋아하다?

**If all checked**: Move to intermediate materials

## Mindset Tips

1. **Embrace mistakes** — [[40-Review/Common Mistakes|Common Mistakes]] shows you're learning normal pitfalls
2. **Consistency beats intensity** — 10 min/day > 2 hours once a week
3. **Link new to old** — Always connect new grammar to something you know
4. **Use the examples** — Every grammar file has real sentences; learn with them
5. **Celebrate small wins** — Mastering one particle is real progress
