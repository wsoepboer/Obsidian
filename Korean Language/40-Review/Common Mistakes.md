# Common Mistakes to Avoid

Pitfalls and how to prevent them.

---

## Particle Confusion

### ❌ Mistake: Using wrong subject/object particle

- ❌ 사과을 좋습니다. (wrong)
- ✓ 사과가 좋습니다. (correct)

**Why**: 좋다 (is good) takes the subject particle 이/가, not 을/를.

**Rule**: Subject particles (이/가) mark what "is good"; object particles (을/를) mark what you're eating.

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/Topic & Subject (은는 이가)|Topic & Subject]], [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/좋다 vs 좋아하다|좋다 vs. 좋아하다]]

---

### ❌ Mistake: Confusing 좋다 vs. 좋아하다

- ❌ 나는 음악이 좋아합니다.
- ✓ 나는 음악을 좋아합니다. (I like music.)
- ✓ 나는 음악이 좋습니다. (Music is good to me — both work!)

**Why**: 좋아하다 requires an object (음악 + 을/를). 좋다 takes a subject (음악 + 이/가).

See: [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/좋다 vs 좋아하다|Full explanation]]

## Verb Conjugation Errors

### ❌ Mistake: Forgetting irregular verbs

- ❌ 덥어요. (wrong)
- ✓ 더워요. (correct — ㅂ irregular)

**Rule**: ㅂ irregular verbs change ㅂ → 우 before -어요.

Examples:
- 덥다 → 더워요 (hot)
- 춥다 → 추워요 (cold)
- 많다 → 많아요 (many — actually regular)

See: [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite|Irregular conjugation guide]]

---

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong vowel harmony

- ❌ 좋어요. (wrong vowel harmony)
- ✓ 좋아요. (correct — ㅗ vowel uses -아요)

**Rule**: Check the vowel in the stem:
- ㅏ, ㅗ → -아요
- Other → -어요

See: [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite|Vowel harmony rules]]

---

### ❌ Mistake: Not conjugating 하다 properly

- ❌ 공부하어요. (wrong)
- ✓ 공부해요. (correct)

**Rule**: 하다 becomes 해요, not 하어요 or 하아요.

Examples:
- 공부하다 → 공부해요
- 좋아하다 → 좋아해요
- 일하다 → 일해요

See: [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite|하다 exception]]

## Negation Mix-ups

### ❌ Mistake: Using wrong negation type

- ❌ 나는 고기가 못 먹어요. (wrong)
- ✓ 나는 고기를 못 먹어요. (I can't eat meat — ability issue)
- ✓ 나는 고기를 안 먹어요. (I don't eat meat — choice)

**Why**: 못 = can't (inability); 안 = don't (choice)

See: [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/Negation|Negation rules]]

---

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong structure for "be not"

- ❌ 저가 선생님 아니에요. (wrong structure)
- ✓ 저는 선생님이 아니에요. (I'm not a teacher.)

**Why**: 이/가 아니에요 takes 이/가, not 은/는 for the subject.

## Sentence Structure Issues

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong word order

- ❌ 좋아해요 사과를 나는. (completely wrong)
- ✓ 나는 사과를 좋아해요. (I like apples.)

**Rule**: S + O + V (Subject + Object + Verb)

---

### ❌ Mistake: Forgetting particles altogether

- ❌ 나 사과 먹어요.
- ✓ 나는 사과를 먹어요.

**Why**: Particles clarify the sentence structure. Always include them.

---

## Formality Level Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong formality in context

- ❌ To a teacher: 괜찮아요. (too casual)
- ✓ To a teacher: 괜찮습니다. (formal)

**Rule**: Use -습니다 with elders/authority; -어요 with friends.

---

## Quick Prevention Checklist

- [ ] Did I use the right particle (은/는 vs. 이/가 vs. 을/를)?
- [ ] Did I check for irregular verbs (ㅂ irregular, 하다)?
- [ ] Did I apply vowel harmony (-아요 vs. -어요)?
- [ ] Did I choose the right negation (못 vs. 안)?
- [ ] Did I follow S-O-V word order?
- [ ] Did I match the formality level?
