# Particle Overview

Particles (조사) mark grammatical relationships in Korean. They attach to nouns to show their role in the sentence.

---

## Essential Particles

| Particle | Rule | Use | Example | Lesson |
|----------|------|-----|---------|--------|
| **은/는** | 받침 O→은, X→는 | Topic/subject | 사과**는** 과일이에요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-06-18 - Particles & Formal Speech\|18 Jun]] |
| **이/가** | 받침 O→이, X→가 | Subject | 사과**가** 좋아요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-06-18 - Particles & Formal Speech\|18 Jun]] |
| **을/를** | 받침 O→을, X→를 | Object | 사과**를** 먹어요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-06-18 - Particles & Formal Speech\|18 Jun]] |
| **도** | Noun + 도 | Also/too | 저**도** 좋아해요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-06-25 - More Particles\|25 Jun]] |
| **과/하고** | 받침 O→과, X→와 | And/with | 형**과** 공부해요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-06-25 - More Particles\|25 Jun]] |
| **의** | Noun + 의 | Possession | 선생님**의** 개 | [[10-Lessons/2026-06-25 - More Particles\|25 Jun]] |
| **에게/한테** | After noun | To (person) | 엄마**에게** 전화해요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-07-19 - Numbers & Giving\|19 Jul]] |
| **만** | After noun (replaces other particles) | Only/exclusivity | 동생**만** 학생이에요 | [[10-Lessons/2026-08-02 - Indirect Objects & Exclusivity\|2 Aug]] |

## Place & Person — which "to"?

| Target | Static (be / go) | Action happens there / from |
|--------|------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Place** | 에 — 학교**에** 가요 | 에서 — 학교**에서** 공부해요 |
| **Person** | 에게 / 한테 — 친구**에게** 줘요 | 에게서 / 한테서 — 친구**한테서** 받아요 |

For 에 vs. 에서, the **verb** decides: 있다/없다/가다/오다 → 에; anything you *do* → 에서.

---

## No-받침-change particles

These never change shape, whatever the noun ends in: **에게, 한테, 만, 에, 에서, 도, 의**.
Only 은/는, 이/가, 을/를 and 과/와 flip on 받침.

---

## Quick Tips

- **받침** = final consonant in a Korean syllable
- **Subject vs. Topic**: 이/가 points out specific subjects; 은/는 introduces general topics
- **Particles combine**: e.g., 저는 (I, topic) + 친구와 (with friend) = 저는 친구와
- **만 replaces** 은/는, 이/가, 을/를 — but **stacks** after 에게, 에, 에서 (에게만, 집에서만)
