# Compound Particle: 과/하고

Connects **multiple nouns** with "and" or "with."

---

## Two Options

### 과/와
**Rule**: 받침 O → 과 | 받침 X → 와

**More formal**

**Examples**:
- 선생님**과** 학생 (teacher and student)
- 아버지**와** 어머니 (father and mother)

### 하고
**Less formal, more conversational**

**Examples**:
- 선생님**하고** 학생 (teacher and student)
- 친구**하고** 놀아요 (play with a friend)

---

## Patterns

### "And" (Multiple things)
- 도서관**과** 식당이 있어요.
  (There's a library and a restaurant.)

### "With" (Doing something together)
- 저는 형**과** 공부합니다.
  (I study with my older brother.)
- 친구**하고** 영화를 봐요.
  (I watch a movie with a friend.)

---

## Quick Table

| 받침? | 과/와 | 하고 |
|-------|------|------|
| O | 친구**와** 만나요 | 친구**하고** 만나요 |
| X | 형**과** 놀아요 | 형**하고** 놀아요 |
