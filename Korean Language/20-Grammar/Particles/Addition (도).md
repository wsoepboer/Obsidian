# Addition Particle: 도

Marks something **added** or **similar** to what was already mentioned.

---

## Pattern

**Noun + 도**

No 받침 rule — just attach directly.

---

## Meaning

"Also," "too," "as well." Indicates that something is similar or in addition to another.

---

## Examples

**Person also does it**:
- 저**도** 좋아해요.
  (I like it too.)

**Multiple things**:
- 한국 노래를 좋아합니다. 한국 음식**도** 좋아합니다.
  (I like Korean songs. I also like Korean food.)

**Variety**:
- 밥**도** 먹고, 국**도** 마셔요.
  (I eat rice and also drink soup.)

---

## Practice

**Fill in 도**:

- 빵\_\_\_ 좋아해요.
  - Answer: 빵**도** 좋아해요 (I also like bread.)
