# Possession Particle: 의

Shows **ownership** or **relationship** — the Korean equivalent of "'s" in English.

---

## Pattern

**Noun + 의 + Noun**

Meaning: The first noun **possesses** or **relates to** the second noun.

---

## Examples

**Ownership**:
- 선생님**의** 가방 (teacher's bag)
- 아버지**의** 책 (father's book)

**Description**:
- 선생님**의** 개 (teacher's dog)
- 한국**의** 음식 (Korean food / food of Korea)

**Relationships**:
- 나타샤 씨**의** 오빠 (Natasha's older brother)

---

## Full Sentences

- 선생님**의** 개가 귀엽습니다.
  (The teacher's dog is cute.)

- 나타샤 씨**의** 오빠는 회사원입니까?
  (Is Natasha's older brother an office worker?)

---

## Practice

**Question**: 누구**의** 가방입니까?  
(Whose bag is it?)

**Answer**: 선생님**의** 가방입니다.  
(It's the teacher's bag.)
