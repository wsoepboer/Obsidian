# Exclusivity Particle: 만

Marks **"only / just / alone"** — attached after a noun to exclude everything else.

---

## Rule

Same form regardless of 받침 — 만 never changes shape.

| Base | 받침 | + 만 |
|------|------|------|
| 동생 | O | 동생**만** |
| 연필 | O | 연필**만** |
| 드라마 | X | 드라마**만** |
| 한국어 | X | 한국어**만** |

만 **replaces** 은/는, 이/가 and 을/를 — it takes their slot, and those particles don't stack on top of it.

- 리사 씨는 노래**를** 좋아해요. → 리사 씨는 노래**만** 좋아해요.
  (Lisa likes songs. → Lisa likes **only** songs.)
- ❌ 노래**만을** 좋아해요 / ❌ 동생**만이** 학생이에요 *(grammatical in formal writing, but not what you want at this level)*

---

## Pattern

**Noun + 만 + Verb**

---

## Key Examples

- 저**만** 영어를 해요.
  (Only I speak English.)
- 저는 여동생**만** 있어요.
  (I only have a younger sister.)
- 저는 한국어**만** 공부해요.
  (I only study Korean.)
- 동생**만** 책을 읽어요.
  (Only my younger sibling reads books. / My younger sibling alone reads books.)
- 저와 언니는 회사원이에요. 동생**만** 학생이에요.
  (My older sister and I are office workers. Only my younger sibling is a student.)
- 나타샤 씨는 친구에게 편지**만** 보내요.
  (Natasha sends only a letter to her friend — no gift.)

---

## Where 만 goes changes the meaning

만 attaches to whatever you're limiting, so moving it moves the emphasis:

| Sentence | What's exclusive |
|----------|------------------|
| 저**만** 동생에게 책을 줘요. | **I** and nobody else give it |
| 저는 동생**에게만** 책을 줘요. | I give it to **my sibling** and nobody else |
| 저는 동생에게 책**만** 줘요. | I give **a book** and nothing else |

Note row 2: after 에게/한테 and 에/에서, 만 **stacks** (에게만, 에서만, 집에만) — it only *replaces* 은/는, 이/가, 을/를.

- 저는 집**에서만** 공부해요. (I study only at home.)
- 주말**에만** 쉬어요. (I rest only on weekends.)

---

## 만 vs. 는/은 (topic)

Both attach after a noun, but 는/은 introduces a topic while 만 excludes everything else.

| | Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---------|---------|
| **는/은** | Noun + 는/은 | As for X... (topic) |
| **만** | Noun + 만 | Only X (nothing else) |

**Example pair**:
- 저**는** 영어를 좋아해요. (As for me, I like English.)
- 저**만** 영어를 좋아해요. (Only I like English — everyone else doesn't.)

---

## 만 vs. 도 (addition)

These are exact opposites, and they occupy the same slot:

- 저**도** 학생이에요. (I'm a student **too** — others are as well.)
- 저**만** 학생이에요. (**Only** I'm a student — nobody else is.)

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/Addition (도)|Addition Particle 도]]

## Practice

**Fill in with 만**:

1. 쪽영 씨는 누나가 있어요. 쪽영 씨는 동생이 없어요.
   → 쪽영 씨**만** 누나가 있어요. (Only Jjogyeong has an older sister.)
2. 나타샤 씨는 친구에게 편지를 보내요. (선물은 없어요.)
   → 나타샤 씨는 친구에게 편지**만** 보내요. (Natasha sends only a letter, no gift.)
3. 저는 영어, 한국어, 일본어를 공부해요. → (Korean only)
   → 저는 한국어**만** 공부해요.
4. 저는 집, 도서관, 학교에서 공부해요. → (at home only)
   → 저는 집**에서만** 공부해요.
