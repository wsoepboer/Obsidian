# Directional Particles: 에게 / 한테

Marks the **target** of an action — the person to whom something is given, said, done, etc.

---

## Two Options

### 에게
**More formal**

### 한테
**More casual, conversational**

Both are correct; choose based on context.

---

## Pattern

**Noun + 에게/한테 + Action**

---

## Key Examples

### Giving/Sending
- 아버지**한테** 선물을 줘요.
  (I give a gift to my father.)
- 할머니**에게** 카드를 보내요.
  (I send a card to my grandmother.)

### Calling/Writing
- 엄마**가** 저**한테** 전화를 해요.
  (Mom calls me.)
- 친구**한테** 편지를 써요.
  (I write a letter to my friend.)

### Expressing to someone
- 선생님**에게** 질문을 해요.
  (I ask a question to my teacher.)

---

## With a direct object (에게/한테 + 을/를)

A sentence can carry **both** an indirect object (에게/한테) and a direct object (을/를) at once.

**Pattern**: Noun(target) + 에게/한테 + Noun(thing) + 을/를 + Verb

- 동생**에게** 책**을** 줘요.
  (I give a book to my younger sibling.)
- 나타샤 씨는 친구**에게** 편지**를** 써요.
  (Natasha writes a letter to her friend.)

---

## Quick Reference

| Action | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Give | 부모님**에게** 선물을 줘요 |
| Send | 친구**한테** 문자를 보내요 |
| Call | 누나**에게** 전화해요 |
| Write | 할머니**한테** 편지를 써요 |

---

## Practice

**Fill in 에게 or 한테**:

1. 가영 씨가 리사 씨\_\_\_ 전화를 해요.
   - Answer: 가영 씨가 리사 씨**에게/한테** 전화를 해요
2. 프엉 씨가 저\_\_\_ 선물을 줬어요.
   - Answer: 프엉 씨가 저**한테/에게** 선물을 줬어요
