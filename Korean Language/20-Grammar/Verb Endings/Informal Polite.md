# Informal Polite Ending: -어요 / -아요

The **most common** verb ending in everyday Korean conversation.

---

## Formation Steps

1. **Remove 다** from the base verb/adjective
   - 좋다 → 좋
   - 먹다 → 먹
   - 오다 → 오

2. **Check stem vowel** (the vowel in the final syllable)
   - ㅏ or ㅗ → add **-아요**
   - Other vowels → add **-어요**

3. **Apply exceptions**:
   - No 받침 + vowel combo → contract
   - ㅂ irregular: ㅂ → 우
   - 하다 verbs → 해요

---

## Vowel Harmony Rule

| Stem Vowel | Ending |
|-----------|--------|
| ㅏ, ㅗ | -아요 |
| ㅓ, ㅜ, ㅡ, ㅣ | -어요 |

---

## Examples & Irregulars

### Regular Conjugations

| Base | Remove 다 | Vowel | Rule | Result |
|------|----------|-------|------|--------|
| 먹다 | 먹 | ㅓ | ㅓ→어요 | 먹어요 |
| 좋다 | 좋 | ㅗ | ㅗ→아요 | 좋아요 |
| 자다 | 자 | ㅏ | ㅏ→아요 | 자아요 |
| 보다 | 보 | ㅗ | ㅗ→아요 | 봐요 |

### Exception 1: No 받침 + Vowel Harmony → Contract

| Base | Result | Contraction |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 오다 | 오+아요 | 와요 |
| 가르치다 | 가르쳐요 | (합쳐짐) |

### Exception 2: ㅂ Irregular

- **덥다** (hot) → 더+워요 = **더워요**
- **춥다** (cold) → 추+워요 = **추워요**
- **많다** (many) → 많+아요 = **많아요**

### Exception 3: 하다 Verbs

- **하다** → 해요 (합쳐짐)
- **좋아하다** → 좋아해요
- **공부하다** → 공부해요
- **일하다** → 일해요

---

## Real Sentences

**Statements**:
- 날씨가 좋아요. (The weather is good.)
- 먹어요. (I eat.)
- 더워요. (It's hot.)
- 공부해요. (I study.)

**Questions**:
- 좋아요? (Is it good?)
- 뭐 해요? (What are you doing?)
- 어디 가요? (Where are you going?)

---

## When to Use

- **Friends and peers**
- **Casual conversation**
- **Everyday speech** (most natural)
- **Texting or informal writing**
- **Speaking down to younger people** (without being rude)
