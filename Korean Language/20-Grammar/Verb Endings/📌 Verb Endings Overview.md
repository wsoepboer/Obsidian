# Verb Endings Overview

Verb endings (어미) show **politeness level** and whether you're **asking or stating**.

---

## Two Main Systems

| Ending | Politeness | Use | Example |
|--------|-----------|-----|---------|
| **-습니다/-습니까** | Very formal | Formal speech, news | 좋습니다 / 좋습니까? |
| **-어요/-아요** | Informal polite | Conversations, friends | 좋아요 / 좋아요? |

---

## Quick Conjugation Path

1. **Start**: 좋다, 먹다, 가다
2. **Remove 다**: 좋, 먹, 가
3. **Pick ending**: -습니다 or -어요 / -아요
4. **Apply rules**: Check 받침, vowels, irregulars

---

## Detailed Guides

- [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Formal Polite|Formal Polite]] (-습니다 / -습니까)
  - Very respectful, official tone
  - Common in formal speech, reports

- [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Informal Polite|Informal Polite]] (-어요 / -아요)
  - Friendly, conversational tone
  - Most common in everyday speech

---

## When to Use Each

### Formal (-습니다)
- Speaking to elders or authority figures
- Formal presentations
- News broadcasts
- First meetings

### Informal Polite (-어요)
- Friends and peers
- Casual conversation
- Everyday speech
- Younger people to each other

---

## Practice: Which Ending?

**Scenario 1**: Talking to your Korean teacher
→ Use **-습니다**: 안녕하십니까? 좋습니다.

**Scenario 2**: Texting a friend
→ Use **-어요**: 오늘 날씨 좋아요!
