# Questions (의문문)

Korean questions follow the same word order as statements (no inversion). Rising intonation and question words are the markers.

---

## Yes/No Questions

Use the **same structure as statements, but with rising intonation** at the end. No word order change.

| Statement | Question |
|---|---|
| 가요. (I go.) | 가요? (Do you go?) |
| 좋아요. (It's good.) | 좋아요? (Is it good?) |
| 학생이에요. (I'm a student.) | 학생이에요? (Are you a student?) |

**In writing**, you can use the formal question ending:
- Statement: 갑니다.
- Question: 갑니까? (formal question marker)

---

## Question Words (의문사)

Place the question word in its **natural position** (same position as the answer would be), with rising intonation.

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **뭐 / 무엇** | what | 뭐**해요**? (What are you doing?) |
| **누구** | who | **누가** 왔어요? (Who came?) |
| **어디** | where | **어디** 가요? (Where are you going?) |
| **언제** | when | **언제** 만나요? (When do we meet?) |
| **왜** | why | **왜** 안 왔어요? (Why didn't you come?) |
| **어떻게** | how | **어떻게** 했어요? (How did you do it?) |
| **몇** | how many | **몇** 명이에요? (How many people?) |
| **얼마** | how much | **얼마**예요? (How much is it?) |

---

## Question Word Examples

### "What?"
- 뭐 해요? (What are you doing?)
- 이게 뭐예요? (What is this?)

### "Who?"
- 누가 왔어요? (Who came?)
- 누구를 만나요? (Who do you meet?)

**Note**: 누구 = who (object), but when it's the subject, use 누가 (누구 + 이/가).

### "Where?"
- 어디 가요? (Where are you going?)
- 어디에 있어요? (Where is it? — at where)
- 어디에서 왔어요? (Where did you come from? — from where)

### "When?"
- 언제 가요? (When are you going?)
- 언제 올 거예요? (When will you come?)

### "Why?"
- 왜 안 왔어요? (Why didn't you come?)
- 왜 웃어요? (Why are you laughing?)

### "How?"
- 어떻게 해요? (How do you do it?)
- 어떻게 지내요? (How are you? — "How are you doing?")

### "How many?" / "How much?"
- 몇 명이에요? (How many people?)
- 몇 개 주세요. (Give me a few.) ← 몇 + counter
- 얼마예요? (How much is it?)
- 얼마나 좋아요? (How much/how very do you like it? — expressing degree)

---

## Question Particles

Korean also uses special question particles in formal/written style:

| Particle | Usage | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-ㅂ니까?** | formal yes/no question | 갑니까? (Do you go? — formal) |
| **-나?** | plain/informal question | 가나? (going? — casual/written) |
| **-냐?** | plain question (tougher tone) | 뭐 하냐? (What are you doing?!) |

---

## Complex Questions

### Embedded questions
Put the whole question clause before the main verb using -는지, -인지, or -냐고:

- 언제 갈 것인지 모르겠어요. (I don't know when I'll go.)
- 뭐하는지 봤어요. (I saw what he's doing.)
- 누가 했냐고 물어봤어요. (I asked who did it.)

---

## Indirect/Reported Questions

When reporting a question someone asked, convert it to reported form:

| Original | Reported (with -냐고 하다) |
|---|---|
| 뭐 해요? (What are you doing?) | 뭐 하냐고 물어봤어요. (He asked what I was doing.) |
| 갈 거예요? (Will you go?) | 갈 거냐고 했어요. (He asked if I would go.) |

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Using a statement ending in a question
- ❌ 가세요. (Go. — command/statement tone)
- ✓ 가세요? (Are you going? — question with rising intonation)

### ❌ Forgetting subject particle with "who?"
- ❌ 누구 왔어요? (grammatically incorrect)
- ✓ **누가** 왔어요? (Who came? — 누구 + 이/가 = 누가)

### ❌ Wrong position for question word
- ❌ 갈 거예요 언제? (awkward)
- ✓ 언제 갈 거예요? (When will you go?)
