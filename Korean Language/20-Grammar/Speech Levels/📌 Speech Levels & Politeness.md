# Speech Levels & Politeness (높임법)

Korean **grammar encodes social relationship directly into verb endings**. Choosing the wrong level is a grammar error, not just a tone issue.

---

## The Four Main Speech Levels

| Level | Name | Verb ending (가다) | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formal formal** | 합쇼체 | 갑니다 | Announcements, news, military, formal presentations, very official speech |
| **Polite informal** | 해요체 | 가요 | **Default polite speech** — most common in daily life, conversations with acquaintances |
| **Casual** | 해체 / 반말 | 가 | Close friends, family, younger people (intimate, can sound rude if misused) |
| **Plain/written** | 해라체 | 간다 | Writing, novels, diaries, quotes, headlines, reports |

---

## Quick Examples (가다 = "go")

- **합쇼체**: 학교에 갑니다. (I go to school. — very formal)
- **해요체**: 학교에 가요. (I go to school. — everyday polite)
- **해체**: 학교에 가. (I'm going to school. — to close friend)
- **해라체**: 학교에 간다. (He goes to school. — written/narrative)

---

## When to Use Each Level

### 합쇼체 (Formal formal)
Use with:
- **Strangers** or elderly people you don't know well
- **Official/public speech** — news broadcasts, government announcements, military
- **Formal situations** — interviews, presentations, written formal reports
- **Customer service** contexts (bank, hospital, etc.)

**Warning**: Overusing 합쇼체 in casual contexts sounds stiff and unfriendly.

### 해요체 (Polite informal) — DEFAULT
Use with:
- **Acquaintances, colleagues, classmates**
- **Most everyday conversation** — this is the most natural, versatile level
- **When in doubt** — starting with 해요체 is the safest choice
- **Online, casual writing**

**Note**: 해요체 is neither too formal nor too casual; it's appropriate for most social interactions.

### 해체 / 반말 (Casual)
Use with:
- **Close friends** (same age or younger)
- **Family members** (siblings, close relatives)
- **Younger people** (if you're older)
- **Very casual, intimate contexts**

**Warning**: Using 반말 with someone senior or unfamiliar is considered **rude and disrespectful**. Only use with peers or those explicitly close to you.

### 해라체 (Plain/written)
Use:
- **Fiction/novels** — narration and dialogue
- **Writing, reports, academic papers**
- **News headlines**
- **Quotes or reported speech**
- **Does not appear in live conversation** (except in older, formal writing)

## Subject Honorifics: -시-

To show **respect toward the grammatical subject** of the sentence (insert -시- into the verb stem), used when the subject is someone senior or honorable. **Never use for yourself.**

### How -시- works

Remove -다, insert -시- before the conjugation:

- 가다 → 가**시**다 → 가세요 / 가십니다
  - 선생님이 가세요. (The teacher is going. — respectful)
  - 선생님이 가요. (Same, but less respectful)

### Special honorific verbs (complete replacement)

Some verbs have entirely different honorific forms:

| Regular | Honorific | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 먹다 | 드시다 / 잡수시다 | eat |
| 있다 / 계신다 | **계시다** | be/stay (person) |
| 자다 | 주무시다 | sleep |
| 말하다 | 말씀하시다 | speak/say |
| 죽다 | 돌아가시다 | die (euphemistic) |
| 주다 | 드리다 | give (to someone senior) |
| 묻다 | 여쭙다 | ask (respectfully) |

**Examples**:
- 할아버지가 **계세요**. (Grandfather is here. — respectful)
- 엄마가 **드셨어요**. (Mom ate. — respectful toward mother)
- 선생님께 선물을 **드렸어요**. (I gave a gift to the teacher. — humble/respectful)

---

## Object Honorifics

Show respect toward the **recipient or object** of an action through:
- **Particles**: 께 instead of 에게 (for a person)
- **Humble verbs**: 드리다 (instead of 주다), 여쭙다 (instead of 묻다)

**Examples**:
- 선생님**께** 선물을 **드렸어요**. (I gave a gift to the teacher. — respectful)
- 할아버지**께** 여**쭙**았어요. (I asked grandfather. — respectful)

---

## Combining Speech Levels

You can mix speech levels in a sentence, but the **final verb's politeness level dominates**:

- 저는 학생**이**고 **가요**. (I'm a student and (I) go. — 가요 sets overall politeness)
- 할아버지**가 계시**고 아버지**도 가세요**. (Grandfather is here and father goes too. — 가세요 is formal)

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Using 반말 (casual) with someone older/unfamiliar
- ❌ 선생님, 어디 가? (rude — 반말 to teacher)
- ✓ 선생님, 어디 가세요? (correct — 해요체 or 합쇼체)

### ❌ Forgetting -시- when speaking about someone senior
- ❌ 할아버지가 가요.
- ✓ 할아버지가 가세요. (with -시-)

### ❌ Not using 께 with an honorific person
- ❌ 선생님**에게** 물어봤어요. (technically OK, but less respectful)
- ✓ 선생님**께** 물어봤어요. (more respectful)
