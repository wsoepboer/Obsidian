# Advanced Endings

Complex patterns for expressing nuanced meanings like appearance, causation, and change of state.

---

## -는 것 같다 ("seems/appears that")

**Pattern**: Present tense + **-는 것 같다** ("it seems that / it looks like")

Creates nominalized verb (with -는 것) that acts as the subject of 같다 (be similar/seem).

- 비가 **오는 것 같아요**. (It **seems like it's raining**.)
- 그 사람이 화난 **것 같아요**. (He **seems angry**.)
- 날씨가 좋아질 **것 같아요**. (The weather **seems like it will improve**.)

**With past tense** (what seemed to have happened):
- 그가 떠난 **것 같아요**. (It **seems he left**.)

---

## -기 때문에 ("because of the fact that")

**Pattern**: Nominalized verb (**-기**) + **때문에** ("because of")

More formal/written than -아서/-어서. Emphasizes the **reason or cause**.

- 시간이 없**기 때문에** 못 가요. (Because there is no time, I can't go.)
- 날씨가 나쁘**기 때문에** 집에 있어요. (Because the weather is bad, I'm staying home.)
- 공부가 어렵**기 때문에** 도움을 청했어요. (Because studying is difficult, I asked for help.)

**Comparison with -아서/-어서**:
- -아서/어서: shorter, more conversational
- -기 때문에: longer, more formal/written, emphasizes the reason

---

## -게 되다 ("come to / turn out to")

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-(으)게 되다** ("it comes to pass that / end up doing")

Expresses a **change of state or situation**, often outside the speaker's direct control or intention.

- 한국에 **살게 됐어요**. (I **ended up living** in Korea.)
- 한국어를 **배우게 됐어요**. (I **came to study** Korean.)
- 영국에 **가게 될 거예요**. (It **will come to pass that** I go to the UK.)

**Feeling/perception:**
- 그것이 **싫어지게 됐어요**. (I **came to dislike** it.)
- 아이들이 **자랐다**. vs. 아이들이 **자라게 됐다**. (Children grew up [naturally, as a result].)

**Contrast with -게**: Plain -게 is an adverbial ending ("in such a way / so that"); -게 되다 adds the notion of "it came to pass" or "it turned out that."

---

## -(으)려고 하다 ("be about to / intend to")

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-(으)려고 하다**

Expresses **intention, plan, or imminence** ("was about to...").

- 가려고 해요. (I'm intending to go / I'm about to go.)
- 공부하려고 했어요. (I was intending to study / I was about to study.)
- 밥을 먹으려고 했는데 전화가 왔어요. (I was about to eat when the phone rang.)

---

## -아/어 놓다 ("leave in a state of having done")

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-아/어 놓다**

Emphasizes that an action is **completed and left as a result** (preparation or completion).

- 준비해 놨어요. (I've prepared [and left it prepared].)
- 문을 열어 놨어요. (I've left the door open.)
- 밥을 해 놨어요. (I've cooked [and the meal is ready].)

---

## -어 버리다 ("end up doing / completely do")

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-어 버리다**

Indicates **completion or finality**, often with a sense of regret, inevitability, or emphasis on "completely."

- 실수를 해 버렸어요. (I went and made a mistake / I messed up.)
- 모두 먹어 버렸어요. (I ate it all up.)
- 잊어 버렸어요. (I completely forgot.)

---

## -고 싶다 ("want to")

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-고 싶다** ("want to do")

- 가고 싶어요. (I **want to go**.)
- 먹고 싶어요. (I **want to eat**.)

**Negation**: -고 싶지 않아요 (don't want to)
- 가고 싶지 않아요. (I don't want to go.)

---

## Quick Reference

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-는 것 같다** | seems/appears | 온다고 해요 |
| **-기 때문에** | because of (formal) | 없기 때문에 못 가요 |
| **-게 되다** | come to / turn out to | 가게 됐어요 |
| **-(으)려고 하다** | intend/about to | 가려고 해요 |
| **-아/어 놓다** | leave in a state of | 준비해 놨어요 |
| **-어 버리다** | end up/completely | 해 버렸어요 |
| **-고 싶다** | want to | 가고 싶어요 |
