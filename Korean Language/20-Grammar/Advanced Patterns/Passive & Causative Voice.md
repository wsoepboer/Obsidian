# Passive & Causative Voice

Two ways to shift focus in a sentence: passive (being acted upon) and causative (making/letting someone do).

---

## Passive Voice (피동)

Changes focus from the **agent (do-er)** to the **patient (affected)**.

### Formation Methods

#### Method 1: Suffix infixes (-이, -히, -리, -기)

Inserted between the root and -다, creating an entirely new verb form.

| Active | Passive | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 열다 (open) | 열리다 (be opened) | The door was opened. |
| 보다 (see) | 보이다 (be seen/visible) | It is visible. |
| 쓰다 (write/use) | 써지다 (get written) | It got written. |
| 듣다 (hear) | 들리다 (be heard) | It was heard. |
| 깨다 (break) | 깨지다 (be broken) | It broke / was broken. |
| 놀다 (play) | 놀리다 (be teased) | He was teased. |

**Examples**:
- 창문이 **열렸어요**. (The window was opened.)
- 목소리가 **들렸어요**. (The voice was heard.)
- 컵이 **깨졌어요**. (The cup broke / was broken.)

#### Method 2: -아/어지다 (become, get)

Attaches to the stem to express a passive or change-of-state meaning.

- 깨다 (break) → 깨어지다 (become broken)
- 잠그다 (lock) → 잠겨지다 (become locked)

**Examples**:
- 문이 **열어져요**. (The door opens / is opened.)
- 불이 **꺼져요**. (The light goes off / is turned off.)

---

## Causative Voice (사동)

Makes the subject **cause or let someone else do something**.

### Formation Methods

#### Method 1: Suffix infixes (-이, -히, -리, -우, -기, -구, -추)

Inserted into the root to create the causative form.

| Base | Causative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 먹다 (eat) | 먹이다 (feed) | Make/let eat |
| 앉다 (sit) | 앉히다 (seat/make sit) | Make someone sit |
| 입다 (wear) | 입히다 (dress/make wear) | Dress someone |
| 자다 (sleep) | 재우다 (put to sleep) | Make sleep |
| 세우다 (stand up) | (already causative in form) | Stand up / erect |
| 날다 (fly) | 날리다 (make fly) | Cause to fly |
| 타다 (ride) | 태우다 (give a ride) | Give someone a ride |

**Examples**:
- 아이에게 밥을 **먹이세요**. (Feed the child.)
- 할아버지를 의자에 **앉히셨어요**. (She seated grandfather on a chair.)
- 아기를 **재워요**. (I put the baby to sleep.)

#### Method 2: -게 하다 (make/let)

Attaches the particle -게 to the infinitive, then 하다.

- 먹다 → 먹게 하다 (make/let eat)
- 공부하다 → 공부하게 하다 (make/let study)

**Examples**:
- 아이에게 공부하게 했어요. (I made the child study.)
- 친구를 웃게 했어요. (I made my friend laugh.)

---

## Comparison: Active, Passive, Causative

| Voice | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | 나는 문을 열었어요. | I opened the door. |
| **Passive** | 문이 열렸어요. | The door was opened. |
| **Causative** | 나는 그에게 문을 열게 했어요. | I made him open the door. |

## Common Passive Verbs (Memorize These)

These are high-frequency and worth memorizing:

- 보이다 (appear, be visible) — from 보다
- 들리다 (be heard) — from 듣다
- 깨지다 / 깨어지다 (break) — from 깨다
- 열리다 (open) — from 열다
- 닫혀있다 (be closed) — from 닫다
- 꺼져있다 (be off) — from 끄다
- 켜져있다 (be on) — from 켜다

---

## Common Causative Verbs (Memorize These)

- 먹이다 (feed)
- 입히다 (dress)
- 앉히다 (seat)
- 재우다 (put to sleep)
- 태우다 (give a ride)
- 웃게 하다 (make laugh)
- 울게 하다 (make cry)
