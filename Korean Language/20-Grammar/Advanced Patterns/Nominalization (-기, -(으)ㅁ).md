# Nominalization: -기 and -(으)ㅁ

Converts a verb into a noun, allowing you to use verbs as the subject or object of a sentence.

---

## -기 Nominalization

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-기** → gerund ("the act of doing X")

- 걷다 (walk) → **걷기** (walking)
- 먹다 (eat) → **먹기** (eating)
- 자다 (sleep) → **자기** (sleeping)
- 공부하다 (study) → **공부하기** (studying)

---

## -(으)ㅁ Nominalization

**Pattern**: Verb stem + **-(으)ㅁ** → abstract noun ("the state/fact of doing X")

Vowel harmony: 받침 O → -음, 받침 X → -ㅁ

- 죽다 (die) → **죽음** (death)
- 살다 (live) → **삶** (life)
- 움직이다 (move) → **움직임** (movement)
- 웃다 (laugh) → **웃음** (laughter)

---

## Usage Comparison

| -기 | -(으)ㅁ |
|---|---|
| More common, especially in modern Korean | More formal/literary |
| Used in instructions, examples | Used in abstract/conceptual nouns |
| 걷기는 건강에 좋아요. (Walking is good for health.) | 삶은 소중해요. (Life is precious.) |

---

## Examples in Sentences

### -기 Nominalization

- **공부하기**는 어려워요. (Studying is difficult.)
- 매일 **운동하기**를 좋아해요. (I like to exercise daily.)
- 밥 **먹기** 전에 손을 씻어요. (Before eating, I wash my hands.)
- **가기** 전에 전화할게요. (I'll call you before going.)

### -(으)ㅁ Nominalization

- **죽음**은 두렵다. (Death is frightening. — literary)
- 이 **움직임**은 위험해요. (This movement is dangerous.)
- **웃음**은 최고의 약이다. (Laughter is the best medicine.)

---

## With Particles

Nominalized verbs take particles like nouns:

- 공부하기**를** 좋아해요. (I like studying. — 를 = object particle)
- 공부하기**는** 어려워요. (Studying is difficult. — 는 = topic particle)
- 공부하기**전에** 밥을 먹어요. (Before studying, I eat. — 전에 = "before")

## Practice

**Convert to nominalization**:

1. 춤을 추다 → \_\_\_\_\_ (dancing)
   - Answer: 춤을 추기

2. 일하다 → \_\_\_\_ (labor/work as a noun)
   - Answer: 일함 (or 일 as simple noun)
