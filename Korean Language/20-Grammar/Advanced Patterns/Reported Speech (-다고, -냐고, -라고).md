# Reported Speech (간접 화법)

Convert direct quoted speech into reported (indirect) speech. Different endings for statements, questions, commands, and suggestions.

---

## Pattern: Quoted sentence + 고 하다

The quoted clause uses the **plain/stem form**, then -고 하다 is attached.

---

## Reported Statements: -다고 하다

**Pattern**: Plain form (dictionary form or past) + **-다고 하다**

- Direct: 비가 온다. (It rains. / It is raining.)
- Reported: 비가 **온다고** 했어요. (He said it was raining.)

**Examples**:
- Direct: 나는 학생이다. (I am a student.)
- Reported: 그 사람이 학생**이라고** 했어요. (He said he was a student.)

- Direct: 시험을 합격했다. (I passed the exam.)
- Reported: 시험을 합격했**다고** 했어요. (He said he passed the exam.)

---

## Reported Questions: -냐고 하다

**Pattern**: Plain form + **-냐고 하다** (yes/no questions)

- Direct: 갈 거야? (Will you go?)
- Reported: 갈 거냐고 물어봤어요. (He asked if I would go.)

**Examples**:
- Direct: 뭐 해? (What are you doing?)
- Reported: 뭐 하냐고 물어봤어요. (He asked what I was doing.)

- Direct: 집에 있어? (Are you at home?)
- Reported: 집에 있냐고 물어봤어요. (He asked if I was at home.)

**Variation with 인지**: -인지 can also report yes/no questions (slightly more formal):
- 갈 거인지 물어봤어요. (same meaning as above)

---

## Reported Commands: -라고 하다

**Pattern**: Stem + **-라고 하다** (imperative speech)

- Direct: 가라! (Go!)
- Reported: 가**라고** 했어요. (He told me to go.)

**Examples**:
- Direct: 먹어라. (Eat!)
- Reported: 먹**으라고** / 먹**어라고** 했어요. (He told me to eat.)

- Direct: 공부해라. (Study!)
- Reported: 공부하**라고** 했어요. (He told me to study.)

---

## Reported Suggestions: -자고 하다

**Pattern**: Stem + **-자고 하다** (suggestions/proposals)

- Direct: 같이 가자. (Let's go together.)
- Reported: 같이 가**자고** 했어요. (He suggested we go together.)

**Examples**:
- Direct: 밥 먹자. (Let's eat.)
- Reported: 밥 먹**자고** 했어요. (He suggested eating.)

- Direct: 영화 보자. (Let's watch a movie.)
- Reported: 영화 보**자고** 했어요. (He suggested watching a movie.)

---

## Quick Reference Table

| Type | Direct | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| **Statement** | 간다. (Goes.) | 간**다고** 했어요. |
| **Past** | 갔다. (Went.) | 갔**다고** 했어요. |
| **Question** | 갈 거야? (Will go?) | 갈 거**냐고** 물어봤어요. |
| **Command** | 가라! (Go!) | 가**라고** 했어요. |
| **Suggestion** | 가자! (Let's go!) | 가**자고** 했어요. |

---

## Examples in Context

- 친구가 "내일 올 거야"라고 했어요. (My friend said "I'll come tomorrow.")
- 엄마가 "밥을 먹어라"고 했어요. (Mom told me "Eat your food.")
- 선생님이 "숙제를 하셨어요?"라고 물어봤어요. (The teacher asked "Did you do homework?")
- 우리가 "같이 가자"고 했어요. (We suggested "Let's go together.")

---

## Politeness with Reported Speech

The reported speech itself can be in different politeness levels:

- 간다고 했어요. (reported plain form + polite ending)
- 간다고 했습니다. (reported plain form + formal ending)
- 간다고 해요. (reported plain form + present tense)

The reported clause is always plain/dictionary form; the final 했어요/했습니다 carries the politeness level.
