# Negation: Ways to Say "Not"

Three distinct ways to express negation in Korean, each with different meanings.

---

## 1. Be Not: 이/가 아니에요

**Pattern**: Noun + **이/가 아니에요**

Used when something **is not** a particular state or identity.

**Examples**:
- 저는 고양이**가** 아니에요.
  (I am not a cat.)
- 이건 사과**가** 아니에요.
  (This is not an apple.)
- 영수는 학생**이** 아닙니다.
  (Yungsu is not a student.)

---

## 2. Do Not / Don't: 안 + Verb

**Pattern A**: **안** + Verb (simple negation)  
**Example**: 안 먹어요 (I don't eat)

**Pattern B**: Verb stem + **-지 않아요** (stronger negation)  
**Example**: 먹지 않아요 (I don't eat — emphasizing the refusal)

**When to use**:
- Expressing a **choice** not to do something
- General refusal or disagreement
- Habit ("I don't usually...")

**Examples**:
- 저는 고기**를** 안 먹어요.
  (I don't eat meat. — *my choice*)
- 여기에 안 가요.
  (I'm not going there. — *decision*)
- 요즘은 공부를 안 해요.
  (Lately I'm not studying. — *habit*)

---

## 3. Cannot / Can't: 못 + Verb

**Pattern A**: **못** + Verb (simple inability)  
**Example**: 못 먹어요 (I can't eat)

**Pattern B**: Verb stem + **-지 못해요** (stronger inability)  
**Example**: 먹지 못해요 (I can't eat — emphasizing the inability)

**When to use**:
- Expressing **inability** or **lack of capability**
- Physical or circumstantial barriers
- "Despite wanting to, I cannot..."

**Examples**:
- 저는 치커리**를** 못 먹어요.
  (I can't eat chicory. — *unable to*)
- 한국어**를** 못 해요.
  (I can't speak Korean. — *not capable*)
- 시간이 없어서 못 갔어요.
  (I couldn't go because I had no time. — *circumstantial inability*)

---

## Quick Comparison

| | Pattern | Meaning | Nuance |
|---|---------|---------|--------|
| **이/가 아니에요** | N + 이/가 아니에요 | Is not | Identity, state |
| **안 + V** | 안 + verb | Don't / Won't | Choice, refusal |
| **못 + V** | 못 + verb | Can't / Unable | Inability, circumstance |

## Real Scenarios

**Scenario 1**: You don't like spicy food.
- ✓ 저는 매운 음식**을** 안 먹어요.
  (I don't eat spicy food. — *my choice*)

**Scenario 2**: You're allergic to peanuts.
- ✓ 저는 땅콩**을** 못 먹어요.
  (I can't eat peanuts. — *physical inability*)

**Scenario 3**: That person is not a teacher.
- ✓ 그 사람**은** 선생님**이** 아니에요.
  (That person is not a teacher. — *state/identity*)

## Practice

**Choose the correct negation**:

1. 나는 초콜릿을 \_\_\_. (I don't eat chocolate.)
   - A: 아니에요 ✗ (wrong structure)
   - B: 안 먹어요 ✓ (*my choice*)

2. 그건 사과가 \_\_\_. (That's not an apple.)
   - A: 못 먹어요 ✗ (wrong structure)
   - B: 아니에요 ✓ (*state/identity*)

3. 다리가 아파서 \_\_\_. (My leg hurts, so I can't walk.)
   - A: 안 걸어요 ✗ (implies choice)
   - B: 못 걸어요 ✓ (*inability*)
