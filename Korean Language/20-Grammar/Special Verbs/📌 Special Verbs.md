# Special Verbs & Patterns

Verbs and patterns that don't follow standard conjugation rules or need special attention.

---

## Topics

### [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/좋다 vs 좋아하다|좋다 vs. 좋아하다]]
Two common "like/good" verbs that are frequently confused.

- **좋다** = "is good" (subject experiences goodness)
- **좋아하다** = "to like" (subject likes an object)

### [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/Negation|Negation]]
Three ways to say "not" or "don't" in Korean.

- **Be not**: 이/가 아니에요
- **Do not**: 안 + verb
- **Cannot**: 못 + verb

---

## Practice

**Which is correct?**

1. 저는 사과\_\_\_. (I ___ apples.)
   - A: 가 좋습니다 (apples are good to me)
   - B: 를 좋아합니다 (I like apples)
   - **Both are correct!** Different emphasis.

2. 치커리는 \_\_\_. (Chicory, I ___ eat it.)
   - A: 못 먹어요 (can't eat it)
   - B: 안 먹어요 (don't eat it)
   - **Both work**, but 못 implies inability, 안 implies choice.
