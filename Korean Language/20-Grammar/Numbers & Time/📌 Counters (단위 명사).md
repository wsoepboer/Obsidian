# Counters / Unit Nouns (단위 명사)

In Korean, you **cannot count most things with just a number**. You must use a counter (noun) that specifies the type of thing being counted.

---

## How Counters Work

**Pattern**: Noun + **Number (usually native, shortened) + Counter + (particle)**

**English equivalent**: Like saying "three sheets of paper" or "five heads of cattle" — Korean requires this structure always.

- 사과 **세 개** (literally: apple, three, units → "three apples")
- 학생 **다섯 명** (literally: student, five, people → "five students")
- 책 **두 권** (literally: book, two, volumes → "two books")

---

## Common Counters (일반적인 단위 명사)

### 개 (gae) — General objects (catch-all)
Used for **inanimate objects** when no more specific counter applies.

- 사과 한 개 (one apple)
- 의자 두 개 (two chairs)
- 컵 세 개 (three cups)
- 과자 다섯 개 (five cookies)

---

### 명 (myeong) — People
Standard polite/formal counter for people.

- 학생 세 명 (three students)
- 선생님 한 명 (one teacher)
- 사람 여덟 명 (eight people)

**분 (bun)** — The honorific form of 명, used when showing respect (guests, elders, customers).

- 손님 두 분 (two guests — respectful)
- 할머니 한 분 (grandmother — respectful)

---

### 마리 (mari) — Animals
Counts most animals.

- 강아지 한 마리 (one puppy)
- 고양이 세 마리 (three cats)
- 소 다섯 마리 (five cows)
- 새 두 마리 (two birds)

---

### 살 (sal) — Age (casual)
Used for age in everyday speech.

- 한 살 (one year old)
- 세 살 (three years old)
- 스물다섯 살 (25 years old)

**세 (se)** — Formal written counter for age (uses Sino-Korean number).

- 이십 세 (20 years of age — formal/official documents)

---

### 권 (gwon) — Bound books/volumes
Counts books, magazines, volumes.

- 책 한 권 (one book)
- 소설 두 권 (two novels)
- 잡지 여섯 권 (six magazines)

---

### 장 (jang) — Flat, thin objects
Paper, tickets, photos, etc.

- 종이 두 장 (two sheets of paper)
- 사진 한 장 (one photo)
- 우표 다섯 장 (five stamps)
- 표 세 장 (three tickets)

---

### 병 (byeong) — Bottles
- 물 한 병 (one bottle of water)
- 맥주 두 병 (two bottles of beer)
- 우유 한 병 (one bottle of milk)

---

### 잔 (jan) — Cups/glasses of liquid
Counts servings/portions of drinks.

- 커피 한 잔 (one cup of coffee)
- 물 두 잔 (two glasses of water)
- 와인 세 잔 (three glasses of wine)

---

### 대 (dae) — Vehicles, machines, devices
Cars, computers, phones, appliances.

- 자동차 두 대 (two cars)
- 컴퓨터 한 대 (one computer)
- 휴대폰 세 대 (three cell phones)
- 자전거 한 대 (one bicycle)

---

### 벌 (beol) — Sets of clothing
Traditional clothing (한복), outfits, suits.

- 옷 한 벌 (one outfit/set of clothes)
- 한복 두 벌 (two sets of traditional Korean dress)

---

### 켤레 (gyeolre) — Pairs (matched)
Shoes, socks, gloves, earrings.

- 신발 한 켤레 (one pair of shoes)
- 양말 두 켤레 (two pairs of socks)
- 장갑 세 켤레 (three pairs of gloves)

---

### 그릇 (geureut) — Bowls/servings of food
Particularly rice and soup.

- 밥 한 그릇 (one bowl of rice)
- 국 두 그릇 (two bowls of soup)

---

### 채 (chae) — Houses/buildings
- 집 한 채 (one house)
- 빌라 두 채 (two villas)

---

### 번 (beon) — Number of times/occurrences
Counts actions or frequencies.

- 한 번 (one time / once)
- 두 번 (two times / twice)
- 세 번 (three times)
- 여러 번 (several times)

**Pattern**: Verb + 번 (has done X times)
- 해본 적이 없어요. (I've never tried it. — lit. "have never done it even once")

---

### 시간 (sigan) — Hours of duration (native number)
**Important**: uses native Korean numbers, not Sino-Korean.

- 두 시간 (two hours)
- 세 시간 (three hours)
- 한 시간 (one hour)

**NOT**: ~~이 시간~~ (incorrect — would use native 둘)

---

### 분 (bun) — Minutes (Sino-Korean number)
**Important**: uses Sino-Korean numbers.

- 십분 (10 minutes)
- 삼십분 (30 minutes)
- 오십오분 (55 minutes)

**Note**: 분 also means "person" (honorific for 명), but context clarifies: 삼십분 (30 minutes) vs. 세 분 (three people, respectful).

---

### 개월 (gae-wol) — Months of duration (Sino-Korean)
**Important**: uses Sino-Korean numbers.

- 삼 개월 (three months)
- 육 개월 (six months)

**달 (dal)** — Alternative for months (uses native Korean).

- 세 달 (three months — native alternative)
- 아홉 달 (nine months)

---

### 년 / 해 (nyeon / hae) — Years
- **년 (Sino-Korean)**: 오 년 (five years — duration, Sino-Korean)
- **해 (native)**: 다섯 해 (five years — alternative with native)

---

### 층 (chung) — Floors of a building (Sino-Korean)
- 삼 층 (3rd floor)
- 십 층 (10th floor)
- 이십 층 (20th floor)

**패턴**: 몇 층? (What floor?) → 구 층. (9th floor.)

---

### 명분 (myeong-bun) — People (formal/administrative)
A slightly more formal register for counting people in official/administrative contexts.

- 열 명분 (portions/servings for ten people)

---

### 대 (also) — Hits/shots/occurrences
In addition to vehicles, used for blows, injections, etc.

- 한 대 맞았어요. (I got hit once.)
- 주사 세 대 맞았어요. (I got three shots/injections.)

---

### 쌍 (ssang) — Matched pairs
Like 켤레, but sometimes used for other matched items (earrings, gloves).

- 장갑 한 쌍 (a pair of gloves)
- 귀걸이 한 쌍 (a pair of earrings)

---

## Word Order for Counters

**Everyday spoken order** (most common):
- **Noun + Number + Counter + Particle**
- 사과 세 개를 샀어요. (I bought three apples.)
- 학생 다섯 명이 왔어요. (Five students came.)

**More formal/written order** (less common):
- **Number + Counter + 의 + Noun**
- 세 개의 사과를 샀어요. (I bought three apples. — more formal)

In everyday speech, the noun-first order is standard.

---

## When the Number Comes First

Some phrases place the counter number before the noun, especially in titles or labels:

- 세 명의 학생 (three students — literary/formal)
- 두 개의 사과 (two apples — more written)

But in conversation, **noun + number + counter** is far more natural.

---

## Shortened Forms with Counters

Remember that 1, 2, 3, 4, and 20 shorten before counters:

- 하나 → 한 (한 개, 한 명, 한 시)
- 둘 → 두 (두 개, 두 명, 두 시)
- 셋 → 세 (세 개, 세 명, 세 시)
- 넷 → 네 (네 개, 네 명, 네 시)
- 스물 → 스무 (스무 살)

Numbers 5 and above do NOT shorten: 다섯 개, 여섯 명, etc.

|---|---|---|
| **General objects** | 개 | 사과 세 개 |

| **Animals** | 마리 | 고양이 한 마리 |
| **Books/volumes** | 권 | 책 두 권 |
| **Paper, photos, tickets** | 장 | 종이 다섯 장 |
| **Bottles** | 병 | 물 한 병 |
| **Cups/glasses of drink** | 잔 | 커피 두 잔 |
| **Vehicles, machines** | 대 | 자동차 두 대 |
| **Clothing sets** | 벌 | 옷 한 벌 |
| **Pairs (shoes, socks)** | 켤레, 쌍 | 신발 한 켤레 |
| **Bowls of food** | 그릇 | 밥 한 그릇 |
| **Houses** | 채 | 집 한 채 |
| **Times/occurrences** | 번 | 한 번 |
| **Hours (native)** | 시간 | 두 시간 |

| **Age (formal)** | 세 | 이십 세 |

## Practice

**Fill in the counter**:

1. 사과 두 \_\_\_ (two apples)
   - Answer: 개

2. 선생님 한 \_\_\_ (one teacher)
   - Answer: 분 (or 명, but 분 is more respectful)

3. 책 세 \_\_\_ (three books)
   - Answer: 권

4. 신발 한 \_\_\_ (one pair of shoes)
   - Answer: 켤레

5. 지금 여덟 \_\_\_ 삼십분 (8:30 now)
   - Answer: 시 (시간이 아니라 just 시 for the hour)
