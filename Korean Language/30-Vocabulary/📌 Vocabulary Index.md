## High-Frequency Words

**Master these first**:

**Verbs**: 가다, 먹다, 듣다, 있다, 없다, 좋아하다, 하다, 주다, 보다

**Adjectives**: 좋다, 맑다, 덥다, 춥다, 예쁘다, 많다

**Particles**: 은/는, 이/가, 을/를, 에게, 와/과

**Time**: 오늘, 내일, 지금, 언제

---

## Study Tips

1. **Start with verbs** — Most essential for making sentences
2. **Learn by category** — Group similar words together for memory
3. **Use in context** — See how words combine with particles and endings
4. **Revisit regularly** — Space out reviews to cement learning
