## Context Guide

### When Meeting Someone Formal
- 만나서 반갑습니다.
  (Nice to meet you.)

### When Saying Goodbye
- **To someone leaving**: 안녕히 가세요.
- **To someone staying**: 안녕히 계세요.

### When Receiving Gratitude
- 아니에요. (Not at all.)
- 별 말씀을요. (Don't mention it.)
- 제가 더 감사합니다. (I'm more grateful / you're welcome.)

### When Receiving an Apology
- 괜찮아요. (It's okay.)

### Birthday
- 생일 축하합니다!
  (Happy birthday!)
