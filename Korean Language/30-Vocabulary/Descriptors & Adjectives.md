## Practice Sentences

- 오늘 날씨가 **맑아요**. (Today's weather is clear.)
- 이 옷이 **예뻐요**. (This outfit is pretty.)
- 여름에는 **더워요**. (In summer, it's hot.)
- 강아지가 **귀여워요**. (The puppy is cute.)
- 한국 음식이 **많아요**. (Korean food is abundant.)
