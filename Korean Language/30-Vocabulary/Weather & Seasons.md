## Practice Sentences

- **봄**에는 따뜻해요. (In spring, it's warm.)
- **여름**에는 덥고 습해요. (In summer, it's hot and humid.)
- **겨울**에는 **눈**이 **내려요**. (In winter, it snows.)
- 오늘 날씨가 **흐려요**. (Today's weather is cloudy.)
- 내일은 **비**가 **와요**. (Tomorrow it will rain.)
