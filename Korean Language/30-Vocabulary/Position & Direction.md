# Position & Direction

Position nouns (위치 명사) for saying where something is.

---

## Position Nouns

| Korean | English | Lesson |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 위 | on / above | 08-15 |
| 아래 | under / below | 08-15 |
| 앞 | front / in front of | 08-15 |
| 뒤 | back / behind | 08-15 |
| 옆 | side / next to | 08-15 |
| 사이 | between *(usually 사이에)* | 08-15 |
| 왼쪽 | left side | 09-03 |
| 오른쪽 | right side | 09-03 |

---

## How to use them

Korean puts the position word **after** the reference noun, then adds 에 — the reverse of English word order.

**Pattern**: Reference noun + position noun + 에 + 있어요/없어요

| Korean | English |
|--------|---------|
| 책상 위**에** | on the desk |
| 침대 아래**에** | under the bed |
| 학교 앞**에** | in front of the school |
| 집 뒤**에** | behind the house |
| 회사 옆**에** | next to the company |
| 은행과 서점 사이**에** | between the bank and the bookstore |

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/Location (에)|Location Particle 에]]

---

## Practice Sentences

- 휴대전화가 책상 **위에** 있어요. (The phone is on the desk.)
- 고양이가 침대 **아래에** 있어요. (The cat is under the bed.)
- 은행이 회사 **옆에** 있어요. (The bank is next to the company.)
- 학교 **앞에** 서점이 있어요. (There's a bookstore in front of the school.)
- 행거가 침대와 책상 **사이에** 있어요. (The hanger is between the bed and the desk.)
- 은행이 어디**에** 있어요? (Where is the bank?)
