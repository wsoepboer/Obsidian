## Notes

- **노트북 vs 공책**: 노트북 is a **laptop** (loanword), while 공책 is a paper **notebook**. Easy to mix up.
- **도와주다** = 돕다 (help) in the "-아/어 주다" do-for-someone form — literally "help and give," i.e. do it as a favour.
