# 2026-08-02 — Indirect Objects & Exclusivity

**Lesson Date**: 2 August 2026

## Grammar Topics

### Particle 에게/한테 — direct object + indirect object together

Recap from [[10-Lessons/2026-07-19 - Numbers & Giving|19 Jul]]: 에게 (formal) / 한테 (casual) mark **who the action is directed toward**. This lesson adds the case where the sentence carries **both** a direct object (을/를) and an indirect object (에게/한테) at once.

**Pattern**: Noun(target) + 에게/한테 + Noun(thing) + 을/를 + Verb

**Examples**:
- 동생**에게** 책**을** 줘요.
  (I give a book to my younger sibling. — target: sibling / direct object: book)
- 나타샤 씨는 친구**에게** 편지**를** 써요.
  (Natasha writes a letter to her friend.)
- 쪽영 씨가 저**에게** 선물**을** 줘요.
  (Jjogyeong gives me a gift.)

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/Directional (에게한테)|Directional Particles]]

---

### Particle 만 — "only / just / alone" (new)

Attached after a noun to exclude everything else. Same form regardless of 받침, and it **replaces** 은/는/이/가/을/를/에게/한테 — particles don't stack on top of 만.

**Examples**:
- 저**만** 영어를 해요.
  (Only I speak English.)
- 동생**만** 책을 읽어요.
  (Only my younger sibling reads books.)
- 동생**만** 학생이에요.
  (Only my younger sibling is a student.)

**Contrast with 는/은** (topic, not exclusive):
- 저**는** 영어를 좋아해요. (As for me, I like English.)
- 저**만** 영어를 좋아해요. (Only I like English — everyone else doesn't.)

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/Exclusivity (만)|Exclusivity Particle]]

## Practice

**연습 I — 에게/한테 fill-in**:

1. 가장 씨가 리샤 씨**에게/한테** 전화를 해요.
   (Gajang calls Lisa.)
2. 나타샤 씨는 친구**에게/한테** 편지를 써요.
   (Natasha writes a postcard to her friend.)
3. 쪽영 씨가 저**에게/한테** 선물을 줘요.
   (Jjogyeong gives me a gift.)

**연습 II — 만 fill-in**:

1. 쪽영 씨**만** 누나가 있어요. 쪽영 씨는 동생이 없어요.
   (Only Jjogyeong has an older sister. Jjogyeong doesn't have a younger sibling.)
2. 나타샤 씨는 친구에게 편지**만** 보내요.
   (Natasha sends only a letter to her friend — no gift.)
