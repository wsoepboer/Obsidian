## Grammar Topics

### Sentence Structure (S-V-O)

- Basic order: **Subject → Verb → Object**
- Example: I (S) eat (V) apple (O) → 사과를 먹다

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/Object (을를)]]

---

### Particles: Overview

| Particle | Rule | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| **은/는** | 받침 O→은, X→는 | 사과**는** 과일 |
| **이/가** | 받침 O→이, X→가 | 사과**가** 좋음 |
| **을/를** | 받침 O→을, X→를 | 사과**를** 먹다 |

See: [[20-Grammar/Particles/📌 Particle Overview]]

---

### Formal Polite Ending: -습니다 / -습니까

**Steps**:
1. Remove 다 → 먹, 가, 좋
2. Check 받침 (final consonant)
3. Add -습니다 (statement) or -습니까 (question)

| Verb | 받침? | Question | Statement |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| 좋다 | O | 좋습니까? | 좋습니다 |
| 가다 | X | 갑니까? | 갑니다 |
| 듣다 | O | 듣습니까? | 듣습니다 |

See: [[20-Grammar/Verb Endings/Formal Polite]]

---

### Special Comparison: 좋다 vs. 좋아하다

| | Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---------|---------|
| **좋다** | S + 이/가 + 좋다 | X is good |
| **좋아하다** | S + O + 을/를 + 좋아하다 | I like X |

**Examples**:
- 사과**가** 좋습니다 (Apples are good)
- 저는 사과**를** 좋아합니다 (I like apples)

See: [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/좋다 vs 좋아하다]]

---

### Negation Patterns

1. **Be not**: 이/가 아니에요 → 저는 고양이가 아니에요
2. **Do not**: 안 + verb → 안 먹어요
3. **Cannot**: 못 + verb → 못 먹어요

See: [[20-Grammar/Special Verbs/Negation]]
